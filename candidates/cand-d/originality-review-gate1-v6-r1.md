## What the five columns describe for v6

1. **D1 – silhouette/geometry.** The declaration has two rectangular bodies, 32.6 and 7.6 mm long before end plates, each 25.4 × 24 mm in section. The end pitch planes are 48.0 mm apart along x at y = +0.6, z = −4.6 mm. Visible cross-section is not the cylindrical servo output or pilot viewed in isolation. Flush metal pans, a lid, the asymmetric head flange and a two-ended crown bar affect what could be seen.
2. **D2 – coupler.** A presented passive stud enters an interrupted pilot bore. The rocker tilts about its own pin to accommodate depth differences at the two slot flanks. It is neither a closing pawl nor an additional body joint. A splined SCS0037 input winds a series torsion spring against the sleeve; after a dwell, steep and shallow cam stages pull faces together. Seventy-two radial face teeth at each mate take torque in flank bearing. Neither spring bias nor servo holding torque is credited for passive retention. Reverse unwinds the spring until a metal dog bears against the skirt. The clearance-bored rear collar, hollow hardened cross-dowel with separate soft rivet/keepers, and two interrupted journals support and react sleeve loads; **this transverse pin is not a mating lug**. The keyed cassette reacts servo case torque; the spline screw is not the path for stud ejection.
3. **D3 – actuation/locomotion.** The gearmotor, pinion and sector rotate the head and tail about one vertical pivot with a ±15° working range. A fixed sequence of such joints can bend a preconnected line. Rotation of the head's serial servo only cycles the coupler. Neither its spring nor the rocker imposes a second commanded yaw, propels a lone unit, or mechanically drives the next link's motor.
4. **D4 – assembly.** Two opposite end normals, one allowed roll (0°), no branches; start as a supported chain. Distinguish externally presenting the male stud at the entrance shelf, commanding a closure, reaching the shallow flank, and actually clearing the loaded joint. No automatic pickup, guaranteed capture or guaranteed loaded release. The declaration explicitly sets bounded independent release to false. Inter-unit power transfer is not part of this assembly.
5. **D5 – visual identity.** Two substantial polymer bodies with machined end features do not by themselves establish a colour design. The declared evidence is shape, local material/surface contrasts and visible fastening/skin boundaries. No undocumented paint, emblem, illumination or swarm rendering was assumed. The internal spring cannot make the exterior resemble a coil.

## Family, exclusions and decision

| Required check | Independent finding on the frozen declaration |
|---|---|
| Family B (Q62 §3) | **Member.** Two elongated-in-combination segments, one revolute body joint, and opposite end sites on the same axis yield chain-native geometry without treating the cam or rocker as another joint. The other Family B [cand-b Z-link](../cand-b/dossier.md) stows side-by-side through 180°, mates at alternating 180° roll and uses a spring latch. v6 is an in-line, small-yaw, roll-zero, cam-held stud. Sharing a family does not make these the same structure. |
| Cube with internal flywheel | **Not present.** The unit is neither cubic nor propelled by an impulse wheel. The actuator inventory identifies a joint motor/pinion/sector and a servo that turns the sleeve. Spinning motor parts and a wound spring do not constitute braking a flywheel to propel a cube. |
| Needle- or particle-like silhouette | **Not present.** Across a 48.0 mm pitch the body is 25.4 × 24 mm in section. The short protruding pilot is one end of an individually visible, broad segmented unit, not its silhouette. No particle-rendering rule is declared. |
| Q61 §3.1–2 and G8 | F1 high: zero; F2 high: zero. No reference has two highs. ATRO, TYCOON and the electrical bayonet are different designs from different sources, not different names or stages of one robot. Neither exclusion is present. |
| Q61 §3.3–5, independence and G7 | 104 rows × 5 = 520 reasoned, cited cells: 268 none, 185 low, 64 moderate, 3 high. The close cells record their retained feature, differentiator, and identifiable primary material. All high cells are disclosed in the opening table; the hash and independent reviewer are recorded above. |

**Changes made as a result of this paper review:** none. This is a new assessment of the changes since v5, not an edited v5 record. Beckhoff D2 stays high. TYCOON D2 moves from moderate to high with the head-mounted serial servo and specific inserted-male/rotating-female layout. US6226068B1 D2 moves from moderate to high because torsional energy now supplies the rotating sleeve's axial draw, not merely because v6 contains a spring somewhere. The rocker raises a distinct load-distribution comparison (N9); the rear collar raises an analogous retention design (N4). Neither turns the module into a pivot-latching space port or turbine. The metal skin/cassette, local servo controls and absence of guaranteed release do not establish a new locomotion mode or a new family.

**Limits and use.** This is not an exhaustive prior-art search, a freedom-to-operate opinion, a physical demonstration, an official G6/G7/G8 run or selection of a Gate 1 survivor. Not every corpus paper or fictional work was available in full: non-close cells can cite the registered descriptions, secondary fiction survey, or first-party project pages. M-TRAN I/II use the authors' indexed paper passages; the Roombots connector uses the conference paper on a mirror; Belke uses the author's thesis instead of the rate-limited publisher; RIMRES uses the DFKI mirror; TYCOON uses the authors' publisher passages/captions and supplementary record rather than claiming an unrestricted PDF read; WPI supplies poster-level evidence only. A shared servo brand, generic spring, term like “bayonet”, or search snippet not attributable to a primary author was not by itself used to decide a close cell. The frozen representation does not determine all aspects of colour, finish or group appearance. Re-review at final visuals and after changes to geometry, coupling, motion, assembly or visuals; a newly identified second high on one design could reverse this outcome.

**Outcome: pass for this declaration and reference set only. This review reduces copying risk and does not claim absolute originality.**

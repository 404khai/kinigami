# cand-d v6 — loaded release is not established

Q95 and Q96, exploratory engineering, 2026-11-20. The cam can retain an already engaged pair on paper, but a healthy head cannot guarantee release at its rated torque over the declared loads. The declaration therefore sets bounded independent release to **false**. This is not a gate verdict or proof that the 60 mm class is infeasible. Preserve the v5 declaration, both reviews, run 10 and Q94. Protocol revision **1**, not 2.

## Cartridge assembly and force path

All coordinates are mm; x increases toward the head pitch plane, x36.40. Datum dimensions, friction, strength and tool access are paper callouts, not measurements. Section calculations: [rear_drive.py](scripts/rear_drive.py); [coupler.py](scripts/coupler.py). No adhesive retains the rotating assembly.

1. Machine the housing/flange, collar, hollow dowel and relieved spline driver from certified AMS 5643 17-4 PH stock. Finish critical features **after** aging to H900. Rear fixed land x27.16–27.61 on the collar OD 10.040–10.045; rotating skirt ID 10.070–10.080. Forward stationary land x35.05–35.30 OD 6.990–7.000 inside sleeve bore 7.015–7.025. Relieve other journal surfaces at least 0.10 radially; qualify assembled OD and mating sleeve ID coaxiality/form to ≤0.005 TIR each at both ends. Both interrupted journal stations can be side-loaded. Rear land to front span ≥7.44; sleeve tilt is charged to tooth engagement.
2. From the rear, install the notched PTFE front thrust ring and C95400 sleeve/skirt. Shoulder x30.35 loaded forward. Slip Ø7.025–7.045 collar over the Ø6.980–7.000 rear tube, inside the skirt. Set collar by measuring the actual shoulder and fitting a 0.090±0.005 spacer. Match-bore the **clamped housing and collar on a fixture without the sleeve**, clean, then reassemble and clock the opposed Ø2.70 skirt ports on the bench. Cross-bore Ø2.200–2.212 at x28.650±0.015. Front guide floor x29.800±0.015 is flat; it and the transverse bore remain distinct.
3. Insert Ø2.190–2.200 hardened cross-tube (ID ≤1.05). Flat counterbore the outside of each collar lip and fit a Ø2.50 × 0.12 stainless keeper washer. A soft Ø~1 annealed 304 rivet through the cross-tube traps the two washers: form its heads into the remaining recess with opposed dies. The washers bridge the cross-hole. Never try to upset the load-bearing hardened tube. Seat every head at least 0.05 below the locally relieved OD; limit combined face relief/counterbore depth to the stock debited by the stress model. Verify keeper travel cannot expose a pin end to the rotating skirt. Gauge 0.053–0.127 end play, front pilot-to-floor ≥0.035 and centered free rotation; reject interfering rivets or misaligned seats. Disassemble by drilling the soft keeper on the bench.
4. Fit driver to the actual Ø3.9/28T spline (not an assumed involute), servo tip x25.350±0.025. One M1.7×3.5 retaining screw; gauge real thread depth, fit/select counterbore for ≥1.7 engagement and ≥0.15 bottom clearance. Limit head to r≤2 and x≤27.35. Central clearance recess r2.15 reaches x27.45±0.03 in the otherwise solid rear of the housing. Install driver before the fixed cartridge blocks axial access. Boss x22.72, plate x23.35–24.15 r6.24; clearance to case ≥0.130 and moving skirt ≥0.033. This screw takes **no** mating-stud ejection load.
5. Input leg turns the wire spring in the closing sense. Coil Ø0.4064 wire, mean Ø9.20, 3.75 turns. Input/output wire centre planes 1.900±0.008 apart, input x24.969±0.004; coil body ≤2.317, wound ID ≥7.31 over Ø6.75 pilot, free OD ≤9.649. Capture legs against tangential walls, with radial slip for diameter change and ≥0.127 axial float at the skirt pickup. The output leg passes into a sleeve-fixed pocket beyond r5.05. Keep it in the solid angular sector away from the reverse finger. A wider reverse pad at r5.64–6.24, x29.35–30.00 bears on the metal groove end **only in reverse**; relieve the shank. Cut 298° of open-ended arc for ≥257.1° relative wind plus pad width and tolerance. Keep a continuous inner rim from the relieved skirt bore to r5.53 except at explicitly charged tool ports. Clock both ports away from the reverse stop; check the full relative sweep on a fixture, including sleeve float. No free rotation about the servo case is assumed.
6. Assemble the cassette, trimmed external ear, floor-captive gate, case shims and screwed spline outside the shell. Set both members to calibrated open datum, insert the fixed cartridge from the detached front side and slide the open-ended dog and spring pockets into engagement. Mount the flange on the side piers, not the thin lower edge of the end plate. Shell threads carry no structural load: screws enter captive steel nuts/metal parts. Components, shell window, lid/pan returns, screw lengths, removal order and all mass proxies are specified in the generator and the frozen package section below.

Tension on a fully seated stud → rocker/crowns → cam sleeve → front PTFE thrust washer → steel flange → fasteners/nuts and end-plate piers. Positive ejection → rear sleeve shoulder → collar → hardened cross-tube → solid housing → flange. The 30 N sizing case with one collar lip loaded gives nominal pin combined stress 51 MPa. Conditional 530 N reversed fault gives 906 MPa pin, 560 MPa ligament shear and 225 MPa bearing. Even the rear housing tear-out screen (658 vs 676 MPa nominal yield in shear) has little margin; stress concentration, cyclic contact, pin flexure and punch-through are **not** certified. At 176.5 N·mm the driver nominal bend is 208 MPa and pad bearing 104 MPa. These do not establish recoverability at the small bronze flank, porous PA12 mounts or spline. Isolate after a stall; do not repeatedly drive through a jam. Catalogue stall is a diagnostic, not an upper bound on impact or externally imposed thrust on a zero-lead shelf.

## Alignment, passive retention and the failed reverse calculation

Q31 starts with a supported chain, not autonomous docking. The powered coupling starts with the stud **externally presented**, both lugs at the open shelf, on a level table. There is no hidden fixture after presentation. Cylindrical pilot Ø3.986–3.990 mates with Ø4.000–4.008, accounting for the two missing guide arcs. A Ø1.00 bronze pivot allows the flattened 304 rocker to tilt inside the 2.74 × 1.64 fork pocket: check free rocking through ±1.8°. The body guide and opposing 72 machined face teeth bound nominal roll and transmit torque in bearing, not friction adhesion. No powered or virtual flattening assumed.

| Feature | Calculation / limitation |
|---|---|
| Cam | 31.5° dwell; 2.720→3.180 at 2.00 mm/rad, then →3.765 at 0.331. Travel 145.94°. Dwell bridges both mouths; it supplies no axial advance. |
| Angular drive | Home 14°, close 259°, reverse command 2°. Min–max actual travel and obstruction torque (~12 N·mm) include tracking, backlash, spring rate and stop setting. Command angle does not prove the sleeve reached the shallow lead. |
| Tooth entry | Phase error ≈1.40°; debit pilot tilt, sleeve-vs-housing tilt, tooth height, lug offset and stack. Earliest first contact is beyond the transition. First contact does **not** imply both teeth and both crowns carry load. |
| Self-lock | At the worst shallow lead, tangent ≤0.334/3.505=0.0953. Lower assumed cam friction is 0.15 dry or 0.10 oiled. This holds only on the loaded shallow flank inside those regimes; no credit for the servo or spring unpowered. |
| Load | Always apply pull 4W, shear W, twist 2W×12.7, out-of-plane bending 48W, in-plane 14.5 N·mm (separate 52.2 joint fault). Keep them during reverse even after the spring unloads. Do not divide them by motor stall torque. |
| Partial seating | On a tilted/partial pair a single guide flat can take the **entire** fixed twist. Pilot side loads include both shear and the bending couple. Even the two-tip calculation lacks the force to correct worst roll/pitch/yaw simultaneously; nominal Hertz stress exceeds the provisional bronze first-yield screen before allowing edge contact. Partial shelf/steep states are not passive locks. |
| Loaded reverse | Head unwinds spring then its metal dog pushes the sleeve to open, no active tail. For unilateral contact use both journal reactions and the moment of one axial lug, not a 50:50 pair. Allow zero front-thrust drag while forming maximum preload and adverse dry drag while releasing it. In the steep ejection branch the feedback coefficient exceeds one: the model supplies no finite upper torque. It is not a measured jam. |
| Release indication | At the open stop the teeth/crowns can clear geometrically; no full powered withdrawal of the remaining pilot is claimed. The servo sensor is on its input, not the sleeve or mating face. Do not count an obstruction as success. |
| Fault | At 176.5 N·mm and μcam≥0.10, positive flank force alone screens at 71 N steep, 258 N shallow, approaching 504 N in the near-flat blend, before fixed forces. At exactly zero lead externally imposed thrust is independent of motor torque. No fault survival claim outside these assumptions. |

The first Q95 layout used SCS0002: 58.920 mm, measured **in the draft evaluator**, but only 15.69 N·mm rated (4.8 V) against the preliminary 40.91 dry budget. The [SCS0037](https://www.feetech.cn/Data/feetechrc/upload/file/20260622/6391771882565263995959110.pdf) offers 49.03 rated at the same rail, rather than its 147 N·mm stall. SCS0043 has no rated advantage there and is deeper. Even a free rocker has a finite resistance moment from its pivot, cheeks and loaded slot: being free does not fix its load split. The script also screens an **unbuilt** separate torque key and a low-moment equalizer; these are not part of this declaration. A low-friction front washer alone cannot remove the rear guide feedback.

## Reproduce and inspect

Run the five scripts in [scripts/README.md](scripts/README.md). In particular, `coupler.py --q96-only` prints one-sided and mixed-friction cases at the declaration mass and 46 g; `--fixed-key` is an alternative, not the present stud. Inputs are design limits unless explicitly sourced. No physical or dynamic tests were run.

Handbook friction, 304 yield data, material coupon strength and an assumed 2 kN/mm assembled stiffness are **not** an as-built operating certificate. Measure mixed friction, drag, deflection, seating, case/rail limits and reversibility before any later-stage claim.

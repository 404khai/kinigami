# cand-d v7 — a three-station transverse shutter

**Paper candidate, exploratory. Not an accepted lock.** Q99 authorizes a new cartridge in the Q85 two-segment body; Q97 records why the rotating wedge cannot simply be fitted with a more powerful motor. Q98's screw/nut fits but does not fix its seven-contact self-stress and jaw release. V7 actually replaces those parts. Fabrication, dynamics, cycle life and a capture envelope are not demonstrated.

## What carries a load?

The ring has three centres at radius 4.65 mm and angles 90°, 210° and 330° about the body x axis. The head datum is x37.25 and the tail datum x−11.60, on y+.60,z−4.60. Place the far tail by that transform, not by snapping the spheres. Its three radius-1.05 mm spherical-ended members, with *radial* necks toward the axis and then axial stems, present unobstructed axial and tangential poles. Use custom ground, hardened bearing-steel crowns/neck stock retained in the machined carrier, not welded finished bearing balls. Attachment strength of that root remains a design obligation. The two bolt ears, cassette, fixed spline hub and body joint use the v6 mount, not the v6 cam. No second motor or central draw stud.

| Face coordinate | Steel bearing surfaces | Reaction in the nominal locked state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 axial | Rear pads opposite radially moving keeper bridges | Pull, push, pitch and yaw |
| 3 tangential | Opposed straight cheeks at each station | Transverse shear and twist |
| Radial at each station | None | Do not invent a seventh reaction or load on a hidden pilot |

The hard guide gap is deliberate. Finished axial pole distance in the section is 2.21–2.29 mm and ball diameter 2.08–2.12: 0.09–0.21 mm axial backlash before elastic motion. This avoids opposing hard normals but **does not meet Q85's driven tolerance take-up**. Q99 does not waive Q85. Head dwell restrains keeper travel without motor holding torque; an overtravel stop on the servo alone would not secure each keeper. Neither 120° spacing nor a common motor establishes equal sharing, or a rigid, backlash-free connection. No high friction clamp is credited.

## Continuous release and where it fails

In the section coordinates the rear seat is x33.60, ball centre x34.65, keeper underside x35.85. One radially moving keeper straddles the stem at each site. The ball actually has to pass the withdrawing loaded edge, not just clear a line through its centre. Over 12–108° the planar cam moves the keeper lip r4.31→5.96, then dwells. From 112–156° the separate barrel track moves the rear stops outward by 1.80 mm. Reverse through the same paths to reset. Keep the stops back until the transverse keepers have cleared, not while a ball is between an edge and a cheek end. Use only the external quasistatic presentation of a stationary unit for insertion; Gate 1 supplies no corrective yaw, onboard locomotor or continued external fixture.

[Section sweep](scripts/shutter_section.py): 0.281 mm certified nominal planar web with the specified width and sampling debit; 0.105 mm open radial clearance after dimension and pose debits; last nominal cheek end x34.96, axial exit budget 0.220 mm after the listed debits. These are sectional bounds, not a swept solid collision proof for every reachable tilted or partially engaged entry. In particular an eccentric ball moving at a loaded keeper edge may reach a cheek end **before** full radial retraction. A proposed outward-free pickup could limit the edge normal only if the actual guides and backstop allow it. It is not silently substituted for the evaluated cam.

[Wrench bound](scripts/reciprocal.py): normalize moments by the station radius. Use the invertible six-column matrix with columns (n,c×n) to resolve the six normal axes. For each absolute reciprocal component bound friction at **both actual poles**, plus normal angle and centre displacement errors. A positive weighted row-sum certificate below unity bounds the entire nonnegative feedback; it applies also if some contacts are absent. At μ≤0.23, normals within 0.5°, centre deviations ≤0.18 mm and radius ≤1.06 mm, the certified factor is ≤0.813. At the conservative 50 g screen: 13.39/14.44 N per axial/tangential site (combined service), 24.18/25.34 N including table correction, 23.36/23.71 N in the specified joint fault. The fixed reference envelope is 4W pull/push, W shear, 2W·12.7 torsion, W·50.8 bend and 14.5 N·mm yaw; the fault substitutes 52.2 N·mm yaw. A force includes its specified moment arm; arbitrary actuator or contact faults are not bounded by the servo rating. The iterative bound is conditional, not proof that a piece can sustain that load, nor that it never tips beyond the admitted coordinate range.

At the smooth flat poles, using 210 GPa, Poisson ratio 0.30 provisional and minimum ball radius 1.04 mm, 25.34 N gives 3.92 GPa elastic Hertz centre pressure and 0.0030 mm total approach. JTEKT's [basic static bearing load reference](https://www.jtekt.co.jp/products/bearing/en/support/bearing-knowledge/5-5000.html) lists 4.20 GPa for conventional ball bearings other than self-aligning; [ISO 76 scope](https://www.iso.org/standard/38101.html) explicitly excludes important truncation and non-equivalent surfaces. This is a useful sensitivity, **not** certification of the custom flats, neck or an edge. [Hardened 52100 properties](https://sdballs.com/product/aisi-52100-chrome-steel-balls/) list 60–66 HRC, 210 GPa and 7.80 g/cm³. Never use the high-strength Hertz comparison for PA12, bronze, annealed 304 or an unqualified weld.

At μ=0.23 in cam and slide, one-sided guide feedback and a 3 N·mm other-drag allocation give 4.199 N·mm per N of base radial resistance; 10 N needs 44.99 N·mm, 17 N needs 74.39 N·mm. **The budget depends on stage.** Once all keepers are out, the inclined end of a tangential cheek can still bear. Even the conditional meridional escape bound returns 7.53 N sufficient total push at 50 g. On the barrel cam one follower can be opposite one loaded pad; span 6.60 mm, offset up to 10.03 mm, effective force/torque factor and guide friction leave just 1.20 N conservative credited push under the same 49.03 N·mm rating. This is below even the simple 50 g compression-and-table-drag demand of 2.45 N. These are design budget failures, not a prediction every physical assembly jams. The allowance is not a certified bearing drag maximum. Improving the flat-contact proof does not fix ejection.

**Blocked and unpowered states.** Only fully seated ball sets with the radial keeper backstops in place can be called captured. A debris obstruction, a single lagging keeper or a bent crosshead is not that state. Motor current/shaft angle is not output-state sensing. Emergency de-energization does not physically withdraw a jammed keeper; the present design has no proved midstroke limiter/recovery chain, nor independent installed sensors that certify screw/keeper/ball state. Retaining the v6 servo's own controller, limits and voltage sense is not a substitute. Do not claim independent loaded release. No required state change is inferred from time elapsed or software flags.

## Package, QA and provenance

Retained hardware, source URLs, circuit allocations, trapped motor case, key and rocker screw, M1.6 metal nuts and disassembly order are inherited from [v6](dossier-v6.md). Remove its stud, teeth, rocker, sleeve, torque spring and reverse-dog cartridge. Bench-build the rotor head, front guides, three runners and moving pad frame; install the splined hub before offering up the front cartridge, then bolt on the outer ears. Tail screws are installed with the controller out. The wire, fastener and auxiliary material proxies are not proposed mounting positions. The body translates and rotates in 3D; the two sites at zero roll form the evaluator's supported chain. No 5-unit dynamic result is inferred.

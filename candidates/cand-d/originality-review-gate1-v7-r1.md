# Cand-d v7 · independent paper originality review · r1

**Pass under Q61.** The replacement is not novel simply because it has three stations or because its jaws are called shutters. SINGO and PetLock are close precedents for the actual lock; several chain robots use the same basic bending drive. No reference matches at **high** in two dimensions. Neither fictional class has a high cell. This finding concerns originality, not whether v7 retains or releases a load.

**All high cells** (distinct references are not combined; closely related publications are not counted as independent inventions):

|---|---|---|---|
| D2 | [SINGO on SuperBot, Figs. 1–3][r-SINGO] | A single motor rotates a disk whose face tracks synchronously drive radial locking slides. | Three bridges close behind fixed mating crowns; no opposing set of movable biting jaws. Rear-stop motion is separate. |

A linked primary paper, inventor disclosure, manufacturer's manual, or maker's own account supports each moderate/high judgement; the text alongside that rating is its differentiation note (the retained feature and the difference). For [Jubilee's locator][r-JUB] also read the [separate lock design][r-JUB2]. For [F1][r-A1] also read the [Disney production paper][r-DIS]. The linked frozen registry descriptions for distant references retain their earlier-source qualifications; their original access dates are **not** being changed to today's. Fictional works were not all re-watched/read. For TYCOON I read indexed **author text and captions** for Figs. 12–15, including the Oldham coupling, and its [authors' supplement](https://tandf.figshare.com/articles/media/TYCOON_modular_robot_capable_of_torque_synthesized_collaboration_with_complementary_intermittent_gear_mechanism/31175639); the publisher denied the direct PDF. AIST author PDFs were read via indexed text after a direct timeout; a mirror of the Fable manufacturer's manual was used. Product listings without cutaways support no claim about hidden cam geometry. The Gimatic QC page returned mixed pneumatic parts and was screened out as evidence of a *manual* latch. No full-scale teardown, video-only force inference, claim construction, patent clearance or exhaustive global novelty search.

**Reading the matrix.** None = no relevant correspondence; low = generic; moderate = distinctive in kind, but materially different in specific form/principle; high = same distinctive form or principle in that dimension. No averaging. On a component-only row, the absence of a comparable whole robot is not evidence *against* a close D2 match. Conversely, an internal latch is not body locomotion. “Keep” describes what is actually retained in this frozen declaration; it is not a proposed change.

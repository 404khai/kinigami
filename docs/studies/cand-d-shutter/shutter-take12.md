### Edge lead, locking and return

Pin follows a narrow captured groove on a **pickup**, not on the blade. Its inboard shoulder pushes a separate blade tang outward. The tang can leave the shoulder by ≥1.75 in a relieved guide, more than the entire 1.65 stroke including tolerance; no outer rigid wall in that range. A low-force spring between pickup and tang draws the blade back onto its shoulder. Example design target 0.20 preload, ≤0.15 N/mm, ≤0.47 N at full lead. Check free length, solid height, installed force and sliding drag before selecting stock. If unloaded breakaway exceeds preload, inward reset is not guaranteed; do not convert this into a hard closing wall. Outward reset is positive once the shoulder catches the tang. The hard open stop is beyond the worst sphere plus misalignment. This arrangement alone **does not lock** the blade.

Separate fixed-cage steel index tab on the rotating lock tier sweeps behind a notch on each **actual blade**, not behind its pickup. Closed seat at n4.31; limit permissible tab entry to +.06 radial error (.28 nominal pole overlap left); require ≥.15 overlap after ball and edge gauge. Tab drops out in first 0..10° while pickup is on the broad flat; reverse order on closing. A blade stuck out blocks the tab or the controller inhibits entry; must withstand one jammed tab, and cannot report lock merely from commanded angle. No hard closing wedge. Detailed nonintersecting angular tab/neck/rail geometry and no trapping ahead of the lead blade **not proved**. This is a separate tier, not added width to the .82 groove.

### Moving loads, jams and stops

For flat-contact load 10..15 per station at μ=.20.. .23, three single sliding interfaces cost 6..10.35; second loaded guide interfaces and skew can raise effective base drag to 10..17. Do not double charge side reactions if using measured base drag. G=(s′+μr)/(1−μs′/r) is torque per cam-driven axial/radial force; gib normal from cam side load and eccentric drive moment consumes further friction. Script assumes e=3.02 and effective opposed guide span L=3.70 for radial blade, not perfect load sharing. At upper drag: 61.14 (.20) or 74.39 (.23), including **assumed** 3 other losses vs 49.03 rated. .23 allows at most 10.96 base force. Offset from revised disk midplane to lip is actually 3.16; the first screen is optimistic unless the drive support is raised. At 17 N require entire radial factor ≤2.71; at r3.95, G≈2.12: with .23 and this pressure angle e/L must be roughly ≤.2 (not .82), even before more parasitics. Longer individual keeper tracks need a new guide load path, not simply more rotation.

Common-ring stripper single driven pin opposite one loaded pad: e=10.03, L=6.60, feedback=.886, aggregate allowance 1.20. Prefer independent captured pads for further development. Each has an aligned rod at r5.45, pad r4.65 and cam contact r4.50. With a proposed 3.10 axial guide span and 2.20 transverse antiroll span, bound gib normals from cam side thrust, torsion and axial eccentricity. Even if all pads move unloaded except ONE at 8 N, .23 gives 86.91; at three ×8 gives 254.72. These do not include extra guide-end overhang, rod sag or contact drag beyond 3. **No positive design established at requested load.** Even ideal frictionless eject at 24 N ×2.644 mm/rad exceeds rated before the cam/gib terms. Separate armed springs may trade re-arm angle/time for peak torque; must prove independent retain/release, delivered minimum vs actual resistance, clearance of all blades, safe three-spring recharge and stop impacts. Spring ejection is not positive displacement.

Fault: at 200 input screen one hard angular end stop at r5.50 takes 36.4 N tangential; propose pin Ø1.0 in double shear, supported span 1.2 → 23 MPa direct shear, 111 MPa nominal bending. A single loss-of-contact radial follower on constant lead ~1.07 could see ~186 N even frictionless at that fault; Ø.8 with .45 cantilever → ~1.66 GPa before notch. **Reject** the unsupported pin. Wide clevis with intermediate bearing, two-sided support and independently bounded drive before the blend are needed; ramp endpoint zero lead has no finite ideal torque-to-force bound. The ring stop handles commanded *endpoint* torque only. One ball's Ø.96 radial neck at 15 N ×2.1 has ~363 MPa nominal bending before fillet, one-sided Hertz and fatigue; not rated. No sharing credit across the three stations.

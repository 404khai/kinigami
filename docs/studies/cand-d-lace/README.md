# Cropped crowns on the compact laced layout

**Exploratory.** The #3 chain passes through a passive U; its two head ends draw on a travelling rocker. The [mechanics fixture](../cand-d-beaded-lace/README.md) bounds the imbalance *only* while the free tip is positively retained, the rocker floats off its stops, and the legs do not bear on outside guides. Do not carry that constraint into unthreading. This study fits finite contact stock to an intermediate package; it is not a successor declaration.

## Loaded face

The Take 53 integration datum is x=16.5 head face, 38 mm pitch, boss centres at x=17.04 on an R13 triangle clocked −90°. Ports at y=±6, z=0. Normal directions are 60° off X. Force lines pass through the original R1.85 sphere centres nominally. Instead of full spheres, each of the six tail lands has a cropped R8 convex nose, radius .90, .051 mm sag, .35 backing to a prismatic root. The six head flats each have a 2.04 × 2.40 mm working rectangle. Outer hulls enclose the curved noses; their flat outer bounds are not the contact shapes.

`face.py` solves the six frictionless equilibrium equations. For each row of the inverse contact matrix it minimizes the two-end draw contribution over both extremes of the bounded axial fraction, including arbitrary lateral directions within the indicated cone. It debits every seat's normal and moment error using axial balance at the first zero reaction; a singular-value check rules out rank loss within the same perturbation. For R8 crowns the virtual centre shift adds (8−1.85)·2sin(.6°/2) mm to the moment uncertainty. The loads are the earlier wide force/torque box with the revised joint torque at 38 mm pitch and a 20.65 mm foot-arm trial. These are conditional sufficient draws for the *existence* of all-compressive normal reactions, not actual measured loads or positive lower bounds on every frictional reaction.

Requires both endpoint-force directions within 6.7° of X, endpoint axial ratio ≤1.16, .18 mm port position error, .19 mm seat-centre error and .6° total normal error, *including elasticity*. Thus 39 N from the earlier equal-end, 3° study cannot be transferred. Taking 60 N as an imposed upper draw, a 70 g table load and μ=.23 gives total normal ≤218.34 N by axial balance, also an upper bound on one pole. The R8 sphere-on-plane model (213 GPa, ν=.29, both parts) gives radius .224 mm and 2.074 GPa peak pressure there; the trial crown has .402 mm radial margin after the location and normal error. This is not an allowable fatigue or yield pressure, or evidence of finished-part hardness. Edge, beam, relative pose and fastening tolerances still require a structural model. The rocker, seat, force and friction limits are design targets, not supplier ratings.

For comparison, a narrow pair of ports at y=1±2 feeding an Ω-shaped R6 passive return reduces draw sensitivity even with no tension-sharing bound. At 62°, R13 in the *clock-zero* trial, 3° port cone and 70 g it needs 52.32 N table draw. The inverse bends cost 18.44 mm centreline depth and 46.04 mm path, vs 9.50 and 25.85 for the R6 U. This is not an installed alternative. [Tsubaki's Epsilon datasheet](https://tsubaki.eu/assets/tsubaki-tc-epsilon-2025-3-en.pdf), pp. 3–4, gives 1.905 mm pitch, .36 kN **minimum tensile**, and a 9-tooth sprocket of 5.57 mm pitch diameter, not a lateral-bend or custom end-link rating. [Sava](https://www.savacable.com/sava-expertise/pulleys-design-information) recommends 25 uncoated rope diameters at the pulley root for 7×19; its [Ø.018-inch rope](https://www.savacable.com/products/precision-and-small-cable/cable-bare-7x19) needs about 11.43 mm root diameter. Smooth rope does not supply positive feed or a rated tip.

## What the installation screen actually covers

`take53-provisional.json` freezes the early **short-motor** envelope, not its later revision. `twin_crowns.py` replaces the six facet reserves and three ball landings, three rear bridges and two sill posts. A lower crossbar ties to the two old sills; an outside positive-Y tower links it to the top rail. Two thin roof spines run above the transverse motor, joined by a yoke avoiding the upper boards; the rear tie meets the fixed draw support. The bounding-hull sum for the separate frame pieces is 1102.24 mm³ before union or machining. Do not count it as zero mass; the structural frame, joints and attachments have not been rated.

Every positive projection gap is a separating-plane lower bound. Head/tail axes have nonnegative x, so a positive bound also holds through aligned forward withdrawal. On the local stock: ≥.5471 mm from new flats to inherited passive stock, ≥.4351 from the frame to all mated stock, .20 mm from crowns to extended stripper, and ≥1.8525 mm between a nonpartner crown and flat. The central ball contacts and bridge-tie/body overlap are intentional. At reference draw the new support clears the rocking stock by ≥.3472 mm through ±17° (continuous rotation debit included). In the larger ±2 mm translation box the conservative bound is −.0505 mm; an unproved gap is **not** a material crossing. Rocker motion against other fixed hardware, two-module yaw, local loads in the roof, composite deflection, seating assembly, rear swept stock and tilted tail reach are not certified. Fabrication and tolerance margins are not subtracted from these nominal gaps.

Reproduce with Python, NumPy and SciPy from here: `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONHASHSEED=0 python3 -B face.py` and `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONHASHSEED=0 python3 -B twin_crowns.py`. No changes to criteria, candidates or official results.

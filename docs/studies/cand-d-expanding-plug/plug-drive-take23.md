## Actual terminal, shared package and decision

1. **Unsigned end travel is not clearance.** At x25.03 this solid nose contains a central disk radius ≥8.289. A one-piece stripper passing it has inradius >p5. Three outer rods do not constrain arbitrary tilt. Need direct true tail pose (three noncollinear known targets plus retention/lateral discrimination); no credit for motor, one rod or one missing pad. A directly measured 17.30 *tail* displacement, .45 cage advance and .40 axial error leave .42 on face stock R≤19.8 at 6° but −.615 at 9° in the plane screen. At whole-body R31, the 6° screen is −.748 (x_min=23.4925 and pivot x28.4925). Count initial pad gap, compliance and tilted pad contact; retract under opposing loads and check continuous body/table sweep. Bounding-plane failure is not itself a collision.
2. **Distributed round ends are attractive but blocked here.** For equal b3 hemispheres at centre/R11×6 and hoop major R19, worst projected distance in footprint is max(11/sqrt(3), inter-ring bisector distance at 30°). With D bounded around 6.4, p5+b3=8 implies n_x≈.60 and axial surplus ~.41 per N after μ=.23, only while nearest cap is supported over full stroke/depth and p5 centre is on the forward branch. Edge of hoop/backside and external approaches still need treatment. An R3 central stem crosses the sole crown at x25.806 and three parked heels at r1.53.

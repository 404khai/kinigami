# Take 25 — inward bulb latch: drive and stowed package

**Result: exploratory. Reject the installed package at 35 mm straight pitch.** Keep the L18 shoe and cam-follower idea for a loaded single-station fixture, not a Gate 1 candidate. Two independent solid-stock witnesses matter: the aft pivot shaft is inside its neighbour's motor, and the stationary sleeve needed for even 4 mm cup travel is more than 60 mm from the far side of the tail. Neither a different clocking of the whole drive nor moving the centre of its enclosing sphere repairs these witnesses.

No central draw crown, per-lobe torus, deep blind tail floor or separate 55 N draw preload. Preload/take-up cannot be routed through PA12 or an unmetered hard stop.

For lever length 18, h₀=2.450, q=asin((h−h₀)/18); the neutral button is V−18t. Positive rotation takes −t toward +n. The crank at neutral is V−9.50n−5.00t (radius 10.735); its pin centre runs from (12.468,18.862,12.693) at h3.020 to (11.667,19.925,16.791) at h−4.10. Pin moves 4.098 tangentially; its varying n-coordinate needs a two-sided slot, not a rigid attachment to a straight nut. Ø3.10 stem runs 6.15 rearward along the rotating normal to a 4.40-wide, 2.20-thick beam. The entire beam clears the three axial *motor bodies* by ≥2.49 in x. At e−4 a flattened 2.90-wide follower clears the beam in e by .35 before play and load. The pin extension, boss and cheek are not thereby cleared.

The guided fork shifts the crank; turning the same screw backward unloads the closing flexure **then mechanically pulls the captive opening shoulder**; no spring-only return. - **Metering and faults:** measure actual downstream torque at each shoe; 32±.8 N tangent means 576±14.4 N·mm about the pivot. A 2.15 mm flexure stroke carrying ~65–75 N suggests ~30–35 N/mm; not a calibrated spring. A shoulder that bottoms on an early obstruction defeats the cap. Provide an independently tripping close-side axial overload clutch before bottoming, a positive opening dog and unloaded park retention. The clutch threshold, free stroke under an early jam, latch-off/rearm, flexure calibration, residual bias and self-hold are **not demonstrated**. A screw-input torque limiter alone does not bound shoe load tightly (see below). Do not freeze or hold a real load with this layout.

## Moving load, motor and bearings

The coordinating 90 g calculation assumes verified nine-contact seating, μ≤.23, .028 chord normal error, .30 position error and **actual** 32±.8 N/shoe tangent. Its worst frictionless separating-face residual is +1.800 N. Its Coulomb upper bounds are 213.731 N on one rear pad, 147.301 N on one V flank and 192.064 N summed at one shoe. Use 198 N resultant incl. friction and 214 N rear for structure; do not double a maximum single-flank load or divide a total by three. With .40 radius rear land the .028 rear tilt does not fit the Hertz patch: separately verify ≤.012 rear tilt or redesign and repeat. No verified 90 g vehicle mass.

At full lock on the high side of angle tolerance, take 12 N·mm of pivot drag *as a moving-load trial only*. Slot normal arms are |dt/dq| and |dn/dq|. The pin force is at most (32.8×18+12)/(|dt/dq|−μ_slot|dn/dq|) under this model. The keyed crosshead needs two guide planes 7.5 apart resisting ≤5.1 mm normal eccentricity; reaction sum model ≤2F×5.1/7.5+μ_slot F, with an additional 2 N drag. The screw calculation uses pitch diameter 1.74, 60° thread, flank μ=.18 and 1 N·mm thrust-bearing loss. These are test conditions, not a catalogue worst case.

## Cup, fabrication, observation and recovery

Take 24's oblique L18 bore axis t=.16549−.182294z (z along n) and actual radius 2.475–2.500 clear the full button/shank by only .0168 after .020 axis error. The rear of the fixed throat must end by z−1.90 and open laterally; do not count a nonexistent continuous rear tube. With 1.50–1.55 wall and h−4.10±.02, tip ≤−1.670, then independent relative error .060; translate only after all three shoes are parked. For the intact front enclosure, the 45° recessed mouth, shallow blend and minimum annulus give ≥.0868 N/N outward for empty lip. The matching arc/free-coefficient ≥.02978 N/N includes rigid-sphere spin μb/L; the L19 .031 figure is **not** used. Remaining drag ≤.008 N/N and residual close bias ≤.40 N are unverified prerequisites. Unload before opening. A motor-angle endpoint is not a clearance observation.

The separate powered bowl has a hole-free floor at x+6.30 home, three rear metal pad stops and a continuous mouth band. Two keys/rods on a rigid yoke clocked off the crossed tabs, retained nuts/screw in both directions, physical extended hold and independently sensed home are trial allocations. At 9 mm all pads move with the floor and openings shift 7.19 along the original facet; the deep-park/oblique-port package uses the complete sleeve, **not just the projection of a shifted circular hole**. A preliminary pair of rods at R15.6 with 2.35 bosses clips a proposed R17.60 race bore by .35. The dry-guide screen for 8 N useful push needs 25.85 N screw thrust (9.8 guide spacing/9.2 takeoff offset); this does not meet every possible free-leading case. No installed yoke, keys, cup motor, stop stiffness or powered return under jam is proven. Actual 3D clearance of the bevel/quill stock to the rear floor, 18° facets and moving skin is also unresolved.

Construct the bulb as two formed shells around a metal neck collar and an internal load spider joining the hard V/rear lands. A trial Ø24.96 × .8 undimpled 304 shell is 11.74 g at .008 g/mm³; the three nominal motors/gearheads add ~15.6 g before supports. [Hollow stainless reference](https://sdballs.com/product/stainless-steel-hollow-balls/) supports formed sphere diameters/wall classes, **not** a profiled latch rating. Join from the inside or weld and finish the outside flush over buried seams, with the remaining closure under the sealed neck collar. Do not put exposed weld reinforcement or a fastener across a probe path. Inspect wall transition/continuous rolling-ball profile, welded seam (NDT), internal spider, neck/land proof and fatigue before closing the second shell. No proof of invisible seam strength, hardened-case depth or completed mass. The Ø3.10 elbow section alone screens ~479 MPa gross at 198 N; the uncorrelated thin whole-beam weak-axis screen is ~1.09 GPa before notches. Neither is a strength certification.

Assembly order: fixture/inspect shell and rear cartridge; fit each arm and axle in split cheeks from the rear; fit guide, flexure and two-sided roller before bolting on the cup/sleeve; install drive pods from the aft side and the cell with guards last. **Blocked at aft shaft–motor overlap.** Removing the outer housing or changing assembly order doesn't allow two permanent solids to coincide. On power loss, inhibit further structural motion, keep unsupported links externally supported, do not return the cup against an occupied mouth. Manual service would require independent access to each drive and removable tail restraint; no safe access in a loaded chain is proven. Witness all six flank and three rear seats, each actual transmitted effort, every parked shoe and the bowl's actual displacement/yaw/hold, then direct tail clearance before return. Never infer success from one switch, encoder or current. A slip, missing seat, backdrivable nut, mismatch, power loss or stuck arm is an unresolved state, not a successful release.

The larger 39° branch with a separate piston on off-axis runners clears nominal crossed tabs at 45°, not the support/rim assembly; its R13.5 hoop/telescope trial spans 68.78 straight and at least 63.54 over the earlier ±15° hinge range.

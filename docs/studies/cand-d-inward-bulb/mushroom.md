# Take 24 — a central mushroom, three swing shoes, and a moving bowl

**Result: exploratory. Build one loaded shoe/port and one rear-pad coupon; do not freeze a coupler.** The three-flank-pair geometry and a small rear piston dimple can coexist on a single Ø24.96 mm tail. A rigid R2.4 shoe need not cross its bore on a long, heavily side-loaded slide. The selected tangential swing clears a tilted bore over its complete stroke. The existing direct motors do **not** fit the swing arm. Neither a replacement actuator nor a complete passively locked 60 mm package is demonstrated.

Run from this directory with Python 3 and no third-party modules:

```sh
python3 -B mushroom.py
python3 -B translating_bowl.py
python3 -B bowl_swing.py
```

Every core point is ≥p+b from O: the inflated button exclusion is inside both cutters. The contacting flank is backed around the whole cap, its physical patch is ≥.354 mm inside the opposite working cavity, and every other station's core cutter clears that entire cap by ≥1.929 mm. No saddle matches a button centre to a fictitious pose. The exact solid may need offset grinding/EDM and inspection; two subtractive sphere cuts in **finished** stock are not this construction.

**Rear and connecting stock.** In addition to the cutters, retain core points k with max over the three pads of w·(k−Q)≥0, Q=C+p w. This gives a rear-facing dimple rather than a projecting flange. ## Moving bowl: the retained volume, the cut, and the empty hole

Use a one-piece moving metal bowl, not a telescope as well. Its rear pads push forward with it. In port coordinates z along n, v along e, ρ radial in the facet, remove stock to

```
z = min(0, max(−.40−v tan8°, −.32(5.30−ρ))).
```

Nominal maximum removal .7955 mm. Gauge a ≥44° front mouth plane, return slope ≤.34 and an uninterrupted original planar annulus R5.35–6.75. The return has ≥.0888 N/N positive axial projection. At a sharp ideal empty lip a tangent p-ball's intersection disk in the mouth plane must fit within the closed elliptical rim; its radius cannot exceed the ellipse's minor semiaxis. The front edge normal cone cannot point near −m (m is the mouth normal), excluding the spurious rear-sphere solution. Conservative half-width 2.56 gives actual draft ≥17.81° and ≥.0868 N/N outward with Coulomb friction. For a bore-side normal the opposed ring intersects the probe. A disk crossing into the bore from the front cannot reach the rear cut: for the selected tilted bore the rear opening is >.121 mm deeper along m than maximum p-ball intrusion. These are **geometric** bounds; do not load a sharp cut edge as a Hertz-bearing surface. A production lip radius and its contact strength still need their own rolling-core proof and coupon.

Stop the moving wall between z=−1.50 and −1.55. At h=−4.10±.02 the shoe tip is at most −1.670; even allowing .060 mm relative positioning/sweep error it is behind the entire moving shell provided that shell stays above the original outer plane −1.55. Forward translation s increases clearance by s sin36.7° or more. Thus no hardware may be left projecting through the moving holes; keep fixed bearings, back stops, wiring and return edges out of the whole cup sweep. The yoke's actual location, yaw and each parked tip require sensing, not just motor encoder counts. At 4 mm actual yoke travel there is ≥2.45 mm normal clearance even to the moved *back* plane. This is local clearance, not a claim that a free tail followed the yoke by 4 mm.

**Front enclosure is a condition, not an empty hole.** The inward funnel is the intersection of six free half-spaces, also clipped at the front by a 16° circular bore with R17.70 at the deck. Its concave creases do not expose a negative face; their possible reactions are positive combinations of the incident positive-draft normals (on a perfect concave crease a finite interior p-ball cannot touch both at their intersection). The back is blind. A circular moving rim R18.25, 1.30 deep, runs inside a complete positively drafted fixed sleeve, R18.39 at the home deck, actual ≥13.8°. Keep fixed sleeve ahead of the furthest rim by .30. Maximum modelled radial slit is 1.3441; first-entry sag <.040 through that slit. A p-ball cannot pass behind the rim. For an outward-facing touch on the conical band, the opposite stationary circle at the same x intersects the ball whenever clearance <2p times its outward radial normal component; this excludes the low/negative-x band normals. No slender plate/neck is admitted through that slit. The band, sleeve, blind rear, and as-built exterior shell must be continuous for the front-connected-component argument. The outside of the sleeve and other body surfaces are **not** thereby globally certified. Front hull dimensions at 4/6/9 mm stroke need Ø39.79/40.82/42.36 incl a .40 outer wall; this is a diameter screen only. It does not place these circles inside a 60 mm containing sphere against the motors and the far end. The earlier unclipped R19.50 hoop at x=+2.80 is optional, not used to prove this variant.

## Controls and stop conditions

1. Index/hard-stop the bowl and prove all nine contacts seated; check load, pose, normal error and rear land. Verify actual per-shoe tangent effort 32±.8 N, limited take-up and structural hard backing. One cap disappearing is not a seated state.
2. Positively remove stored closing effort and hard-back reaction. Verify the worst shoe's residual torque/drag while the tail can load it. A stuck support or charged spring invalidates the free-lead bound.
3. Permit each shoe to lead to deep park; prove individual clearance and actual direction. With any remaining leader use its conditional force budget, not the all-parked value.
4. Advance the guarded bowl only after all tips/backs are clear. Measure actual delivered push and independent tail displacement or disappearance of reactions; distinguish separation from a stalled yoke or a moving bowl with no tail contact. Remating, outside-body contacts and recovery from power loss during partial travel remain open.

**Comparisons, not combined parts.** (i) An intact Ø20 single-dish bulb on the earlier independent 50–55 N draw cartridge has exact b_in=8.0961 nominal, 8.0410 conservatively; one contact ≤142.55 N only with an independent ≤16 N net lateral limit, otherwise axial-only ≤382.90 N. It still needs the external seats and cartridge. (ii) A straight Ø4.8 guide with two rows 2.2 apart bounds summed bearing load at 731 N, requiring effective starting friction ≤.00695 for the .035 N/N guide budget. A single [IKO LWL2](https://www.ikont.co.jp/global_data/download/pdf_catalog/cat5510.1ML.pdf), table p11, screens above unity even assigning its best one-block 0.64 N·m moment rating in a simple force-plus-moment sum (not a manufacturer's combined allowable); 12.5 mm block plus ≥7.175 stroke needs ≥19.675 along its rail. LWL1's 113 N static capacity is below the transverse-force screen alone. Neither nominal dynamic friction nor the two-block moment row qualifies starting drag. (iii) A separate straight Ø14 ejector cylinder fails the independent common-translation certificate with the parked buttons; 11.5° skirt draft can pass for the bounded bulb core, not the long backing, and ≥13.5° actual draft is sufficient without that pose argument. An 18 mm telescope at 17.5° with one .65 wall/.12 clearance/2.35 overlap reaches about R9.90 at the required guard's back edge x−7.778/R9.300. It is not the proposed moving bowl.

**Decision boundary.** The new shape eliminates a tiny moving blend and the six separate tail lobes; it does not demonstrate an installed coupler. Next discriminators: ground lip and last-contact indentation, loaded swing/shaft deflection and datum error, measured starting torque, stationary shell and yoke interferences, non-self-locking commanded unload, full 60 mm mass/package, and actual load/pose sensing.

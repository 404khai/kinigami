# Take 70 — keyed rotor and hollow tail
**Result: exploratory. Do not advance the installation.** The installed reservation can be enclosed nominally in a 59.948 mm sphere through the modeled articulation. That is not an assembled fit, a mass certificate or a loaded-release result. The front insertion route is blocked, the dog does not have unconditional power-off retention, and its small geared actuator cannot extract under the adverse 500 Nmm example at its optimistic continuous torque. The proposed deep spherical cup conflicts directly with the quill.

This is a fresh study, not a change to archived Take 67, cand-d, Q12, Q85, Q100, any threshold or any official result. No Gate, candidate, manufacturing release or test of a physical prototype. All calculations and raw outputs here have the single result label **exploratory**.

## Force paths and what stops motion
**Disk.** Local stationary flat −6.95→+1.58 over 8.53 travel; R12.42 running inside mouth R14.54. Three R10.68-station rollers with ±.0762 axial flank freedom give a tilted-plane rim interval **+1.43645..+1.72355**. The rear limit for the *forward/in-bore center branch only* uses g=2.17, backing R=2.45 and R−sqrt(R²−g²)=1.312635. Rear margin 0.12381 mm. At the upper endpoint use nx=min(sqrt(1−(g/R)²),1−pmax/R); cos(acos(nx)+0.6°)−0.23 sin(acos(nx)+0.6°)=**+0.06613**. A further 0.15231 mm forward error exhausts that force margin; 0.12381 mm aft error exhausts the rear margin. Bearing play beyond the modeled flanks, compliance, rotor tilt, dimensional error and mount deformation are not bounded. No claim for all centers/contacts or about the amount of reaction available.

**Rotor and hollow tail.** The 7075 rotor has finite keyed steel disk-track, face-track and worm-ring inserts (three integral tabs each), not a coating. The radii/axial lands and complementary pockets are editable. At 500 Nmm charged to just one tab, nominal average bearing is about 43/26/22 MPa respectively; axial loading, tab roots, parting and joint strength unverified. Disk/face sampled tool sweeps are inflated 0.02020/0.02415 mm. Nominal smallest rear walls 0.204/0.566 mm; pressure angles up to 52.58°/20.53°. The separate analytic worm/hob defines engagement; the SCAD rotor alone does not cut the tooth profile. The steel tail uses 2.50/1.66 rolled offsets about the same core, three radial ribs, diaphragm, port sleeves and blind end closures. Nominal external excluded volume is not steel mass. Actual hub/port joints, seams, finish, fatigue and resistance to local crushing are unverified.

**Guidance.** Each sliding crosshead has opposing X faces on two radial rows separated by ≥4.60 mm. Axial gap 0.040 per side, useful transverse width .65; both rows remain registered across the modeled leaf stroke. With a 9.5 mm axial moment arm, a provisional 140 N radial force needs 289 N per row, about 542 MPa nominal average on one .65×.82 pad and 28.9 N two-row drag at μ=.05. Two tangential cheeks alone do not react this pitch. Gross conditional 102 N/seat and 228 N/jaw from the **70 g** Take 67 load box give ~140.45 N in the central link and ~900 MPa simple P-pin bending; not transferable to the actual unknown mass or proof of survival. The 28±10% N spring example gives 858–1049 MPa conditional Wahl stress. Actual stock condition, individual loads, tilt, lubrication and cyclic life are open. The conservative spring force is not reduced for internal guide drag during seated virtual work. Travel does not prove any button seated.

**Drive and both torque signs.** A 3 V 0615 / 256:1 motor, always-meshed 16/32 spur pair and 130T worm drive the rotor. A separate 4:1 geared motor drives a blind finite cam, roller yoke and Ø.43 straight pin into a 12-hole staked square-journal star (R1.31 index). The drive never disengages. One dog step is 0.231° ring, .00395 mm nominal draw / 0.055 N spring change. At a registered hole there is 1.28 mm straight overlap; at 1.5° worm misalignment only the chamfer admits the pin. Opposite flanks of the fully seated shank geometrically block either torque sign. The analytical tooth witnesses block by ≤0.22° wheel displacement on either side over all 12 registrations **if the bolt is seated and the worm radially centered**, with 3.20° shaft-angle debit and ±.015 mm axial worm float. Not total loaded backlash. Stops first meet at ring −3.294°/+105.794°.

The finite cam has end walls and an over-center portion; its slope at the nominal parked point favors one axial load sign and not the other. A stop is not a bidirectional cam latch. At an illustrative 500 Nmm external torque and μ=.23, even lossless torque reflection gives .675 N extraction friction; peak lift 1.231 mm/rad requires **.831 Nmm vs .562 Nmm optimistic rated output**. The 3 V motor's .221 Nmm stall also yields only .796 Nmm at 4:1 and the maximum .90 efficiency. No credit for ordinary worm friction, a reverse command, or assuming the shank unloads. Unconditional power-off retention, gearhead output play, yoke antirotation and loaded withdrawal are open. Superseded support roots are preserved (R.2597 spindle and R.0171 pin collisions); offset roots now clear the extended working cylinders by ≥.120/.114 mm. Housing connections are geometric, not rated.

**Assembly failure.** A solid disk axle shares an R.160 mm ball with untouched front-shoulder stock during straight front insertion of the preassembled disk and rollers. Loose radial axles or a split ring need a removable closure, re-established load path, positive axial retention and real tool clearance. A 9 mm star-removal corridor cleared selected unrelated stock only; a staked journal is not automatically serviceable. No verified sequence exists for the captured steel inserts, disk, bearing caps and spring cartridges. A commanded final state is not physical assembly or release.

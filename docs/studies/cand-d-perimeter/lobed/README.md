## Moving phase: what the angular cover does and does not establish

Use a common clockwise-to-counterclockwise opening rate of one rad/s and trial tail velocity λ along +X. In the PARK chart the relative velocity for a rotating pole is V=(−z−λ cosθ,u−λ sinθ,0). For n **from tail into metal**, the worst contribution per unit normal is n·V−μ|n×V|. A fixed pole instead needs −n_x−μ sqrt(1−n_x²)≥0. This compares real contact forces on **both bodies**, not a sequence of prescribed tail states. Where every force meets the inequality, external adverse axial force Aext can cost at most λ Aext in total rotating-axis torque, before real drive friction, preload, inertia or unmodeled support. Not a bound on individual normal pressure or per-leaf torque in an overconstrained linkage.

At λ=1.10 mm/rad the nominal interior contact set passes a continuous cover through 0..β:

- Conical lower face, vertical toe and side faces, lower side/toe/rear trims, both compound side corners, and the toe+side line.
- A smooth concave rear circle (rho=1.16) cannot support an R2.45 sphere in its interior by second variation. At the arc/side edge the radial normal component ≤1.16/2.45; all remaining normals there are included.
- Fixed .20-skin cheek inner side, lower edge and forward edge/corner. Intact cone slopes and the rounded-direction cone at their kink assist axially. The fixed rear cap's forward convex arc also assists: u>.30 for exposed rho=1.10 above its cone and below w+.95, so its unit normal has forward x>.30/1.10. A blend with the lower cone normal retains the sign.

The checker's **occupied point cloud is not a clearance mesh**. For each interval box in phase, feature position and positive normal-cone mixtures it either lower-bounds the worst Coulomb projection across the entire box, or proves every candidate inscribed R2.45 center penetrates at least one actual fixed/moving point. Analytic bounds on q, normalized normals, rotations and feature slopes are debited; no conclusion depends on failure to find a nearby point. Normalizing a vector of midpoint length l with perturbation e<l changes its direction by ≤asin(e/l); rotations displace a point by ≤radius×angle. The rear roof root is monotone near rho=1.16 and abs(dp/dt)<.18. Symmetry covers both t ends. 3,254 certified boxes, no untested midpoint interpolation. For a larger backing ball its tangent R2.45 sub-ball suffices. This is nominal rigid geometry; **it does not certify moving tolerances**, upper/back surfaces of the leaf, the rear high-depth p trim, the seat/screw/clevis, or outer cheeks and housings.

# Take 64 — fixed bore, pivot leaves, captured rising disk

**Exploratory; reject this installation.** A 120°-repeated two-track drum provides both signs of axial disk reaction and puts the roller input behind each leaf. The finished drum cuts through the retained joint spine, the lower rod cuts through a worm bearing pillar, and a Geneva handoff is unbacked. No preload, release, durability, fabrication or Gate claim. No change to Q12, Q85, Q100 or the 60 mm envelope.

Three posts carry R.98 rollers on R.73 radial journals at R10.68, phase +60° from jaws, positively captured by two axial steel flanks throughout. Three stationary pad pairs straddle moving key ribs; they react side force/torsion, not axial load. Each post transfers a sign-changing axial load through a roller to the drum; the drum bearing flange sits between opposed stationary thrust pads returning via rails to the chassis. No central screw or compression-only pads. Front bore is not a rear frame: the outboard mount and steel return are listed.

Inner groove opens on ID, outer leaf groove opens on OD; both in one axially fixed rotor. See cutters, unwrapped contours and editable working-part CSG. Cut the **union** of all blanks. Disk/leaf track minimum sampled repeat webs in arc–X projection 6.46/2.52 mm before sampling enlargement. The apparently negative disk gap from endcap *arc alone* is not material overlap: adjacent caps lie at different X. Cutter stock after sampling enlargement: core 1.338/1.954 mm, axial end wall only .173/.234 mm. These are geometry, not rated webs. Positive/negative axial home flanks are physically separate; finite clearance gives .213 mm total disk dwell play, .165 mm worst edge-skew contribution across three independent roller gaps before compliance. No zero-backlash registration claim. Wheel is a tooth blank with analytic mesh ratio, not a manufactured tooth set.

Reverse unloads the disk groove onto home dwell before the leaf changes angle. With the flange physically following the analytic tangent line and a fully extended button, conditional spring pickup occurs at 13.354°, then continued draw compresses the buttons. This is not a demonstrated approach from a tilted, jammed or unseated part.

## Drive and power-off

One Ø6×32.8 mm transverse motor/gearhead reserve; 16:32 parallel gears, eight-slot wheel fixed to the single-start worm shaft, module .30 / R18 / 120T drum. Motor-facing fork and independent worm thrust races (both signs). One driver revolution gives 45° worm, .375° drum; 560 gearhead turns/105° (5.6 minutes at illustrative 100 rpm). The Geneva pin actively drives the worm on index; no credit for worm friction. At 200/500 Nmm, ring tangential/worm axial forces 11.11/27.78 N. For μ=.12, 20° pressure angle, pitch radius 1.50 and lead angle 5.71°, illustrative efficiency .434; peak gearhead demand 2.07/5.17 Nmm including assumed .72/.80 transmission efficiencies. This is not gear, bearing or speed verification. FAULHABER 0615 S .156 mNm ×256 ×.60 = optimistic 23.96 Nmm, not simply the 06/1 gearhead's 25 Nmm rating. Conditional spring-only torque in intermediate *pickup* reaches 653 Nmm near 13.35°, even though near the nominal detent it is 233 Nmm. Disk peak slope 14.735 mm/rad, pressure angle 54.07°: **589, 1,105, 1,665 Nmm** for 40,75,113 N resisted ejection before friction, guide torque and losses. A nominal 500 Nmm budget is not a release rating.

Registered Geneva at driver 180°: rotating the wheel by ±3° puts real sector stock in the wheel. However at driver 90° the pin is .283 mm outside the wheel and the steel sector clears even at ±3° output; Lipschitz bounds allow interpolation of that small interval (max .120 mm wheel-point travel for 1.5°). No safe positive handoff or power-loss hold throughout; do not release a load relying on this drive. Tooth/journal strength, output lash and tendon-free phase flag remain unresolved. Real stops register only the endpoints. A coded nonperiodic output datum independent of the motor and six independent seat reaction channels are needed. The model allocates seat bridges, rear plunger readers, wire reserve and ring pickup volumes; there are no calibrated channels or validated control.

Q85 conditional sequence: check all six pole reactions and three real button forces/deflections; on the nonzero final slope approach the upper band only if the next complete index remains inside every force window including backlash, guide/meter error and independent spring scatter. In ideal commanded increments alone the last .375° (−3..+10°) adds ≤.00962 mm / .260 N. Measured-at-rest recheck after settling at the **physically blocked** detent, discriminate jam/overload from correct reactions, never infer hold from drive current. ±.15 mm individual installed offsets already remove the common acceptable stroke in the nominal spring example. The fork/cam free play can shift spring force by roughly five N even at nominal mid-stroke, before disk play. No bounded true loaded final increment proven. Stall: cutoff independently, hold/recover with external mechanical support, relieve load and reverse the still-engaged drive only when safe; an uncontrolled handoff prevents claiming autonomous recovery.

## Buttons, forces and weak sections

Tail buttons centred at X−4.31 on R10.3 and +60° from jaws. The same flat insert base X−6.95 / top −5.00 is used, **65°** loaded flank, paired sides drafted outward to base toe 3.81, width ±1.35. Pole has .245 mm residual finite tangent patch after .25 spherical stock rounding, none with .50. This is not a Hertz/contact rating. R2.50 spherical fronts, guided shanks and three separate springs behind flange, no springs at the leaves. Explicit negative bores remove flange/socket material at the three stations. Rear cap/thread support, stop tube, front travel shoulder, foot, separated shank/cap bushes, position target, radial backing and spring volume are listed; guides require a fitted assembly/cartridge, not a smooth no-friction oracle. At the face X−4.75 the radial port gap is .110..199 mm over stroke. Port-edge blends, a non-catching mouth and R≥2.45 on *all reachable front stock*, particularly at cartridge ports/seams, are **not** established. Back-insert cartridges before fixing the fork if possible; driver, retainer access and wiring extraction not demonstrated.

Parent 9-DOF conditional study: service/fault minima 24.690/32.726 N, table 35.404 N nominal ±10% for 65°; **60° distinctly needs 49.368 N**. qx=.60 26 N does not transfer. Trial coil mean Ø3.50, wire .80, 3.5 active turns, 5.5 total, assumed G=79 GPa (not selected, measured or catalog qualified). Midpoint L4.75, k26.95 N/mm, loaded band 31.864..38.944 N, nominal solid margin .219 mm at upper; ideal stress up to 924 MPa. The distinct 60° need exceeds calculated solid travel at the same free length. Captive extension/compression .40/.22 mm, preload at forward stop 24.62 N. Installed effective forces and scatter unmeasured. At 35.404 N on a single 65° flank, clean side-load 75.92 N. Real bush spacing 7.93 and overhang 3.115 mm give possible .03-friction drag 4.07 N, against ±3.54 band; the shorter-span sensitivity is 4.23 N. Guide drag is internal and dissipative, **not** subtracted from spring S in an already seated escape proof; it blocks equating spring travel with true all-pole seating/loaded release.

Use supplied **single-contact** gross envelopes (S=36±10%, not the 35.404 N spring force): for effective guide coefficient 0/.05, seat normal 197/261 N and jaw normal 410/534 N. Independent maxima, not simultaneous and not equally split across three. Clean centered jaw at those loads transmits 714/929 Nmm about H; even an optimistic equal split **between the two tiny bars on that one leaf** gives 3.53/4.59 GPa torsional shear at R.22, and 1.35/1.76 GPa average direct shear. The one-sided drive does not justify that split. Single-pole 40 N axial working-point stall sensitivity alone is 250.2 Nmm on that leaf per mechanics. Dogleg, bearing, single ear, cheek, rail and flank strengths not qualified. The lead's actual-basis LP controls, not motor current.

Clean one-seat normals at the above two loads, with transverse reaction at X−10.11, require signed post forces (+81.3,+57.0,−55.0) and (+107.7,+75.5,−72.9) N. Thus three compression-only stops would fail; both flanks physically exist here, but worst estimated journal bending 317/419 MPa before stress concentrations. Isolated key torsion 1.84/2.44 N·m (N·m, not Nmm), not canceled without other simultaneous contacts. Gross unslotted seat-root normal+bending 120/159 MPa; at worst rise conditional contact pressures reach 1.83/2.11 GPa assuming E*=115 GPa/full roller width. Neither fatigue nor edge loading certified. No delivered hold or force rating.

### Attempt A: barrel and biased shoes
External captured groove on OD barrel works against three stationary followers; captured inner helix takes positive axial motion from an M3×.5 screw nut/roller. Ring-local face grooves move three jaw saddles. Barrel → thrust cheeks → antirotating conical sleeve. Define barrel angle θ=0..105°; jaw rise 1.45 on 7..44° then dwell, sleeve retreat h=8.35 on 49..98° then dwell, both half-cosine. Axial input displacement 6.40θ/105+h, giving a unique monotone motion in both directions. Ring has separate external and internal cuts with an intact radial band R18.10..19.95 (the yaw motor crossing is inside **that band**, not a filled groove). A common motor → bevel → indexed screw drives the sequence, not an attempt to force the Geneva wheel backwards. Captured radial dwell carries clamp load, fixed roller groove bears axial sleeve reaction, braces close to hinge. Radial running support, guide assembly and real load sharing unresolved: e.g. the bearing pillars themselves cross the parked barrel. A rigid-shoe rerun retains these vetoes.

Each radial saddle commands its shoe open/closed positively. Axial compliance **only**: individually guided Ø8/Ø4.2×.20 SCHNORR 000550, four series, free length 1.80, nominal compressed lengths 1.300..1.048 for 33..39 N, centre setting 1.180. Finite Ø8.34 fixed seat, moving Ø8.30 annular shoulder, Ø3.88 pilot, keepers and mechanical travel toes included at station R18.65. Stack encoded as OD keepout, not material for collisions. The axial package fails independently: front metal seat and sharp shoe enclose an R.235 ball; more than .115 remains after R.12 blend allowance. Stroke at each end consumes more than .11 window axial clearance (.010/.022 overrun); extent alone not a collision proof. At full radial throw lower metal seat z=−24.270 vs floor −23.50. Do not add the spring to B by subtracting a notional volume. Parent's 34.10-N equivalent midpoint threshold is conditional on verified actual installed forces; catalogue interpolation is not a measured band. At 36 N axial, slope .38 and μ=.23, closing can require 261 N radial and 200 Nmm axial eccentric moment at the guide. Even .10 sliding friction at 250 N offers 25 N hysteresis; just ±3 N parasitic would require μ≤.012 ignoring the moment. Spring force is not delivered shoe load.

Closing from **real detent**: biased heel rests on rear stop with 0.12 travel to nominal. Contact starts on last s=.0456 of radial closure; with stationary presented flange the shoe translates toward +X to compress its stack. On the actual 10th and 11th mid-indexes measured from closed, the pole survives R.12, tangent margins .136/.088. Using the supplied 33–39 N nominal linear sensitivity, sliding μ=.23, screw η=.28, 16:30 bevel η=.80, index η=.72, required gearhead torques 28.62/37.80 Nmm **before** guide drag. Optimistic [0615 S](https://www.faulhaber.com/fileadmin/Import/Media/EN_0615_S_FMM.pdf) + [06/1](https://www.faulhaber.com/fileadmin/Import/Media/EN_06-1_FMM.pdf) 256:1 continuous ≤23.96 Nmm. ### Attempt B: screw shuttle, short sector cams, positive return
One Ø6×32.8 transverse can, independently supported quill and bevel, eight-slot indexed wheel on screw, opposed thrust races and guided travelling nut. The nut pulls an inboard narrow yoke R13.47..15.52, not a rotating full-OD barrel. Three interrupted planar captured grooves drive trailing **radial** shoe rollers. A second set of three shaped sideplate grooves drives frame-mounted pivot rockers (7.20 output / 3.60 input mm arms), whose output rollers run in straight tangential slots on the annulus. Axial force: sleeve → output slot → rocker/pivot/fixed fork → frame; during motion the inner roller/cam, yoke and indexed screw do work. Radial jaw load → guided saddle → cam roller/rise plate → yoke. Closed radial and axial dwells transfer service load into steel, not motor torque. Guide binding moved to the **master**, not magically eliminated: eccentricity 19, guide span 2.8 and dry μ=.15 gives 2μe/L=2.04. A single short nut key cannot take a loaded eccentric haul without a longer, rated opposing guide couple.

Physical input p=0..16: radial rises .65..5.55; wall does not leave home until 5.90. Smooth speed transitions over 1 mm; retreat complete at 15.25, both outputs dwell to 16. Reverse undoes that same path without needing a gravity/spring latch. The machined yoke profiles are supplied as centreline and roller normal-offset edges. The end-to-end screw travels are 236/256 45° wheel increments for A/B; indexing is **slow**, no speed/duty acceptance. In B, crank centre spacing 4.553, pin diameter .74, crank radius 1.742; actual eight scallops, radial slots and bilateral blocking sector are specified. At physical detent with pin away, ±3° wheel twist would penetrate the opposed metal scallop boundary, i.e. <.0042 mm screw shift each way at nominal. A 1° driver-grid check of sector clearance on the intended path is diagnostic, not manufacturing or continuous handoff proof. Slot/sector backlash can allow rollback during handoff; no assertion of power-loss hold at every active crank angle. Screw self-lock is **not** credited. Need captive torque-transmission detail, two-sided guide ratings, hard travel limits, independent phase/fault discrimination and actual torque-limited recovery. Full-ring A uses a separate centre distance/blank with its own unfinished scallop tooling.

### 3D failures and limits
* B's closed-to-park input hoop crosses the fixed rear spine at p=12, common R.425 at (−2.675,−4.175,−13.257), and the compound upper bridge at p=16, R.257. The clamp can also overlaps the actual **own fork** at yaw 0 by common R.745. These are material, not projection or filled-gear overlaps.
* B complete two powered-head sweeps at pitch 38, independent actuation and ±15° neighbour yaw: conservative gap lower bound −1.877 after .20+interpolation; not certified and not a proven two-head collision. The absent drive in the control is why its positive result cannot replace this. Real opposite **mated** tail vs selected screw anchor/rockers/sleeve fork stock clears ≥1.886 at the nominal closed sample and ≥3.974 parked. At park the screw anchor still projects x+.870 outside the bore; at closed sideplate tips reach +3.463 on r≈16–18. No arbitrarily yawed recapture, ejection, or floor-release claim.
* Floor z−23.50 throughout the rigid B stroke; A lower cartridge hits it. Bare control still does not certify own fork-to-knuckle net clearance. Electronics are reserved packages; placement is bounded grid search, not a proof no better location exists. Listing their box corners makes the Ø62.767 bound specific to the **reservation layout**, not exact component shapes. Connectors, covers, cables, fasteners, shock/duty, friction and the external separation work are unresolved.

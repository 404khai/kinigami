# Take 73 — A short blind radial plug, not an exposed leaf

**Exploratory.** A radial motion with a short bevel and finite surrounding metal removes the incompatible inner-face/outer-drive-boss velocities of the exposed leaf **at the port**. It does not prove loaded release of an assembled Kin. No change to the declaration, decisions, criteria or gates.

### Port profile and mating stock

Use X pointing out of the head, y outboard in the plane of each plug and t tangential, all lengths in mm. Three stations at −90°, 30°, 150°. Keep the Take 66 convex rounded lobes, diagonal button capsules and the R12.42 disk; do not use the full circular flange. The nominal bore radius is b=max(14.56+.52x,14.56−.27·1.095+.25x) from x=−6.95 to the x=0 lip. The moving piston occupies x=−1.04..−.125, |t|≤2.25. Its parked underside is

    y = max(sqrt(b(x)²−t²), 12.75−.75(x+.75)+1.8391).

All upper points within the plug cross-section up to y=17.9 are plug metal or must be shielded by verified stock. Translate the entire plug radially inward by 1.8391 to lock. The parked cap is not flush along the whole plug: the second term leaves a recess in the aft part and at the sides of the crease. Away from the window the fixed shell extends from the true conical bore outward. At each of the four plug edges leave a .050 slot; in particular the fixed front wall runs from x=−.075 to 0. Fixed material used in this computation has |t|≤6.60, y≤17.9; the rest of the conical bore away from the ports is continuous. A thin wall is not rated just because it is in this geometry. Finish, actual cheek stiffness, bearing lands, corners and tool access still matter.

At lock, the true surface pole is (−.75,12.75,0), n=(−.60,−.80,0) on the tail. The support of the entire rounded hull in the opposite direction is attained there, not on an assumed point flange. For example the plane support of the core is .60(−2.25)+.80(10.75)=7.25; offsetting by R2.50 reaches the true pole. The button capsules at the adjacent clocks project onto this station at most 5.15 in y. The two nominal axial boundaries of the flat along its tangent at t=0 are .3625 and .4125 away. The other lobe stations obey the same check. This is not a stress bound on a finite-width bevel. Even the uninterrupted extrapolation of the rear cone has ≥.3006 clearance to this centered rounded hull. Since radial distance on the translated cap is ≥b(x)−1.8391, and on the translated planar bevel ≥12.75−.75(x+.75), their intersection bounds radius below by 12.5025 (i.e., minimum plug radius **at least** 12.5025), only .0825 above the disk. The front-side terminal aperture now needs ≥1.35164 forward rim position with R2.45 and .05 gap debit; at trial +1.43 only .07836 remains. A real stroke with this port has not been installed.

### A continuous, conditional contact cover

Run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONHASHSEED=0 python3 -B plug.py` here. For unit commanded radial opening speed use virtual tail translation +.46 X, zero spin. At a moving pole the relative velocity v=(.46,−1,0); at a fixed one v=(.46,0,0). If n points from stock into the tail, minimum useful projection is n·v−.23|n×v| per unit normal. The smooth bevel, cap and two conical bore slopes satisfy the limits below; positive combinations of incident roof normals at the beveled ridge do too by the triangle inequality. On the lip the additional forward normal is also favorable. The finite slot needs a separate test.

The script covers both axial ends, lateral sides and all four lower corners of the plug and the surrounding fixed cut (use reflection in t). Directions span the lower unit hemisphere, not just face normals. A normal at any height in the extruded cut is a positive combination of incident bottom and cut-plane normals. For each horizontal coordinate its component plus bottom-slope times n_y must have the cut-plane sign (or vanish for no cut); at the crease admit the range of both slopes. For the fixed stock the side-wall signs are reversed. The slope intervals include the entire parameter cell. For each surviving angular/edge interval either its worst work passes, or a putative tangent R2.45 ball must overlap material. The latter checks actual points in adjacent roof-to-outboard segments, with parameter displacement and R times angular uncertainty subtracted from the overlap. An unsuccessful point lookup never certifies collision. Each rejected cell is bisected; no extrapolation between sampled poses.

For moving contacts start at maximum inward extension and bottom height. Lifting a possible center outward at the same x,t cannot increase its distance to an occupied vertical segment while its y stays below the segment top. For fixed contacts use parked plug metal as a witness: its lower boundary moves only inward on locking. This tests the whole 1.8391 stroke, including the aft parked recess. Pairwise trial budget: .012 total position error between pole and witness, plus .20° normal error debited in **both** overlap and work. This is conditional on each corresponding real feature/normal remaining within that budget; it is not a manufactured tolerance or a license to cut new passages. For the outward-lift argument restrict to actual generator centers y≤17.8. Larger-backed smooth parts must contain the tangent R2.45 ball. Small forks, unbacked edges, exterior approaches, table and neighboring bodies are not covered. The floor can only enter a release budget through actual forces and admissible virtual motion, not by calling it a frictionless support.

The common work lower bounds are +.100 on moving surfaces and +.003 on fixed bore surfaces per unit normal. They are sufficient signs, not a lower bound on motor efficiency. If a complete installation had only these contacts, the disk held, an opposing axial external resultant A and no other contact work, then total required radial output satisfies P≤.46A−.100ΣNplug−.003ΣNwall before internal losses. P is the sum conjugate to a common 1:1 radial displacement; it is **not** the largest individual pad or follower force. Guide friction, off-axis drive reaction, unequal key speeds, follower/cam leakage, preload history and parked/unpowered safety still require independent checks. A unilateral housing contact outside this proof invalidates that substitution, not the local port result.

# Take 67 — offset joint, fixed quill and positive parking bolt

**Disposition: exploratory; reject this installation.** The former rear-hoop crossing is no longer needed. A spring-loaded parking bolt can geometrically back the worm without a Geneva handoff, but a fully equipped straight unit cannot fit Ø60, and its steel mass invalidates the 70 g preload screen. No power-off retention, loaded release, complete tolerance stack, qualified material, fabrication package, Gate result or candidate selection.

## Both signs, measurements and release

The worm pitch radius is 1.50 mm, wheel R19.50/130T, source spur 16:32. A 16-hole keyed parking plate is on the **worm** shaft; the motor spur never disengages. The straight steel Ø.88 shank in a Ø1.00 hole at R3.56 retains 1.39 mm straight overlap after entry relief, both signs. Nominal radial difference gives ±.966° worm rotation = ±.00743° drum, before supports and mesh backlash. At 1.5° misalignment only the lead-in may enter; between holes power loss need not seat the bolt. Tooth finishing uses an expanded helical trapezoid hob removed from the wheel swept volume; test retained steel balls against a blocked worm in both directions, not worm friction. Across all 16 dog registers, at each sign a retained R.007 mm tooth ball enters solid worm by at most .21° of forced wheel rotation with bounded ±1.5° shaft clock and ±.015 mm axial float (centered rigid axes). This is a finite nominal hard backing, not a full-system loaded backlash bound. The tilted-groove and drill patterns are independently editable. The lug has real positive overlap with the rotor bridge. Opposed integral shaft collars abut fixed thrust faces axially, .015 mm play per side; 500/1500 Nmm call for 25.64/76.92 N axial, mean plain-thrust pressure 4.79/14.38 MPa. No frictional self-lock assumption; plating, wear, shaft angular motion, bearing flex and load capacity are unqualified.

**Acceptance sequence, not a claimed controller:** monitor individual installed spring force and both face contacts per pole; stop on actual conservative S window, not motor current. Advance at most the next 22.5° worm hole pitch with the motor still engaged. That is .17308° drum, about .00296 mm take-up / .041 N last ideal step near work. Verify full bolt depth, output drum phase, actual spring/contact states and end travel separately. Motor current/cutoff is an overload channel only. Loss while moving or a jam on a hole land is *not* accepted close. To release, support output torque with the still-meshed motor, unload both bolt flanks, pull to clear the plate by .22 mm, verify pin withdrawal, then reverse into opening and ejection. On a stuck pin stop, do not increase preload or claim release. Solenoid attraction is not proven: a trial .08 mm wire, 888 turns in the assigned coil gives only ~.004 N in a simple 1.99-mm single-airgap pull model versus .031 N initial spring force even **before** flank friction; a more favorable magnetic circuit requires demonstration. No commanded action proves a physical response.

## Access and disposition

1. Install yaw fork/shaft and cantilever/center-quill fixture before closing the two separated bearing rows. Fix motor quills and bearing carriers, then insert worm with opposed thrust collars; the integral collars require a split bearing carrier or a removable captured collar. Split screws/dowels and torque capacity are not shown.
2. Assemble leaves/ribs/stubs and central link crosspins tangentially before fitting the two outboard caps/stops of each station. Real pin end/keeper access and loaded profile adjustment require fixtures. Fit slider, pin and independent ways. The tool must not pass through the cone wall.
3. Try a front axial installation of the preassembled disk and its followers: **fails**. At +9.70 mm from home, a disk roller and the uncut front shoulder share an R.235 mm ball. A removable ring sector/end close-out or radial axle installation before the center quill closes would need a positive keeper, reachable tool and strength reevaluation. Do not cut a permanent exit in a working flank.
4. Insert the parked bolt/guide and keyed plate axially; +Z disassembly corridor checked against the listed unrelated fixed stock. Motor’s separate gears remain meshed. Install tail cartridges before joining the fork, electronics last; no complete production harness or service path demonstrated. For a different disk insert the local plate and behind-disk post/backer datums are in quill-inspect67.txt: those regions are **occupied**, not free reserve. A separate probe (quill-space67.txt) clears a moving R6.92 × .73 mm central cylinder at home x−9.30..−8.57; the center quill and three fixed key webs block a deeper solid cup.

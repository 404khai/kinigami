# Take 33 — a measured starting state matters

**Exploratory.** A single reading of the upper clamping force does not qualify the six lower contacts. These are separate quasi-static coupons, not new loads or springs for cand-d. Each uses the R7, 35° lower-seat pattern, an **integral** R8 upper crown and the previous 70 g wrench boxes. The earlier free ball/bed has additional contacts and does not have these compliance matrices.

| Trial | Result | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| [Incremental indentation](compatible.py), upper force initially 45–55 N, lower faces may unload | For all three tested rotation bounds (.010, .020, .035 rad), the outer LP still admits the imposed 250 N upper ceiling. | This relaxation has not bounded service preload below the search cap. It does **not** predict a 250 N contact, or prove that a real pad is unbounded. |
| [Individual compliant contacts](floating.py), nominal geometry, bounded starting shear and known positive normals at every station | Increasing lower/upper tangential stiffness from 20/40 to 350/350 N/mm changes the enclosure gain from 0.123 to 1.928. The latter feedback inequality yields no bound. | Normal and tangential stiffness must be measured separately. Treating a fixed pad as frictionless would hide this sensitivity. |
| [Four-post pad](columns.py), all four posts attached to rigid plates, including lateral-load eccentricity | Lower/upper peak compression per post 20.04/48.53 N; ideal Euler screens 21.72/51.10 N. Without rotational restraint the respective single-post screens are only 5.53/13.01 N. | Not a part selection. No measured end restraint, imperfection, eccentric second-order bending, failure load, cross-axis hysteresis or life margin. Individual negative post forces would require actual tensile joints. |
| [Perturbed support](interval_pads.py), rigid-top case with .007 triad error, .060 mm pole error and .00022 mm differential support motion | The iteration encloses algebraic lower/upper changes as large as 21.34/19.23 N. | A lower land initially carrying 19 N can no longer be proved loaded; do not continue the elastic solve through that loss of contact. |

The fourth test also prints a **different** upper support whose series tangent rate is assumed to lie in 70–210 N/mm. At .0005/.003 mm lower/upper differential support motion the maximum lower/upper changes are 17.64/1.15 N. Consider trial reference readings 18–21 N on each lower land: even before sensor error this would enclose 0.36–38.64 N, **outside** the 1–38 N used to bound the lower contact stiffness. It is not a self-consistent positive-load certificate. The two stiffness intervals are targets, not a rate inferred from a catalogue load at one deflection. An axial spring here would sit **in series behind a powered clamp**, not operate as a spring-closed latch.

**Model boundary.** Equilibrium acts on all contacts simultaneously. At fixed positive secant stiffnesses the inverse normal stiffness gives displacement and force sensitivities; rank-one dependence permits endpoint bounds for these coefficients. For the nominal isotropic tangential spring-slider, the slip centre is in the convex hull of its starting state and the displacement history. The resulting shear increment bound includes initial shear; it is not an equal-load assumption. The perturbed trial adds pose, pole, basis, cross-compliance and support movement allowances. Its nonlinear iteration only reports a box if a slightly enlarged box maps strictly into itself; that is a check on the **specified inequalities**, not validation of the inequalities as a hardware model. In particular the 1.5 path-variation factor, friction law, load/geometry bands, measured starting state and support errors are unverified. Rotating a contact frame over many slip cycles need not satisfy that path assumption.

To proceed, measure all seven reference forces or bound them without equal sharing; repeat combined normal/side loading including compression buckling, support tilt and a missing land. A force window which leaves a stiffness domain must be recalculated with loss of contact allowed. None of this rates a loose-tail opening stroke, a guide or motor, or a complete assembly.

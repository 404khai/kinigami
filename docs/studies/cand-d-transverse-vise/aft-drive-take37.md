# Take 37 — aft-pivot jaw and loose-tail release

**Result: exploratory. Reject this installation at both pitches.** Rotating the full upper jaw about (−2,−4) gets it behind the parked pusher; neither binding the loose tail to that rotation nor inferring release from the motor angle is valid. Three independent material witnesses bound the layout: the crown enters the parked blade, the positive arm enters the stationary full-width landing, and a parked jaw enters the next motor. Head translation improves only some neighbour pairs.

## What actually bounds the opening

**Parked blade.** The physical cropped R7 butt has material at (4.60,.48,0), .05 in from the axial tip. Rotating this point as a test about (−2,−4) puts it at (3.72179,1.55802,0) at 10°: **.37179 inside** the parked blade x[3.35,4.15], y[−1.50,4.70]. This same point is strictly inside at sampled 5.4° through 13.7°. The aligned home butt has only .40 nominal axial gap; neither it nor the angle range is pose-independent. Exact rigid co-rotation runs into the blade; the loose body might move differently. This is a counterexample to a proposed trajectory, **not** proof every possible trajectory jams.

**Can the blade park farther back?** At 10° the witnessed material point alone needs ≥.62821 additional rear stroke for .20 forward-side clearance. To separate the whole conservative crown box by x at that angle, its xmin=3.35793 requires a blade front ≤3.15793, or ≥.99207 extra. A 1.00 rear park gives x3.15, **but is not installed**: the master-carriage end goes to −10.70 versus guide rear −9.85; concentric nut rear x1.20 runs .70 into the bevel's axial allocation ending at 1.90. Housing/stop changes and restored engagement would be necessary. At 90° that same sufficient x-plane test costs 12.642 extra for the cap box alone; this is *not* a necessary stroke for real release. No commanded backdrive through the rear stop.

**Work, deck and floor.** Virtual rotation is a global test field, not an imposed motion or an upper bound on contact force. For contact normal n and projected radius r=(x−xp,y−yp), d=−r_y n_x+r_x n_y and minimum torque per normal newton is g=d−µ√(|r|²−d²). At µ=1: a +y top at (15,−1.75) gives +14.75, but the finite ±z side at (15,−5.65,±10.75) gives −17.08. Floor at (15,−23) gives −2.00; neither side is excluded for an arbitrary loose Kin. Aligned virtual tail/fork outer stocks clear y−23 at the printed angles; this says nothing about an arbitrary rotated housing or grounded equilibrium. Four trial 3×2.6 foot pads at x−12.6/+9, z±8.2, y−23..−21 fit the straight rest but give ymin−26.882/−24.449 at −15°/+15° on an unchanged table. A yaw sweep in space is not a grounded gait.

On a parked +x flat, g=yp−y−µ|x−xp|: −10.63 at (4.15,.48); −11.280 at the 10° witness. At the actual blade face x4.15, y1.558, it is **−11.708**. Even if a net 50 N·mm surplus *after every other load* were verified, that latter nominal contact alone would need N<4.27 N; there is no verified surplus, reaction cap, or tolerance/friction bound here. 50 is an illustration, not a design rating. A hard stop can sustain reaction independent of drive current. Protect the entire face, wing/edge contacts and pose uncertainty, or measure separation at the actual instant; do not convert the illustration into a release condition.

## Reproduce and interpret

From this directory run `python3 -B aft-drive-take37.py` and `python3 -B aft-gear-search-take37.py`. They import the local `oblique-drive.py` / `install.py` geometry. No other library, seed, solver or network call. Named convex stocks use explicit 3D support projections. For endpoint neighbours, 1° yaw steps include 2R sin(.25°) finite-drift debit plus .20 stock. Prefix-vs-cheek checks bound each 5° arc by its endpoint hull plus the radial sagitta, not point samples. Positive separator proves only that stock pair is apart; output gap values may be capped lower bounds. Hard collisions above instead identify material points or intersecting circular sections. No interpolation claim is made for the coarse local scout, and the whole independently opening three-unit motion is not certified. The package is a bounded rejection, not a new Gate 1 or originality result.

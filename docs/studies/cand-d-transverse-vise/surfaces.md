# Take 27 — a transverse paddle on three open V seats

**Exploratory.** A gaugeable six-face cage exists without assuming six simultaneous forces. A real rear shoulder gives the independent ejector something to push; a falling outer deck puts the cheek's terminal edges below the monitored paddle. The 21.5 mm high-open stroke excludes upper/lower bridging by the paddle at *every* orientation, not arbitrary bridging by the rest of a Kin, the floor or its neighbours. **Test one transverse fixture; do not freeze a candidate.**

Reproduce the geometry, continuation inequalities and counterexamples with `python3 -B bench.py`. [Calculator](bench.py). Dimensions mm, forces N, moments N·mm. Tolerances are requirements on the assembled, **loaded** part including registration, inclination, bending, runout and thermal shifts, not separate independent allowances to add later. μ ≤ .23 is provisional. The force matrix, preload, passive hold, screw/yoke, hinge, electronics and actual feet are separate work. Q85, Q100, Q12 and official run 12 are unchanged.

## Acquisition and first-to-last biased closure

With the platen high and the blade parked, a −x insertion at transverse offset y=2.25±.10 has ≥.339 mm lower clearance for tilt ≤.50°, before final lateral indexing. No jaw forces steer this contact-free approach. Move to the registered xz neighbourhood, then bring down the force-limited platen. This is a finite 3D corridor and a testable powered closing path, **not a proof that passive tabletop locomotion delivers the registration**. If y points vertically up in the proposed rest orientation, supply that approach height mechanically (feet/elevator or verified ramp); the flat pusher does not levitate a paddle.

Let Δ be actual upper **biased excess gap**, including relative skin/ball/bed error; let e=.020 bound the signed finite-plane discrepancy at each boss centre over the neighbourhood, including elasticity. Let τ be tilt of the tail plane, ψ its yaw after removing the least tilt, d its centre translation. Start in the known home-clock component, or verify the entry box directly: τ,|ψ|≤2°, |dy|≤.10, |d_xz|≤.53 and planar displacement of each boss ≤.55. These are not software corrections. The finite planes cover every possible orthogonal projection in this box; the collar and neighbouring grooves do not remove them. The ball centre projects onto the *actual* upper R2.50 flat.

With C=cos35°, S₃₅=sin35°, let yi be local y displacement at boss i and ti its tangential displacement. Nonpenetration gives C yi−S₃₅|ti| ≥−e even for an unloaded boss. The centre is their mean and at least one yi≤dy−R sinτ/2. Upper nonpenetration gives dy≤D=Δ+.50(1−cos2°)+.38 sin2°: the offset and wander of the top contact matter. Consequently τ≤asin(2(D+e/C)/R). With A=(e+C D)/S₃₅, Σ|ti|≤3A. The planar block of any such tilt differs from pure yaw by ≤1−cos2° in operator norm; ξ=R(1−cos2°). The three tangents sum to zero and Σ t tᵀ=(3/2)I. Summation and projection then give R|sinψ|≤A+ξ, |d_xz|≤2(A+ξ), and boss excursion ≤2(A+ξ)+2R sin(|ψ|/2)+ξ. Strict return inside *every* trial bound proves continuity on that branch. It does not certify a remote pose with the same gap.

## Optional fold: side, tip, pivot and terminal fairing

For a z pivot, let u run along the tongue and v through its thickness; tip velocity per positive radian is (−v,u,0). On the lower face v=−1.10 opening torque is ≥(u−μ·1.10)N·mm per N. On a **square side** a reaction along z has no opening moment and friction can supply −μ hypot(u,v). The square forward tip receives −u-directed force: worst moment v−μu. At u=18 these are **−4.15 / −5.24**. A low opening torque trace at home is not a side/tip certificate.

A shape that fixes those two forward features within the package: for u≥3.50, v=−1.10..+5.90, side halfwidth 2.65+.58(v+1.10). Front station u(−1.10)=17.10, du/dv=max(0,.30−v/18). Max global x18.824, halfwidth 6.71. Virtual moment n·(−v,u,0)−μ sqrt(u²+v²−[n·(−v,u,0)]²) is ≥.230 on either side and ≥.731 on the tip (N·mm/N, nominal). Conic blends between favourable incident normals preserve positivity; gauge as-built blended projections. These are geometry proposals only; do not substitute them for bearing/guide loads. The **top/back and proximal edge do not pass**: on the top, a −v-directed normal gives −u even at zero friction. No rigid closed blade admits positive opening work on every face (the rotational velocity field has zero flux through its closed surface). A fixed rear wall at x3.10 cannot shield a forward top at x18 during the early swing.

Two distinct approaches:

1. **Bridge the fold with a verified crown contact.** If and only if the blade touches the working 12° spherical part, tail x is within 12.5° of true x incl .5° blade angle; roll about it may be completely free. Height of any eligible point above that R7 centre is ≤12.205 by the per-solid bound |x−Sx|sin12.5°+hypot(y−Sy,z)+bounding radius. Centre is within 7 sin.5° of blade contact height. With a blade ending at y4.70, this leaves **.334 mm** to a folding assembly staying above y17.50, or **3.284 mm** to y25 with the +9.25 tall blade. This covers the eligible paddle/arms only, not a high part of the housing. A preloaded follower may take up the ~.40 rear slack; verify its actual contact type/position/load through fold, do not infer it from screw force or a spring command. Once fully folded at x≤1.65 with rear carriage x≤2.90, an actual crown contact at x≥4.10 also puts the compact parts in front. For housing at x≥12 and cross radius <101 mm the same projection ordering holds; debiting a further 60 sin.5° and .06 for blade lean/stock still leaves >.61 to the carriage for a **verified** ≤60 mm point-to-contact distance. Losing that witness with an unrestricted loose tail is not a passive rear guard.
2. **A sealed terminal mast pocket.** At park use the now-forward underside of the tongue as a flush land at x1.65. Grade the surrounding cheek to x3.10 over ≥2.071 mm at ≤35°. Mask sides, proximal root and back with real material; no unfilled sweep opening behind the face. For a tail admitting a .45 interior ball at every eligible point, a *closed*, coplanar rim/land seam of actual empty inradius ≤.10 admits sag ≤.0113 and angular debit ≤12.84°; outward reaction ≥.5007 N/N. Require uninterrupted coplanar bands on both sides, closed ends and protected back; no tolerance step, fastener or debris counted as a seamless roof. The thin free-ball retainer, housing and other Kin have **not** been certified to that probe size. The fairing could protect the **terminal** state without maintaining crown load; it does **not** protect the exposed top through the swing. An all-sweep shroud would need to cover that forward top/axle as well, without making a new fixed ceiling that can pinch against the floor. The tall sweep to y≈45, stow and fabrication are unverified. Do not combine branch 1's watched contact with an arbitrary-pose claim for branch 2.

## Pressure and physical discriminator

At the .055 gap and **200 N on one flank**, the full .15741-radius contact patch plus .020 registration still has .04659 radial reserve. The rounded falling deck stays .27411 outside its R.45 backing-ball test. The calculator encloses the joint radial/tangential offsets of the boss and patch with closed angular sectors; spending the separate displacement maxima simultaneously would falsely reject this finite land.

At E*=115.38 GPa, the R3/flat Hertz pair at an actual 50/100/200 N screens at 2.43/3.06/3.85 GPa (patch .099/.125/.157 mm). The R7 pusher at the same forces screens at 1.38/1.74/2.19 GPa. Scale with the cube root of actual force, not one sixth of the preload. The .055 cage leaves .204 radial land after the stated allowances; also gauge both radial and transverse patch limits, form and indentation. The .45 rolling construction avoids an unspecified sharp crest, **not** a life problem: the pessimistic two-convex R.45/R.45 comparison at just 5 N is 6.34 GPa. The high-load known planes and a worst incidental moving blend are not interchangeable. No sheet yield or endurance claim, no as-built curvature proof, no measured spring bias or brake/reverse test.

Fixture sequence: instrument gap, load, orientation and body travel independently. Insert at the raised y datum; clamp and mark any five-face stall. At a gauged Δ≤.055 apply the full external wrench with actual hold/preload. Retain positive restraint during relief and measure the partial-pose excursion. Confirm the top physically high or properly shielded, then eject under adverse floor load with a delayed/failed contact; inspect the last loaded land, rolling ball, shoulder and lip for indentation. Repeat with the blade missing the shoulder and a loose tail outside the monitored tube. Refuse service on an ambiguous pose, preload, contact or fold. A fault report is not proof of cleared neighbours or restartability.

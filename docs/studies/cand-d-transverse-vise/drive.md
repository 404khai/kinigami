The adjacent-head test projects each box/cylinder onto candidate separating axes, using its actual support, not the radius of a spherical box. Success means at least one separating plane; failure to find one is not proof of penetration. The .5° grid debits the full point displacement at half-step, 2r sin(.25°/2), plus .20 stock; broad rejection has its own 15° finite-rotation bound. Mating faces are intentionally not treated as two disjoint heads. Tail housing caught on a lip after loss of seating, the joint-sector web, wiring loops and a rolling chain require separate checks.

## Force, speed and faults

Parent/Take 27 load case: 70 g, upper shear ≤.05P, P85±.8 gives a positive frictionless support reserve **after** actual seating. Static existence is not detection and is not proof six faces bear load with friction. No one-sixth division. Without a demonstrated shear/contact condition, the arbitrary .23P screen can exceed 250 N. The ordinary rigid pad remains an option, not a low-shear guarantee.

* Clamp: 1,249.5 N·mm including shear offset; two guide planes 5.6 apart, sum side reactions screened at 450.5 N before guide preload. With guide µ=.23, thread µ=.18, 60° thread and 3 N parasitic drag, permanent screw thrust 191.62 N, torque 58.51 N·mm. Add 1 N·mm ancillary, 4.75 reduction at **.85 total** two-mesh efficiency: 14.74 N·mm at gearhead, requiring ≥.206 internal gearbox efficiency at the RE6 motor's nominal continuous torque. Output shaft and gear wheel need independent radial journals; 59.5/11.4≈5.22 N tangential at the big gear, up to 1.90 N radial, before fault/impact.
* Ejector: target **15 N delivered at the blade**, not at the screw. Parent's 3-D contact analysis needs >8.79 N for actual table/feet µ≤.50; >11.77 at .60, >20.6 at .70. No floor-normal cap equal to weight. At µ=.23 the eccentric master/roll arrangement charges tail lever, screw offset and one-sided roll reactions: feedback .282, 68.63 N screw thrust, 18.54 N·mm on screw; .82 bevel, .85 two-spur and 1 N·mm quill loss yield **13.89 N·mm GP output**. This is conditional on the anti-roll freedom and drag assumptions. The earlier 10.5-span, y−6.85 opposing-guide package reaches ~115 N·mm and is rejected. Tight rails cannot be cured by declaring generous motor torque.
* Both actuators compared with [Maxon RE6 A 349190](https://www.maxongroup.com/medias/sys_master/root/9396141293598/Cataloge-Page-EN-129.pdf), 3 V, .324 N·mm nominal, .242 A, 5,670 rpm; [GP6A 472229](https://www.maxongroup.com/medias/sys_master/root/9406823596062/Cataloge-Page-EN-412.pdf), 531441/2401:1, **30 N·mm continuous**, 28.7 mm combined length, 5 N dynamic axial/8 N radial at 5 mm; .60 maximum efficiency is not a guaranteed operating point. Faster 57:1 GP6A is rated **only 10 N·mm continuous**. Under the 221:1 nominal-speed comparison one-way clamp/eject take ~11.4/2.93 minutes, not seconds. Two actuators alone ~5.2 g each; reversing each once at the comparison current costs ~115 mAh motor-side charge at 3 V before logic/losses. No verified mass, duty cycle, thermal or cell-endurance margin.
* [NSK/ISC catalogue](https://www.nskmicro.co.jp/en_ch_bearing_size_pdf/ISC_P45.pdf) F4‑9M comparator: axial static 771 N. [Fitrun F3-6M table](https://www.fitrunbearing.com/products/chrome-steel-ball-bearing/thrust-ball-bearings/): 3×6×2.8, axial dynamic/static 556/436 N. An F2-6M with 83 N static is not equivalent. No radial rating from thrust data and no added capacities from opposed races. Independent quills, sector-height arm bearings, end journals and screw roots still need toleranced detail/combined-load tests. M2 root Ø1.51, 21 mm worst K=2 column ~286 N gross; buckling does not certify its first thread. A 2.25 long nut nominally has 5.6 pitches; one first flank alone at the above thrust is ~63 MPa nominal strip shear before runout/notches.
* Friction-dependent torque limiting alone **does not regulate P**. Example: 67 N·mm at .35 lead could put 1.20 kN into a frictionless jam; 43 N·mm at .4 lead could put 675 N into the ejector. Both exceed relevant race static comparators. A stalled screw hard against a solid stop is not safe. Use an all-metal series load element/load pickup in the bridge, measured at the downstream face, positive opening shoulder, independently set mechanical torque limiter and a load-triggered disconnect before solid height. A specified torque/force fault disconnect, stroke tolerance, fatigue and independent shaft holding brake able to survive a jam have **not** been packaged or demonstrated. Do not credit retention or release after power loss.

## Acquisition, release and alternate rest

* Six-mm lift to y9.50 gives too little gap for an arbitrarily tilted paddle. A thin blade/visor is not full shielding. A full-height home plug x−4.50..4.15 at y−1.50..9.25 would contain the old (0,0) hinge. Reject it. An independently observed bounded attitude (Take 27 ≤6° tilt/≤3° yaw, crown on actual blade land) is a different branch.
* High lift gives 26.70 mm minimum nominal plane gap. Independent pairwise upper bound on paddle plus twin arms to local +12 is 26.001 mm; Take 27's actual primitives tighten this to 25.465. Neither includes an unrestricted body/adjacent Kin. A fixed load pad may be substituted for the cassette only with new contact and elastic/fit checks. R7 butt working centre local y=.48 (the earlier .85 trial was withdrawn). On the monitored branch butt contact y−.534..4.341, z within ±.75; keep central flat z±2.50. Actual end position and observed tail motion/crown projection, all face relief, posture, table reaction and housing exit matter. A motor count, current or one switch cannot prove clearance.
* Optional +Y-up rest: comparing a 21.5 mm diagonal rail to a folding tongue, the diagonal moves a **fixed** rail end toward x−20.95/y29 (origin-centred diameter >71) and sacrifices the clean mast. Instead one moving bridge can have a local z-pivot x.55, y=face+1.10; use positive conjugate cam for +90° rotation over last 7.5 mm of lift. Use a 4 mm follower crank phased −135→−45°, captured track, end dwell/unloading transition and a separate positive lock stop. The crank's normal moment arm is ≥1.539 mm for the ideal constant-rise segment; giving it full clamp moment would require ~812 N, unacceptable without a separate support/drive proof. Fork at pivot straddles the screw; all central full-width tip stock must be forward enough to clear its head, not a solid arm through the mast. At full fold tongue x≤1.65; root enclosure x≤2.9. Actual moving y may reach +45.5; fixed track fits local box x[−4,+4.6]/y[14,25]/z[7.1,9.3] **before** a rated roller, mounting and transition design. Still open: lock-stop release/re-engagement, pressure angle at blended ends, flush terminal pocket, side/tip contact and sweeping/grounded housing. The 90° variant did not participate in the neighbouring straight-bridge projection test.
* Take 27's corresponding shaped tongue/visor study uses cropped blade y≤4.70 and specific masked contact lands. Under its independently verified spherical butt contact the eligible tail/paddle leaves .334 mm to a folding assembly held above y17.50; do not extend this to all housing, feet or shell. A broad rear face/ejector and a raised or folded bridge do not guarantee an unsupported Kin remains upright.

## Assembly and next discriminator

Assemble metal cheek+masts on a fixture before adding electronics. Fit screw, opposed races/shoulder, independently supported gear train and removable cover on −y; insert nut from open y end, slide the matched master gib and floating follower from x, fit tongue and positive keepers, then short supported bevel and blade from the side. Fit pins/screws before installing obstructions; outside access to covers, manual screw ends and an unloaded removal route are required. If the order cannot be reproduced with standard driver/retainer clearances and retained parts on a bench, reject it. No hidden tail actuator.

Sequence: present while open; verify six-site engagement, torque-limited axial take-up **and** direct load, then hold mechanically. To release, lower load and positively retract platen, observe real gap/fold state, then advance the broad blade under bounded contact and force; hold out until all moving and fixed capture features are actually clear. At ambiguous state, obstructed travel or power loss: inhibit bridge reclosing, externally support the chain, manually unload at the separate drive, inspect and rehome. This is a service concept, not autonomous fault recovery.

**Next test:** build the rail/nut/cheek load fixture, not a candidate. Measure independent actual clamp/pusher forces, free play/rail reaction, loaded tongue tilt, starting torque in both senses, flex-element/disconnect and holding during supply loss. Sweep replicated *physical* covers, screw ends, side links, sector, populated drivers, cables, hinge, feet and shell with a second/third body. Time/charge, partial seating, tilted/reversed floor and a blocked neighbour are part of the test. No loaded-clamp, full-stow, independent-release, G1, G2 or acceptance claim.

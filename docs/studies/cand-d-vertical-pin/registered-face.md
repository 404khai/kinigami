### Mass feeds back into the force claim

For the **solid** tapered face and rounded tail, take a trial homogeneous steel density 7.81 mg/mm³. The calculator encloses volume by recursively splitting cubes at uncertain boundaries. Distance to each swept-ball centreline minus radius is 1-Lipschitz; so is the maximum signed unit-facet distance of a convex polytope, and the minimum of the fields for their union. A cube centre more than half a diagonal inside/outside classifies the whole cube; the final uncertain cubes count only in the upper bound. The exact torus is used here, not its enlarged collision chords. The resulting tail is 41.33–44.80 g, the U-face 7.24–10.46 g (display rounded). These two parts alone consume about 49–55 g. No forks, shaft, drive, fasteners, electronics, battery, shell or feet are included. Hollowing or changing materials would require new contact, section and fatigue checks.

In particular the earlier 70 g load budget is **not a unit mass measurement**.

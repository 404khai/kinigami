# Take 50 — root-driven horn and shortened X guide

**Exploratory. Reject this installation, not the root-link topology.** The direct link removes the driven idle stub and has no crank dead centre. It does not make the module fit: the tested stock misses Ø60 without electronics, the fixed horn motor physically intersects the separator, and a held neighbour blocks the moving carriage. No change to Q85/Q100, criteria, gate, candidate, or official result. No fabrication release.

## X drive, sensor, and alternative socket

Ø3 screw on low fixed T rails at y±5.60. Four two-sided shoes, fore/aft span 6.90. Fixed rail ends stay 10.13 apart at aligned pitch; **not a recovery guarantee**. With adjacent head held still, +X moving keel reaches the adjacent motor envelope by +9.121 stroke; at +16.8 the common point (17.37985,4.72,−18.32) is inside both. Co-advance is not assured by a command. Other ±15° endpoint screens include negative compressed bounds, not additional material collisions or proof of universal jams.

V3's 0.70 mm tapered tower has actual section modulus 0.717 mm³ at the top of the bottom bar. Applying the entire 170.43/208.30 N at an upper land with a 20.195 mm rise gives nominal 4.80/5.87 GPa in one tower. Sensitivity, not a solved six-contact distribution. Baseline 14.4×2.36 backing web at 17.3 mm: 221/270 MPa. A thicker centre alone does not support the side towers. V3 rolling tail + face alone reportedly 48.57..55.24 g; 111 N is not a full-unit rating.

**Paired-web alternative** (separate model group, *replaces* central gauged neck/backing): upper land backers → side sockets at |y|14.8..16.65 → two toes x3.55/9.25 → cross-sills → rail keels. Force pickup moves to the upstream nut (sensor implementation/calibration open). Ø2 bolts with finite heads/bosses. Nominal separator gap ≥.310 including bosses (earlier ≥.740 omitted the toe bosses), mated straight heavy neck ≥.281 at home, fixed rail ≥.220 and own joint ≥1.52 over travel. No six-DOF misalignment, running tolerance, thread pullout or neighbouring-head certification. High-land one-sided force gives 5.13/6.27 N·m at the base, 0.90/1.10 kN per 5.70 mm toe couple and 435/532 MPa gross tensile stress on 2.07 mm² before prying. The isolated 10.80×.96 cross-sill at |y|=8 would see about **1.64/2.00 GPa torsional shear** if carrying the whole moment: the webs did not make the guide support strong. Needs closed transverse box or guides under side sockets plus a new neighbour/stow check.

At centred face load, guide couple reaction 501/613 N at the 6.90 span. Conditional required screw thrust for 170.43/208.30 N face is 301/367 N (μ=.10), 660/806 N (μ=.23); at upper-land height these become 380/464 and 957/1169 N. Corresponding high-land gearhead demands 118/144 and 297/363 N·mm (screw .28, mesh .72). Excludes lateral force, impact and misalignment. Upstream reading includes guide drag; it cannot prove delivered face draw. No gearhead, socket, limiter, hold or load-cell rating.

## Reuse

From this directory set `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONHASHSEED=0`. Run `python3 -B horn-package-take50.py search` for the board search. Then run `for n in link integrate package audit tiles socket model; do python3 -B "horn-$n-take50.py" || exit 1; done`. Each stdout has a checked-in reference; the last two scripts also generate the editable socket and grouped model JSON beside them. The [section drawing](horn-section-take50.png) is a navigation aid only. Groups use named convex supports, not a manufactured B-rep. Keep the no-singularity/short-bearing results separate from the installation rejection. No fabricated hardware, acceptance solver, qualification or candidate.

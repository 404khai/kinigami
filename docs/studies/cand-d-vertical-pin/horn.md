# Pivot a tapered horn through the passive eye

**Exploratory, not a declared successor.** The pivot removes the five translating guides, but introduces a large reaction torque at the axle. [Take 48](horn-take48.md) rejects the first installation. Run 12 and Q100 still govern.

## The curve and the guard

At lock put the working pole at the aft inside of the Take 46 eye (R7.50 spine, R4.20 tube). In XZ polar coordinates about the transverse pivot, each material slice at angle t is a disk in the radial/Y plane. For L=18 or 20, R=L+.90, b(t)=(R+.90)exp(gt)−R and g=.32L/(R+.90). Start at b=.22; end at t=.54/.50 respectively. Positive rotation withdraws the narrow end. Every exposed **smooth** slice has tangential normal component corresponding to slope b′/r≥g. With μ=.23 and 1° normal error, even the adverse contact friction does positive opening work. The narrow cap can push in the opening sense; the wide cap and all supports must be hidden. This argument gives neither drive controllability nor an upper contact-force bound.

[Aligned horn clearance](horn_home.py) checks the exact tangent at the intended home pole. Moving disks at a given world angle are no wider than the home disk there (up to the maximum root width). On the lower arc the squared pole distance grows inward from the intended rim and is convex along that rim; above the neighbourhood all stock is above the aligned tube. No positive home *gap* to the working pole is claimed. The non-ring R4.2 connectors have ≥2.15 mm clearance over the arc. This is a nominal aligned path, not free-eye dynamics.

For L18 the .40-thick lower skin needs a cut over x=8.935..14.119, half-width ≥2.905, before allowance. A 3.065 half-slot with 2.00 front lintel gives a local positive lip-work bound of about .085 N/N and a parked depth reserve near .449 mm (rounded slot; nominal form). [Short forward sleeve](horn_hood.py): truncated at x=−1.15/15.40, with .20 skins, the horn clears front/rear/side/top allocations by 1.051/.289/.704/.328 mm. Take 46 seats clear the **outside** by ≥.181; their larger installed bridges do not. The open rear aperture measures 17.21 × 23.98 mm. Neither the Ø56.884 enclosing diameter of this sleeve alone nor a blind cap there packages the machine.

## Pressure, load and lost motion

[Contact calculation](horn_load.py) encloses candidate smooth pressure poles by subdividing the angle and circumference, charging interpolation, form and datum errors, and excluding the finite end disks. At L18, for the stated **loaded** datum targets, the pole normal is within 5.613° and location within .503 mm of nominal. This does not establish that actual bearings, body and six loaded seats hold those targets. The nominal curvature radii for Hertz contact are 5.688/1.272 mm. At 200 N the illustrative elastic peak is 4.396 GPa; this is not an allowable contact pressure, a life result, or a bound for truncated/offset contact.

At 37.5 mm joint pitch, the Take 44 yaw screen rises to 115.5/161.6 N·mm for service/fault at 70 g. Existence of nonnegative six-seat reactions in the registered geometry (not equal sharing) gives sufficient draws along the tilted datum of 108.6/153.0/138.3 N for service/table/fault. The corresponding adverse X-stage target is 164.8 N. A separate provisional 185 N X-force cap, **if physically imposed**, bounds individual land normals by 277.62 N in the [outer-cone LP](horn_lock.py), using the loaded pole and position uncertainties. At 90 g and a 235 N cap the analogous upper bound is 353.02 N. These are static pressure/strength inputs, not measured limits or ejection forces.

Two nominal equilibria at Fx=163 N, each with positive reactions on all six flat seats and an allowed opposite horn tangential traction, give 246 and 1742 N·mm holding torque. They are witnesses, not extrema. In the latter case an ideal Ø3 steel stub of length 7.30 twists 1.16° (0.364 mm at the pole); Ø5 cuts that to .150° (0.047 mm), before groove backlash, spoke bending, bearing or chassis deflection. Do not transfer the conditional contact-pole bounds to an assembly that moves farther than its error budget. The apparent net opening moment after *assumed* .005 journal drag excludes a loaded cam follower, bore misalignment, preload and motor reaction.

Reproduce from this directory: run `horn_home.py`, `horn_load.py`, `horn_lock.py`, and `horn_hood.py` with Python 3, NumPy and SciPy. The earlier sweep in `horn.py` is a section search, not the aligned certificate. No criteria, candidate, independent review or official evaluation changed.

# Take 10 — Can the jaw drive fit? Q98 decision input, not a candidate

**Recommendation: reserve the coaxial M2.5×0.35 layout. Yes to the dimensional envelope and permanent thread engagement; no to freezing a functioning or overload-protected coupler.** The open items that can still reverse the choice are the one-jaw bridge and follower, overload trip in both directions (including a stuck nut), face separation *after* jaw clearance, drag at breakaway and mass at the revised pitch. Do not declare bounded release. No new candidate, generator, gate run or qualification. Q97's run 11 result for v6 stands.

Units are mm, N, N·mm, g. x is the head axis, global (y,z)=(0.60,−4.60); r is distance from that axis, not z-height. Dimensions below are proposed finished/gauged values, not tolerances a supplier has promised. Read the accompanying [drive_stack.py](drive_stack.py) with Python 3; the printed inequalities are hand geometry, not collision detection, CAD or acceptance tests. Input reference: frozen declaration at dd7c0ad; compare with the retired v6 hardware, do not overlay both cartridges.

Nut motion is relative to the fixed housing. The small steady-state bearing outside its tube is what makes a fine *central* screw possible. Neither the rejected M9 screw nor the provisional 2.8 travel is used. Catalog: [NSK Micro p45](https://www.nskmicro.co.jp/en_ch_bearing_size_pdf/ISC_P45.pdf); [Fitrun F4-8M including tolerances](https://www.fitrunbearing.com/product/f4-8m/). Do not mix load ratings of same-size parts from different vendors.

**Jaw geometry:** proposed head Ø2.18±0.02, neck Ø1.34..1.36, closed tip r0.735..0.755. Worst closed clearance to neck 0.055; minimum captured radial width 1.08−0.755=0.325. Contact stays inside 1.10. Allocate 0.420±0.005 radial on 11.8..12.2° loaded track, 0.130±0.005 on 35..37° clearance track, 1.04..1.06 of zero-radial-motion dwell. At the 0.415 minimum rise the jaw reaches r1.150, only 0.050 beyond the largest head: do not use the steep segment loaded unless the *entire* offset/tilt/deflection error is <0.050 there. Otherwise extend shallow lead and redo the torque/travel budget. Full open radial clearance before these errors ≥0.175. Maximum segment lengths plus 0.08 for blends =3.367 <3.530; also exceeds Take 9's 3.39+0.10 requirement. This stroke contains **no extra powered ejection allowance**. A zero-slope cylindrical dwell backs the jaws radially while latched; captured T-slot/crosspin on the other flank positively pulls them open. Neither a spring nor servo torque is the unpowered radial lock.

**Radial section allocation:** at the front bearing use r≤4.00 stationary race, moving tracks at r4.35..5.85, housing OD r6.30 with discrete internal guide lands. Example Ø0.70 tangential follower centre r4.90..5.45, half-diameter 0.35, leaves ≥0.10 to each radial stock limit if roots locally extended to 4.35..5.90. Open-ended paired rails with ≥0.20 hook/flank retention and a removable metal end keeper; carriage deliberately assembled from that end. Gauge tangential width, root material, rounded track blends and closed dwell under the lateral load. One pin carrying 118 N in double shear at Ø0.70 is 153 MPa average, but with only 1.4 between supports a centre load gives >1.2 GPa nominal bend. A wide backed shoe/integral T-head is required for that load; two jaws cannot be assumed to share. Similarly a single 3.2-wide, 0.70-thick bridge cantilever with 3.2 offset and 118 N gives ~1.45 GPa at root. A gusset reaching 1.25 gives ~453 MPa before notches, but adding this to a continuous disc collides with the front seat or face. Interdigitated fixed/moving ribs or a conical annulus needs a drawing and stress check. **Simple plate and pin baseline fails; no credit for a gusset not yet swept.**

**Radial journals:** thrust bearings do not locate or react side loads. Use an integral hollow rotor skirt *outside* rear bearing: ID≥10.15, OD~10.90. Bronze/steel paired short fixed journal bands at x23.95 and 27.25 (≥3.2 effective span), with clearance elsewhere and an annular web tying skirt to screw. Allocate ~0.05 diametral running clearance, ≤0.012 TIR; select shims and gauge axial/radial drag jointly. Forward cam carrier needs separate opposed keyed sliding lands, e.g. two 1.3-wide gibs 3.0 apart axially with ≥1.0 remaining tangential engagement. They transmit nut torque to fixed body at r~5.5 (36.4 N worst for 200), not through the stud. One-sided follower moments, contact patches and friction can bind these gibs; the thread-only torque does not include that penalty unless the mechanics model already does.

**Spline input and assembly:** rear skirt cup and front bearing separate on the bench; shim fixed rear washer to the flange-tied cartridge, orient races correctly. Mount actual spline hub/retaining screw on trimmed servo with an axial driver *before* cartridge insertion from the head face. Gauge case clearance, depth and retaining-screw head. Two tangential drive ears on the hub (r2.04..2.43, axial overlap 1.2 min) enter rear-open slots on the rotor socket through the rear bearing ID; both hub and socket finish below Ø4.94. At 200, one flank bearing ≈200/(2.04×0.39×1.2)=210 MPa nominal; small section, edge roots, wall opening, actual spline and impact unproven. Skirt journals, not the drive ears, locate the rotor. Maintain ≥0.10 axial float outside the screw head. Insert front bearing/seat, nut and tube, slide rails over followers with radial jaws in the split nose, install keepers and clock/calibrate both stops. Fixture actual travel before mounting nose. Longitudinal screws accessible from front, different angular sectors from jaws; use rear captured 304 parts, not structural PA12 threads. Existing diagonal M1.6 centres ±(7.8,4.2) at R=8.86; lengthen old 2.55 screws by actual +0.80 metal stack to retain existing engagement (3.35 nominal); recesses must not break into guide pockets. Check stiff annular washer backing, one fastener/pier reactions and fit through interrupted guides. Strip in reverse after uncoupling; do not claim access to rear hub through the engaged stud.

## 2. Torque, overload, sensing and pitch

**Loaded path.** SCS0037 rated 49.03 at 4.8 V, NOT its catalog stall torque. Take 9's 46 g, 12° loaded case costs 37.85 at the M2.5×0.35 thread alone, leaving **11.18** including both journals, races at breakaway, path errors, non-thread guides and coupling. Compare like with like; v6's 40.91/33.73 rotary numbers are not linear forces. For a 60° V-thread, d2=2.273, tan λ=0.0490; T/F=(d2/2)tan(λ+atan(μ/cos30°)). For illustrative μ=0.20 the equivalent force is 117.6 N; if the real calculation used a different μ, use its corresponding force, not this back-calculated value. Backdrive threshold μ>0.0425; retention must work on dwell even if friction falls below it. C954/17-4 nut/root are custom: nominal shear area ~π×2.07×1.54/2=5.0 mm², 24 MPa average at 118 N; first thread taking all on ~1.14 mm² sees 104 MPa. Prove actual load distribution, dry and lubricated wear, fatigue and strip/dilation; [Thomson 3 mm production nut](https://www.thomsonlinear.com/downloads/screws/Miniature_Lead_Screws_bren.pdf) is only 22.2 N dynamic, **not a substitute or rating for this part**.

[JTEKT bearing friction reference](https://www.jtekt.co.jp/products/bearing/en/support/bearing-knowledge/8-4000.html) gives stable-operation μ=0.0010..0.0015, not a worst startup coefficient. M≈μ P d/2 gives 0.44 N·mm at 118 N on the larger bore; with light assembly preload ≤5 N on each race <0.48 total. Even a provisional 3× stable estimate ~1.45 leaves 9.73, *not a bound*. For illustration journal μ=0.10 at r5.45 and 2 N·mm reserved for misc leaves Σ|R|≤14.2 N. Solve for both journal reactions with one jaw, loaded sleeve and measured misalignment; no half-sharing. Static/dynamic bearing numbers are neither reversible jam capacity nor fatigue life at this motion. Demonstrate drag with full lubricant/temp/load/endplay range; isolate and flag a fault rather than report arrival after a stall.

**Endpoint vs jam:** a nut-end dog against a rotor-end dog can arrest *rotation* without putting 3.59 kN into a hard axial end wall. It must encounter only in the last <0.35 and have radial engagement after eccentricity, wear and chamfers. At the small threaded-tube radius r1.55 the one-flank load is 129 N at the independent provisional 200 input (176.52 at the 6 V catalog stall, not an impact limit). A last-turn axial overlap ≤0.25 and effective radial width ≤0.40 gives ≥1.29 GPa nominal on the tiny end dog before bending — **reject that stop geometry**. A skirt stop at r5.25, 0.23 axial ×0.80 radial *effective* overlap would instead see 207 MPa nominal. Needs two clocked ends and a rigid return from nut, outside the fixed race/inside moving rails, and stop-arm root check. Not yet packaged; don't credit it. At a midtravel jam without independently verified limiter, frictionless 2πT/0.35 = 880 N at rated, 3,169 at catalog stall or 3,590 at 200; even μ=0.20 can give 622 N at 200, over the weak front race's 526 static. Monitoring the motor and a single soft end stop are not overload protection.

**Aisle:** unused v6 +y box x25.0..32.0, y7.25..11.15, z−7..−2 = 136.5 mm³ beyond r6.30, below boost, behind nut block and clear of sidewall. Extended conservative box x23.35..33.65 with the same section =200.85 mm³; free of *existing non-cartridge* frozen component boxes. Adjacent cell starts only 0.15 beyond the old r6.45 zone at another angle. Two Ø1.6 spring-link guides, axis x, y9.2, z−5.9/−3.0, would fit that aisle with 1.3 between their ODs. There is gross length 10.30, but need retainers, guide rod, travel, two pivots and swept connection from nut/sleeve. One-sided side link at e≈8.6 with short gibs can jam. Even splitting 118 N perfectly would require ≥59 N per path; engineering a bypass that tolerates a full 3.59 blocked stroke at that force in Ø1.6 is **not established**. A pop-out force trip (rather than packing constant-compression coils) needs independently bounded trip/reseat forces and a retained free-running path in **both** directions; a jam of the nut itself is upstream. Do not count the sensor and both links as occupying identical space.

**Indication/power:** [FEETECH pp3–7](https://www.feetechrc.com/Data/feetechrc/upload/file/20260622/6391771882565263995959110.pdf) open-loop continuous mode 2 allows roughly 10.17 turns over 3.56, not a jaw encoder. Place distinct *sleeve* end marks or physical flags; if an overload link disconnects, nut or turn count cannot certify closure/open. Also require proof of actual separation. Reserve radially facing [VCNT2030](https://www.vishay.com/docs/80223/vcnt2030.pdf), 1.85×1.2×0.6, ~1 gap, 3.3 V, gated ≈8 mA emitter (≈26.4 mW input per channel while lit); place two along travel or use a complementary pattern plus index. Alternative [SSCM110100](https://tech.alpsalpine.com/e/products/detail/SSCM110100/) 4×5×1.5, up to 0.35 N, 47 kΩ gives ~70 µA closed. SS part availability, GPIO, interlocks, contamination and witness window remain open. At two simultaneously lit, +52.8 mW at 3.3 V; if 80% board supply allowance add 66 mW input to v6 9.635 W, ~3.24 A at 3.0 V. Unchanged three-state half-duplex TTL/TPS61022, loaded terminal rail 4.8..5.2 V at 0.88 A peak; maintain startup/regen margins and inhibit after jam. No higher-output regulator assumed.

## 3. External tail drawbar and the ejector are different parts

- Tail PCB stays behind x−10.6. Extend external backing metal toward x−13.6 only; tail pitch from −11.6 to **−13.6**, head from 36.4 to **37.2**. A 3.03 max protruding stud tip is x−16.63 nominal at maximum outward travel, not past old x−18.10. Set hard axial travel caps so worst effective tip is ≥34.17 in head at the mating datum. This assumes complementary face geometry still prevents *deeper* mating; don't spend the same axial clearance on tooth deflection twice.
- Illustrative sealed 2.0-deeper cassette: [SPIROL 8×3.2×0.20, 660000; spring table](https://www.spirol.com/assets/files/dsc-disc-spring-design-guide-us.pdf) gives free H0=0.40, F≈8,12,20,26 N at deflections 0.03,0.05,0.10,0.15 respectively. Three opposed-in-series discs: free stack 1.20, 0.90 installed for nominal 20 N; 0.75 installed for nominal 26 N. Seat and guided Ø3.0 stem behind Ø2.18 head, outer rim support, shoulder and replaceable shim. With two 0.40 end plates +1.20 stack +0.35 stud piston +0.30 hard-seat margin=2.65 ≤3.00 available to pitch face from tail outer plane. Disc friction, end tolerance, stack force/draw at both first/last contact and fatigue need supplier bands or calibration. A hard backup can carry exceptional external tension but DOES NOT cap tension after bottoming: geometry must keep normal draw short of the backup; no assumption this 20–26 N interval meets mechanics' preload requirement. Passive metal jaws hold the stud; these springs meter draw, not latch closure.
- Possible **separate** centred head ejector: two opposing pads of annular face between r1.35..2.45 under the central face (teeth begin near r2.6), x36.35..37.2; nonrotating common yoke/equalizer with 0.5..0.65 outward stroke as a first space target only. The bridge/stud also sweep here. An axial ejection actuator, cocking energy and stop/recovery geometry are **not** present in the stack. Do not claim an extra screw turn, add spring loads for free, assume 0.65 clears all faces or treat passive disengagement as positive ejection. A post-clearance sequenced drive or independently sized, cocked release element must show sufficient force/travel with one pad/one tooth bearing and no long trapped pilot. Removed old stud length buys tail cassette depth, not more axial stroke at the head.
- G1 hand witness: sphere centre (10.8262,0,0), original body radius≤29.6782; maximum at extended negative flange ear corner is 29.8811. Even including teeth x37.4 and tail stud bounds, **diameter 59.762**. This only covers listed solids, not omitted ejectors or side links; keep full-width shell unchanged. G5 hand screen: zero roll, pitch **50.8**, neighbours translated by 50.8 and 101.6, end plate to following tail end plate gap **4.6** vs old 1.8. Body floor z=−12 and support polygon unchanged; all listed coupler solids stay above the table. Using the unchanged 34.871 g base COM (from dd7c0ad) plus even 0..15.5 g head, 0..3.6 g tail and 0..1.0 g side hardware at their bounds gives COM x11.23..21.54, y−2.22..2.42, inside head-floor rectangle [1.8,34.4]×[−12.7,12.7]. COM is a provisional bounding check, not a candidate inventory. At 46 g, W=.4511 N, tension 1.805, twist 11.46, out-of-plane bending now **22.92** vs 21.65, +5.83%. Recalculate loaded motion at the new pitch and actual mass; 37.85 is not a guarantee there.

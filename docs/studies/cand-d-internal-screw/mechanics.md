## Clearance alternative and decisive counterexample
Three radial 90° outward V sites about nominal R5.35 mm, location error ≤0.03 mm; central jaw retaining plane with one loaded lip inside e≤1.35 mm. For the nominal linearized directions, axial opening z_i, tangential shift h_i and flank tolerance g_i obey z_i+g_i >= |h_i|tan(44°), while one lip bounds the weighted sum of z_i+g_i by all-in positive gap G. This needs a real positive gap when all six flanks are seated, including errors, not independent worst negative facet gaps summed as positives. Minimum barycentric weight is (R/2−0.03−e)/(1.5R+0.06) = 0.1602. Thus sum(z_i+g_i) ≤G/0.1602. For G=0.04–0.06 mm local axial/tangential travel can reach bounds ~0.250–0.375/~0.259–0.388 mm. Nominal-axis first-order lateral/tilt/roll bounds ~0.17–0.26 mm, 1.8–2.7°, 0.93–1.40°. Opposite extreme to opposite extreme may double displacement. Budget facet width/depth, radial runout, throat, elastic motion, cap curvature, local height offset, finite-rotation and indexing errors before using these as contact guarantees. The 1.10 → 1.35 datum increase is not automatically enough: nominal head radius 1.00 plus 0.26 mm lateral leaves only 0.09 mm for all remaining errors at the 0.06 mm corner.

**Reciprocal wrench vs actual reaction.** With one lip and 2+2+1 flanks, a compatible boundary of the clearance polytope exists: two sites axially and tangentially seated, third at z0=G/λ0 with one tangential flank still touching (h0=±z0 at 45°); three axial and three tangential site coordinates are independent at first order. A deeper/longer facet can *preserve* this partial state, not eliminate it. The source constructs a nonnegative six-dimensional **zero-external-wrench ray** on just these contacts, μ=.23, including friction moment about each R1.4 sphere centre. The lip on the tail at −1.35y has normal −x. With lip normal 1 N, the five positive facet normals are 0.179, 0.264, 0.307, 0.395, 0.229 N. Tangents lie inside the Coulomb cone, not an outer approximation. Smaller e=0.80 and 1.10 also admit rays. These are equilibrium witnesses for idealized contact directions; not a measurement of how much stress develops. Any feasible fixed-service equilibrium of that same contact set can have multiples of such a ray added without changing its wrench, so statics does **not** give an upper one-jaw load. Conversely this does not prove a real assembly can acquire infinite stress. Prove the active set unreachable or bound stored strain/closure drive work, local stiffness, prior impacts, plastic set and release path over reachable states. Form closure or absence of intentional preload isn't that bound. This is a concrete stop for substituting open-loop clearance for measured force control. A capped input work estimate needs stiffness and initial energy bounds to convert to a force, plus a load path with no harder bypass.

## Well and ejection
For all reachable contact normals require n_x−μ sqrt(1−n_x²)>0. Half-angle 39° with ≤2° form/tilt debit and unsupported directional gap ≤0.38 under an interior rolling sphere ≥0.55 mm gives angular debit 20.21°, intrusion 0.034 mm, reserve +0.069 N/N. Must bound the **largest unsupported opening in the relevant contact direction**, not just one drawing dimension; rim and window corners, deflection, neck, exposed jaw and fillet all count. For a nearly flat rear mushroom shoulder there is a conflict: at radial coordinate r on a stud enclosed in radius 1, an interior tangent ball radius .55 requires normal radial component ≥1−(1−r)/.55. At r=.72/.95 this is ≥29.4°/65.4° off axial, incompatible with a 5° loaded retaining shoulder screen. A continuous positive shroud would need positive sequencing and protection during handoff, not just a final open outline. Hold e≤1.35 and throat radius under worst motion separately. No completed nose stack or shroud. At 46/50 g and 4W compression plus W table drag a pusher needs >2.256/2.452 N, plus internal drag and any negative contact axial force. 3.0 N provides 0.548 N reserve at 50 g, if that force is guaranteed throughout useful travel; include pusher reaction in closure. A short 0.3–0.4 mm stroke may demonstrate loss of interlock at current faces, not fully separated bodies. No 3.4 mm positive ejection by lever has been packaged or force-checked here.

**Decision:** retain the 3.53 mm package as a geometric opportunity only. Neither repeatable overload transfer/recovery nor a finite attained one-lip load at a frictionally wedged partial seat is established. Do not advance the mechanism or count separation until those two load paths and the window handoff are closed. No capture, physical qualification or Gate 1 claim.

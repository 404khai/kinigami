## Loads, material and source limits

The full-seat script uses 4W tension, W side load, 2W × 12.7 mm torsion, bending magnitude hypot(50.8W, 14.5 N·mm) before side-force height and eccentric draw. Contact directions in the full-seat calculation are ideal; the independent force-balance check samples transverse load directions, not manufacturing uncertainty. The closed-form sufficient draw screen subtracts the entire adverse eccentric moment. In the separate partial screen the potential stabilising arm is site-radius/2 minus location tolerance, jaw eccentricity, cap friction moment per unit force and side-load-height moment. A zero or negative remainder gives no finite bound by that argument. Its deliberately broad geometry sensitivity is not the tighter Take 9 tolerance stack.

[Slocum, *Kinematic couplings: a review of design principles and applications*](https://hdl.handle.net/1721.1/69013) describes the three-groove principle. That is prior art, not a Kin load rating. A rigid metal contact is not immune to indentation: 20 N on a 1.4 mm sphere against a plane at provisional reduced modulus 110 GPa gives ~2.9 GPa peak Hertz pressure. No benefit from two jaws or six equal contacts is assumed. The [Oerlikon linear-guide sheet, p2](https://www.oerlikon.com/ecoma/files/OerlikonBalzers_LinearGuides.pdf?download=true) lists dry friction against steel of 0.1–0.2 for two specified coatings, **not** certified static breakaway under our load, contamination, wear or edge contact. Do not silently substitute that low value. Sources inspected 2026-11-20.

The [ISC/NSK F5-10M table, p45](https://www.nskmicro.co.jp/en_ch_bearing_size_pdf/ISC_P45.pdf) gives 1.33 g and 1025/1156 N dynamic/static. The [Fitrun F4-8M](https://www.fitrunbearing.com/product/f4-8m/) gives 705/526 N. Neither supplies a start/reverse torque bound for this shoulder, lubricant, preload and misalignment. [ZKL §6.1.1](https://www.zkl.cz/en/produkty/obecne-informace-pro-konstruktery/6-mezni-otacky-a-vibrace) gives M≈μPd/2 with μ=0.0013 for thrust ball bearings; the stated conditions include proper lubrication and P≈0.1C. At 118 N and 5 mm bore that is 0.38 N·mm for one race in normal operation, **not an upper bound on loaded breakaway**. The [FEETECH A/0 sheet, pp3–5](https://www.feetech.cn/Data/feetechrc/upload/file/20260622/6391771882565263995959110.pdf) distinguishes 270° angle-servo mode from open-loop motor mode and states no mechanical angle limit. Counting ~10 turns in motor mode is neither a sleeve-position sensor nor a time guarantee.

## Why an intentional clearance does not close release

A 0.020 mm axial clearance budget can give bounded first-order lash with a flat lip inside the triangle of three V sites. That is useful: unpowered stiffness need not mean arbitrary continuous preload. It does **not** imply tension at the lip is bounded by the applied external wrench. Try this checked counterexample:

- Site radius 5.35 mm, three 90° V pairs, crowned pad radius 1.4 mm. The flat lip acts at (0.12,−0.74,0) mm relative to the cap-centre plane. Each face starts at 0.010/√2 mm normal gap; the opposing lip starts at 0.010 mm axial gap.
- In the script's first-order pose, one V flank remains 0.1173 mm open; the other five and the lip can bear. Each force lies inside the assumed μ=0.23 static cone: the greatest tangential/normal ratio needed is 0.18. The moment arm uses the actual flank contact, **not the sphere centre**.
- The listed nonnegative reactions balance all six wrench components with no external load. Scaling them by a positive factor leaves rigid equilibrium unchanged. This is **not** infinite physical force. Actual deflection, loading history and an independent force limit set it. Merely dividing the external moment by an arm, or solving for one favourable pressure distribution, misses this case. The first-order witness is not a finite-deformation contact validation.

## Independent separation, not just an open indicator

The proposed outward-flared well could make all accessible wall reactions assist withdrawal. For a smooth wall of local half-angle a and Coulomb coefficient μ the worst outward axial projection per unit normal is sin(a)−μ cos(a). At a slot, the effective angle changes: a spherical probe of radius R bridging unsupported width g incurs a normal-angle debit asin(g/2R) and intrusion R−√(R²−(g/2)²). See [release/window arithmetic](release_window.py). These equations cannot be applied to a flat rear shoulder with a sharp edge.

At 50 g, 4W compression plus W of table drag is 2.452 N. A 3 N centred pusher leaves just 0.548 N for other drag even if **all** other accessible surfaces have nonnegative axial projection. Its thrust, actual available post-clearance stroke and absence of reachable retaining edges must be shown separately. Independent release means head actuation can remove the mechanical engagement under the declared load; a stalled nut, open command, unloaded spring or momentary loss of one jaw is not evidence of separation.

## Discriminator before building a candidate

1. Supply a swept, tolerance-dimensioned section through the fixed seat, reinforced bridge/shoe, radial gibs, end dogs, jaws, guard and centred pusher. Include maximum loaded offset and withdrawal through the window. A 0.70 mm follower pin and cantilever bridge are not viable at the sizing load.
2. Bound service breakaway and the actual trip thresholds **in both directions** under one-jaw load at the completed mass/pitch. Apply them to a midstroke obstruction as well as endpoints; verify the mechanism does not re-engage a loaded obstruction, trap a clamp load or strip a thread. Tangential end dogs alone do not protect a blocked nut.
3. Obtain credible supplier/application data or instrument a representative cartridge when published envelopes cannot bound assembled drag. Support the fixed seat; apply the service force and moment at the actual lip and one or two pad sites; separately record input torque, nut travel, sleeve travel, both jaw positions, axial gap and pusher force. Include dry, intended-lubricated and changed-friction reversal; reject an obstruction without calling it a lock/release. A bench result would support a new decision; it does not change the registered gate.
4. Only then update the candidate under a *new*, prior decision and commission both independent reviews. The screw, 4:1 planetary, offset screw and clearance variant are not proven impossible as mechanism classes.

# Take 16 final screen — linear keeper, spherical tail, continuously convex cheek

**Exploratory design-author result.** The sphere fixes contact-patch wander caused by barrel attitude. It does not fix the movement of the three **station centres**, a jammed guide or the package. A low-drag guided coupon gives useful forces; not a demonstrated free release. No pivot, candidate or review edits.

## Section and coupled force calculation

One concrete size, not a sweep of the whole proposed family: R=6.00, b=2.000±.015, keeper meridian Rk≈1.50, cheek Rc≈2.00; require actual centre distances DK=b+Rk=3.50±.03, DC=b+Rc=4.00±.03, working Rk≥1.48. Tail sphere, keeper cylinder extruded along local t, two fixed-side cheek profiles extruded along local r; 90°+120°i stations. Signed forces on the tail: nK=−cosβ x−sinβ r, nC=sinχ x±cosχ t. A hard rear face pushes +x. Hard back-seat/front clearance g=.03–.09 after development/elastic errors, opposed cheek centre spacing >2DC including asymmetric radius/tolerance allowances (more precisely the sum of the two actual inflated cylinder radii). Compliant shoe on one cheek and on rear axial face as in shuttle.md; when retracted these become the correct bare surfaces, no loose bore/rim in the path.

For simultaneous contacts at one station, x_i = x_K0 + DK(1−cosβ) = x_C0 + DC sinχ. On the inner cheek branch t_i=±[g/2+DC(1−cosχ)] relative to its nominal site. One sign or zero per hard pair, never two cheeks. For exact nominal datums the opposing-axial normal product on the *front* keeper is at most (DK/DC)cosβ(1−cosβ)≤.219; the two normals are not independently selectable at their worst angles. As soon as local x_i−x_C0 > DC sin(atan μ + normal-error), every cheek resultant has positive x regardless of side force. Threshold .897 nominal, about .938 with DC max and .5° normal error, or 1.078 including the extra .14 mm local datum budget. This does **not** unload a remaining keeper.

The solver uses a common translating tail-ring origin, no commanded correction. Ring rotation angle ≤3° (all axes); lateral translation norm ≤.50 mm. Local x_i lies within X±R sin3°±.14, with .08 centre and .06 track errors. Split common X into .12 mm intervals; invert both cosine and sine coordinates for each station, allow all covered signs/absences, actual stepped angles with a second-derivative remainder, including passage around a complete keeper onto its exterior arc. A cheek backside cannot be reached inside this small lateral-offset tube. Physical partial arc shoes must cover the relevant arcs and exclude side, tip and backer contacts; a partial arc is not automatically equivalent to a complete cylinder. Use actual pressure/friction at the sphere pole, μ≤.23 at the ball, a 12-sided **outer** Coulomb cone, .5° resultant direction uncertainty, .12 mm distributed contact moment-arm allowance. No equal loading. Complete soft-phase solves use six additional independent bound multipliers; edge solves use three outward-free unilateral blade inequalities. Missing contacts carry zero; a nonequilibrated pose can move. None of this bounds impact.

## Which pose bounds are actually structural?

**All three keepers home only:** a sufficient local confinement argument additionally requires actual combined per-site tail/head datum residual ≤.08 mm in each coordinate, not .08+.06, and all finite faces covering the whole trial boundary. Start inside |transverse translation|≤.50, total rotation θ≤3°, |ring X|≤.75. At first possible exit from this region, all K spans and radial projections still overlap; front arc contacts can't be bypassed on the inside component. Maximum radial error at the pole ≤.50+.08+R(1−cos3°); corresponding closed keeper opening h≤.0511. Hard rear/keeper bound nominal x stations in [−.09−.08,.08+h]; their spread at R6 gives sin(pitch/tilt)≤(.09+.16+h)/(1.5R); mean X is also inside the trial region. Tangential station deviation after removing common roll term ≤q=.045+[3.97−√(3.97²−(.18+h)²)]+.08+(R/2)(1−cos3°)=.136. For site unit tangents t_i the signed coordinates equal t_i·d+R u_x sinθ plus a Rodrigues residual already in q. Since ∑t_i=0, ∑t_i t_iᵀ=(3/2)I laterally, |d|≤4q/3=.182 and |u_x sinθ|≤q/R. Combine with the axial tilt via 1−cosθ=1−Qxx+(u_x sinθ)²/(1+cosθ); θ≤2.316°. Each is strictly inside its trial boundary. A continuous path from the seated component cannot cross it while **all three** structural stops stay home. This is a hard-geometry conditional, not spring friction, docking, or credit for an unverified screw dwell; gauge the assumed errors/coverage. A hard interference must never replace the bounded pads.

**Free partials:** after one blade clears, the argument loses one axial inequality. Tilt about the chord through the two other centres leaves them stationary. At **12°**, the free centre advances 1.871 mm and moves radially just .197 mm toward the axis; its local tangential offset remains zero, comfortably between the flaring cheeks. Sphere curvature is orientation invariant. Thus the named bare faces do NOT derive the ±3° envelope through release; an unknown backing/neck collision would be a new contact, not an attitude certificate. The older unrestricted per-site outer model in cylpair.py returned missing LP certificates, not proof of actual self-stress; it is included as a negative result. Treat free partials and out-of-tube entry, back/side rubbing, actual release after loss of power and jam recovery as unresolved.

**Recommendation:** test one actual curvature-axis slide (or proposed pivot separately), actual 4 mm support spans and gauge F(s) at one loaded shoe under combined force/moment. Reject the linear option if .10 effective guide drag, force/patch window, or physical stroke can't be obtained. Next instrument the one-free-keeper chord rotation under load *without an external attitude clamp*. Add the observed pose families, possible roller backs, stem/track ends and drive stops to the force model before any freeze. A pivot removes slide drag but needs its own swept clearances, friction moment, backed dwell, reverse work and independent force limiter; these results do not rate it.

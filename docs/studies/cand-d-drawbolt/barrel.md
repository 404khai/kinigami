# Take 16 addendum — barrel crowns, contact patches and the missing attitude bound

**Exploratory.** The .60 mm fillet and 12° continuation are worth contact coupons. They do not qualify a six-station or three-station latch. Even after curing the equatorial flat, a legal partially open pose puts the three-station keeper on a ridge. Do not transfer the 43.1 N or 3.38 GPa result between geometries.

## First: which solid is touched?

Write tail coordinates (x,r,t), b=1.045–1.055, H=20, B(u)=b−H+√(H²−u²). K front keeper touches the tail's +x→+r arc; C cheek exit touches ±t→−x. In the **original one-sided** intersection a +t flat is supported on an entire generator: at r=0,t=b, 0≤x≤B(b)=1.01768 (b=1.045). A positive 0.5° cheek tip toward +x maximizes t cosθ+x sinθ at x=B(b), not x=0. Both constraints are active there and the smooth-barrel Hertz calculation is invalid. Similar extrusion at +r after the last keeper contact. A nominally C1 meridian at x=0 does not repair the distal ridge.

**The proposed 12° continuation really fixes that generator.** Set h(x,y)=hypot(x,y) when x≥−sin12°·hypot(x,y), otherwise h=−x sin12°+|y|cos12°. Intersect h(x,r)≤B(t) and h(−x,t)≤B(r). For x≥0, |t|≤.70 and |r|≤b, h(−x,t)≤max(.70/cos12°,b sin12°+.70cos12°). The latter ≤.903; comparing to B(b) leaves ≥.1157 mm in the b=1.045 case (also holds over b range). Opposite signed half is symmetric. Extend or gauge transverse end relief beyond this strip; allow ±.04 mm boundary reserve, not curvature credit on an intersection ridge. At the ±t meridian the continuation length ≈.217 mm along x, longer than a central .065 mm meridional half patch plus b sin(1.5°) and .04 margin. Rear axial seat at −x bears the C barrel (transverse radial), NOT the K barrel. At the rear pole the two cheek signs do not define two independent −x load carriers: if both cheeks remain outside their opposed parallel datum planes, a body with smaller full transverse support width cannot bridge them. For ≤.5° *total relative orientation* a crude width bound is 2b+2√2 b sin(.5°); the increase is <.0261 mm. Compare to the **loaded** minimum gap after all form/dent/tilt errors, not just bore nominal. Contact along two faces of a burr or outside corner is excluded, not silently modeled. Without this width condition don't assume one polarity per coordinate.

## Force and Hertz numbers: distinct models

Use E*=115.4 GPa illustratively, both bodies elastic, local rectangular-section axes aligned, no edge truncation. Effective principal curvatures on an edge are 1/b+1/.60 and 1/H; on the flat replace 1/.60 by zero. Solve elliptical Hertz using κ_i=[3F/(2πE*)]∫₀∞ds/[(aᵢ²+s)√((a²+s)(bₚ²+s)s)], p₀=3F/(2πabₚ). For tilted or rotated curvature tensors recompute their principal values; no certificate for dents, a ridge, or a seam inside a patch. Conditional numerical bounds:

Those are minimum transverse curvature radii in the **fixed-force scalar example**, not material allowables or recommendations to change radius without re-solving contact forces! Flatter transversely improves centered pressure but increases wander H sinε, quickly truncating the patch. The minimum exposed track length is twice (lateral centre scatter + H sinε + actual transverse half-patch + end margin). At .18 mm relative lateral shift, .5° relative tilt and .05 mm end margin this gives 1.360 / 1.443 / 1.487 mm in the three rows. To use the LP's full .45 mm transverse patch budget specify **≥1.710 mm** finished straight usable edge length, not a blank dimension; required barrel surface width ≥1.350 mm before any neck. Relative displacement from the fixed track must actually remain ≤.18; the translating wrench origin does not impose that. On the colocalized ±.70 material strip with .04 margin, maximum allowable transverse-axis tilt at those forces is respectively 1.103°, .984°, .921°, even with a perfectly registered track. These are not capture windows.

## Restricted common-pose certificate

Tail ring reference translates with body, axes parallel to the head. Axial stations at 90°+120°i; tangential at those azimuths or staggered +60°. Rank six: the triple of axial poles spans thrust and both bending axes, the three tangential poles span transverse force and roll; no seventh central clamp. On a circular H barrel the contact point at relative transverse normal component sinε is (b−H+Hcosε)n_m+Hsinε u in outward-tail coordinates. Relative to a spherical pole b n, the extra lever has length 2(H−b)sin(|ε|/2): ≤.166 at .5°, **not** a new .166 total machining allowance. The solver brackets both transverse extremes of this lever plus .45 mm patch, and all .18 mm body-centre uncertainty plus .065 mm meridional patch, normal-ray deviation ≤1.5°, outer 12-ray μ=.23 friction cone and angular interpolation. This is a required effective force/patch cone; verify on the real loaded surfaces. An elastic patch isn't an assertion that all local pressure vectors on a dent lie in that cone. Relative orientation conditions on the physical tail (≤.5° tilt, total transverse-axis/track misalignment ≤.5° in the modeled patch) are **additional** to head normal errors. Not .5°+.5° silently relabeled .5°.

One common ring advance X partitions 0..90° keeper angles at every site. With h≈(b+.60)(1−cosα), X can differ from h by ≤.26 mm (≤.08 plane, .08 station axial, .041 tilt, .01 radius and remaining profile/deformation). For each .12 mm X-cell include the interval of each reachable α, further expand by two 5.625° cells for patch/normal spread, admit optional rear normal only at small X. Absent contacts have zero normal. When any keeper bears, largest local centre x≤1.655+.08+.065+.04; any other centre differs by ≤9.30 sin(.5°)+.16. With straight cheeks reaching **2.40±.04 mm** forward of reference centre, even moving the onset back another .065+.04 can't put a cheek tip in that occupied range. This explicitly excludes conflicting edge states *by geometry* rather than independently granting every site an opposing axial normal. Larger uncontrolled attitude or a shorter cheek invalidates this pruning. On final ejection no keeper remains, all positive rear pushes plus every independently signed flaring cheek are allowed; common body reference removes spurious moment from common translation. A swept back seat may be far from its original x in this stage. Force and moment use actual poles in each cell (script). No impulse or snap assumed.

Outward-free blade force inequality uses actual load projections, not a multiple of normal alone: besides base .8 N, radial drag ≤.23(1.645|Fₓ|+1.5575|F_t|+1.027|Fᵣ|). Requires two guide reactions ≥3.0 mm apart, transverse spacing ≥3.2, |e_t|≤.84, |e_x|≤.70, 2|radial offset|/span≤1.12 over full loaded stroke; bound parameter errors at force rays too. On drive use separately centred e_t,e_x≤.10, k_D≤.36 up to 37°, so 6 N capped closure gives 8.96 N max opposing base in edge dual. Outward pocket, no inward stopper at any angle once an edge bears. Under adverse correction a 35–37° cam on staggered bars screens at **41.21 N** centered vs 31.61 available; with 27–29° it is **28.38 N**. The co-located case needs 34.69 N even at 29°, or 30.45 N at 26° (24–26° section). Formula: q=(tanθ+.23)/(1−.23tanθ), k_D=.23[(1.12+.20/3.2)q+.40/3], D=(resistance+2.4)/(1−k_D), A=qD; add one-sided guide cost as in shuttle.py. Longer stroke follows, not free advantage. Relative geometry, spring unloading and all guide limits must be measured; no proof of 3.530 mm package. A 2.40+.04+.60+1.055+.08+.04 ≈4.22 mm full-separation pusher allocation alone is a first screen, not the earlier sphere's 2.80.

## Decisive partial-pose counterexample and next experiment

Open one keeper; pivot the tail about the chord joining the other two centres. This pitch is a first-order null motion of the two remaining axial coordinates and all three ideal tangential coordinates. At g=.09 (in spec), start at mid-clearance. At **3°** the free third centre advances 1.5R sin3°=.365 mm away from its back seat and shifts only 1.5R(1−cos3°)=.00956 transversely. Any of the original full barrel pole supports grows by at most (H−b)(1−cos3°)=.02598, from its exact support function H+(b−H)cosε; intersection with the other barrel only shrinks it for the co-located case. Sum <.045 half gap. This whole motion fits the six hard-coordinate bounds with deep parallel cheeks; a small common axial translation then puts either remaining keeper on its pole. No missing stop or extra neck contact is credited. Not a simulation of the transit or claim every assembled part can execute it.

For a remaining keeper its t-axis is 60° from the pivot chord; sinε=sin60°sin3°, ε=2.598°. The unconstrained K support goes to (x,r,t)=(1.0241,−.0268,.9065), outside the other barrel by .05462. The constrained support is approximately (1.02657,−.02691,.85012), **both** inequalities active. This is a ridge without needing any finite Hertz patch. A narrow perfectly aligned test holding the remote body level would miss it. Even a fully closed three-pair axial span .090 can permit ~.74° tilt in the first-order geometry (1.5R sinθ across the three sites); after one keeper leads, there is no three-pair tilt bound. Nor do staggered independent barrels guarantee never reaching their end: at the same attitude a 20 mm crown wanders ~.906 mm before counting the patch. Requirement: bound *all reachable relative tail orientations* until the last loaded surface clears or model actual blended return/end surfaces outside the central strip. Don't use an added guide as a seventh contacting locator without redoing forces, and don't assume rocking resistance or servo angle imposes attitude.

**Next recommendation:** first cut one 12° section coupon with the .60 tip, dye/contact imprint and measure F–displacement and pressure/indentation under 0°, .5°, 1°, and the 3° partial-chord case. Support it for single-contact strength trials only; then repeat the one-free-keeper sequence with the tail free to pitch under controlled service wrench, measuring attitude, individual drag and damage. Stop if the patch reaches the intersection/end or outside the measured cone. Either design a continuous, controlled-radius return through that region and re-certify its normals, or find a positive sequence with a proved finite attitude bound. Only then test the four-start drive, take-up and package together. No further candidate work justified by these upper bounds.

Run: `python3 -B attitude.py`; `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -B barrel.py --kind fault --g0 8.96`; repeat for correction/service, and append `--colocated` for the repaired three-centre solid. Bare drive: `--focus drive --g0 .8 --kind correction`; exit: `--stage exit --focus push --kind service --g0 .8`; biased: `--stage charged --kind correction`. Geometry conditions, including the relative-attitude stop, belong to the calculation.

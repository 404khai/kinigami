# Take 16 — complete internal screw, measured take-up, single axial shuttle

**Exploratory design-author study; build a bench coupon, not a Kin candidate.** Direct thread exit fails even in pure tension (see direct.md). Here all working screw flanks stay inside the head; none crosses the mating boundary. Tail tension passes through front keepers, compression through back seats, bending through their station spacing, roll/shear through tangential cheeks. The last keeper edge is a bounded rolling/sliding release into free motion, not a disappearing loaded screw thread. The absence of a static equilibrium means motion, not proof of safe dynamics or load retention after release. Q31, Q85, Q99, Q12 and all registered criteria unchanged; no official run or measured capacity.

## Active take-up coupon: six independent loads, not an 80 N clamp

Start with Take 11's three Ø2.09–2.11 spheres on R4.65; second, separate six-barrel experiment in barrel.md. One full-face guided metal shoe replaces the rear pad at each axial site and **one** tangential flat at each shear site. The other flat remains hard. Bias all tangential shoes in the specified same local positive sense. The shoe can depress to a broad metal land: once flush, structure carries any additional load around the spring. On withdrawal a positive pullback brings the entire face to its flush, hard-backed datum; it cannot leave an exposed bore/rim or retract a face needed as a geometric stop. Do not put a central button inside a larger hole and assume the ball contacts only the flat. Land spacing exceeds maximum member width throughout elastic/wear limits. No spring moves or holds a structural keeper closed.

All-in excess of hard gap over ball diameter g=.030–.090 mm, including loaded geometric and elastic corners. This is tighter than frozen v7's .09–.21 mm axial excess. Backer travel follows actual contact, not a commanded common endpoint. With ball on the opposite land, required free-tip height h=g+δ ranges .223–.340 axial or .205–.340 tangential; with a reversed load the depressed shoe can reach its own hard land and then δ=h. A bypass stop reacting into the housing limits h to .350 **including** .010 mm pickup/overrun, at each site; spring stays elastic/not solid over at least .40 mm. In general g_max ≤ h_cap − δ_trip,max − pickup. At .21 gap this design would need .470, not .350. Measure the physical shoe force with a depth gauge and load cell in both directions; g must stay positive even with an obstruction up to protected load. A jam that ties the shoe to its driver defeats the soft-force inequality. No force is inferred from turns or current.

For actual Q85 take-up, try six independent small rockers driven in the final part of the same shuttle stroke. Each advances until its own adjustable mechanical torque limiter slips at contact; others can continue. For a 1 mm perpendicular working lever the ideal output slip moments are numerically the trip forces in N mm; commission at the **shoe**, not the common drive. Propose a separately backed toothed sector per rocker (e.g. rack at 4× the output lever, ≤.060 arc pitch INCLUDING play referred to that radius gives ≤.015 output recoil). A common positive locking bar backs the six indices and a housing cross-key secures the cylindrical radial shutter dwell. No reliance on thread friction, servo holding torque or spring latch for structural tension. Partial tooth engagement/weak roots can defeat this scheme: actual engagement, stroke and release are required measurements, not drawn parts. On reverse a short pickup retakes spring load before mechanically lifting the indices, then positively retracts all six shoes with shutter still hard backed. Stop and report jam unless the shutter actually closed, each shoe took load in range, and the index is backed; a preset stroke to a hard wall does not meet Q85.

Seat forces exceed **frictionless** service reciprocal coordinates at 50 g (4.786 axial, 1.220 tangential). They do not guarantee forced alignment, all six far hard lands seated under Coulomb friction, or no elastic displacement under load reversal. A depressed shoe maintains contact throughout loading/unloading; there is no deliberate unloaded interval before hard backup. At adverse correction/fault an axial coordinate alone can require ~8.95 N without friction, more than the minimum bias. Retain the elastic deflection and full hard-clearance envelope in pose/strength checks. If the required function is zero shift at all far lands throughout those cases, this coupon is not proof of it.

**Finite force before release.** Let b=(9.8,9.8,9.8,5.6,5.6,5.6) N. The unbounded hard force n has only one active hard polarity per coordinate; the opposed soft contribution is bounded even at the hard land. With Take 11's reciprocal H and external box d, n≤d+(I+H)b+Hn. The same contraction ≤.812 applies to all subsets, no equal sharing. At 50 g single hard normal bounds are 67.90/86.92 N axial/tangential service, 78.67/97.80 correction, 77.85/96.17 fault. A collocated pad/land may carry their sum: e.g. ≤88.47/103.40 N at correction. Loose scalar enclosures, not actual pressures. These are **not** the bare-edge values. At 50 g, 43.34 N on the existing .10 keeper roll and R1.045 sphere gives ~12.17 GPa elastic peak for E*=115.4 GPa: do not prototype the loaded tip as reusable without changing it. The larger-fillet staggered study does not inherit these reactions.

## One shuttle, three strictly sequenced phases

1. **Take up/unload while fully backed.** Target dx_shoe/ds=.35–.37 and measured loaded work transmission efficiency ≥.70 including one-site load, slips on already seated outputs, guide and rocker losses; add 2 N miscellaneous. Envelope .37×3×(9.8+5.6)/.70+2=26.42 N. The efficiency allocation is a force-transfer coupon requirement, **not** a guarantee for a slipping output at zero velocity; directly gauge max input through every stage combination including 5 stopped/1 advancing and index pullback. If the independent torque limits need more stroke or force than this, reject the design. Load release FIRST: verify all six backers are sufficiently relieved/flush (bias zero over remaining face motion), then unlock outer keeper flanks while still on flat poles.
2. **Operate only the bare keepers.** Inner pickups push each blade out but cannot hold an assisted blade back. Open pocket to full clearance, no output end stop while its blade can still bear at an edge. On closing each inward output must have its own ≤6 N limit incl overshoot, compliant over entire jammed stroke or slipping until positive pickup; do not divide the 124 N screw thrust by three. Hold positive dwell out of engagement until each blade is home. The angular mixed LP with actual 90° station clocking bounds one axial edge during capped closing at 38.81/43.30/43.34 N (50 g service/correction/fault, g0′=.8+(1+.468)6 rounded up). After physical unload, total load-dependent radial resistance ≤5.30/10.09/10.18 N respectively; add .8 N each blade, divide by 1−.468; dry 35–37° cam multiplies by ≤1.190. Other disallowed seat corners, re-entry and inaccessible poles remain go/no-go.
3. **Eject only beyond all three physical keeper clearances.** Move the actual three back seats on a centered guided crosshead ≥2.80 mm for the Take 11 sphere cheek section. A separate grounded linkage pushes centrally on the crosshead, not on the rim opposite the loaded ball. No arch/bridge may bottom or deflect past one ball. Previous six-pair cheek-exit proof gives >9.898 N sum delivered after guide drag vs 4W+W and service wrench at 50 g; specify 10.30 N. An earlier gap isn't separation; witness clearance beyond all hard features after power loss.

**Don't omit the one-sided shuttle reactions.** For three 35–37° tracks, let D be summed radial driver force and A=1.190D. Specimen guide lands span ≥6, follower r≤6, axial offset ≤5, drive eccentricity ≤.12 mm. Upper total guide normal ≤√2[(1+10/6)D+12A/6] plus input-eccentricity feedback 2√2·.12F/6; no tangential cam force assumed. Require radial-extruded tracks, separate torque-key support and measured preload/seal drag ≤1 N. Rolling linear-guide drag ≤.012 times that total is a **demand**, not an available bearing spec. Capped input for bare blades is 30.97 N correction or 31.19 fault; seized/sliding at .23 gives a 68.86 N fault **screen**, not a necessity. Thus a 30 N source doesn't certify this phase and a dry slide can't be waved through at 35 N. 80 N would improve the work budget but not the single-output caps or strength. Nut antirotation and screw support are separately charged below; if these guides take that torque too, add their extra drag.

For ejection give an axially guided yoke effective span and roll reaction spacing ≥6, seat r≤4.88, axial cantilever ≤5, center drive offset ≤.12. For unit total seat normal X≤1+.23sin(.5°), S≤.23+sin(.5°). Total square-guide normal ≤√2[(1+10/6)S+2(4.88/6)X]+2(4.88/6)S (includes roll couple), plus input-eccentricity feedback; use μ=.23, 1 N constant yoke drag. This needs 20.10 N axial at the crosshead. Require 1.10–1.15 output/input stroke gain, ≥.80 worst-direction force efficiency of the grounded converter incl side thrust plus 2 N other input drag: ≤30.89 N centered. No output shared equally. Measure eccentric force on each one loaded seat, particularly after obstruction removal.

**No stroke credit from an empty collar:** .360/.35 (retract, reseat and clear pads), 1.63/tan35° (keeper), 2.80/1.10 (exit), .20 provisional sequencing budget = **6.102 mm active** for this profile, before extra reverse jam pickup, physical end reserves or package growth. 3.530 mm Take 10 from a different section is insufficient. Even at 80 N this particular transmission doesn't acquire more travel. Packing all three operations needs another section or real motion-ratio redesign. Six positively sequenced outputs with bearings, stops, indexes, stripper and local sensors are not drawn inside 60 mm.

## Multi-start coupon, both directions

Custom internal M3 blank, axial pitch .50, **lead** 1.50 or 2.00 per turn. Symmetric output clutch 30–37 N mm both directions, including breakaway/overrun. Constrain to a single pressure-flank sign; use working radii 1.12–1.50, lead tolerance ±.020, flank 30° ±.5° **normal** deviation, μ=0–.23. For h=L/2π, z=√(1+tan²30°+(h/r)²), the ideal flank normal has axial component 1/z and circumferential component −h/(rz). For the entire friction cone in one sector, sufficient raising and positive lowering coefficients k satisfy (k∓h)/z≥sin(atan μ+.5°)√(r²+k²). Sum axial components, all positive, so no equal turn loading. Code uses rectangular r/L bounds. At nominal L2,r1.50 (no errors) k_raise≈.761, k_lower,+≈.077 N mm/N. For quasistatic **tightening against passive resistance**, friction cannot supply tightening work: conservative minimum frictionless coefficient from minimum lead and form margin. The torque cap limits *newly generated* thrust, not outside service reactions, impact, an interference fit, pre-existing overcompression, seizure or a second simultaneously loaded flank.

Choose **four-start** for a reversible coupon, not three. At max jam the 3-start leaves only .19 N mm for all static breakaway; 4-start leaves 17.50. Gauge support/journal/clutch drag in both directions at load: ≤5 N mm at work, <17.50 at full formed jam **if no new load overlaps**. Mixed sign: k_lower P_old + k_raise F_new + drag <30; with full old jam and 5 N mm drag only ~15.8 N new work fits. Positively unload trapped elastic force over a measured lost-motion pickup before picking up loaded blades or pusher, or prove the inequality stage by stage. An assisted low-friction backdrive can accelerate a rotor; brake/absorb energy and witness actual release. Clutch/slip indication is never evidence of clearance. Fit double-ended rotational stop dogs in unused sectors or proof-load terminal stops; no assumption that torque slip instantly bounds a dynamic shock.

Neither the nut nor a plain journal can be assumed centered under a one-sector flank. Oppose both axial thrust signs in catalog races; test combined lateral and eccentric load as well as axial load. With z≤1.190 and .5° form error, axial resultant per unit normal ≥1/z−2sin(.25°)−.23[√(1−1/z²)+2sin(.25°)]>.70. Lateral per unit normal ≤√(1−1/z²)+2sin(.25°)+.23<.79. At 124 N this can impose ~140 N side load plus eccentric bending ~186 N mm at 1.5 radius; tab at ≥3 mm can additionally see 37/3=12.34 N. Seized guides, misaligned nut, both-flank bind or galling are not proven reversible. Actual thread must retain useful full-form length at both ends; for two complete turns per start on L=2.02, nut ≥4.04 mm fully formed, screw fully formed length ≥4.04+6.102=10.142 mm **before** runouts,end reserve or jam travel. Take 10's 1.56 mm complete-thread nut is not that nut. No supplier tolerance or body-mass credit for this custom multi-start or the proposed miniature indexers. FEETECH 49.03 N mm rated is not a moving torque curve or 37 N mm guaranteed anywhere in stroke.

**Bench order:** measure six F–δ loops and loaded hard-gap/backup with independent load cells. On a transparent stopped section, mark each index, blade, yoke, gap, compression and both thread ends; shutter must stay hard-backed through all pad release. Block each moving output midstroke at the two friction extremes, independently remove the block, then demonstrate repeatable positive reverse and separation under signed service; bench abort/fault otherwise. Measure actual F(s), breakaway, individual normals and speed/impact, not shaft angle alone. Reboot at partial pad, blade and stripper positions; report unknown until observed locally. Do not run another candidate on this evidence.

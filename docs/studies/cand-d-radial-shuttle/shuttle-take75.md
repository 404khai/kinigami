**Label: exploratory. Disposition: reject this installation.** The guide cassette preserves the local Take 73 interface. The complete covered mechanism does not fit Ø60, has two separate material interferences and cannot guarantee loaded unlocking. No Take 76 elastic seat is installed. Q12, Q85, Q100 and Gate 1 are unchanged; no PR, push, commit, Gate run or fabrication authorization.

## 2. Actuation, lock and physical loads

A new single axial face plate in x=−7.03..−5.03 carries three finite captive grooves, one per directly driven Ø1.80 roller at y=18.35+stroke. There is no inherited rotor/leaf clevis. The pin is Ø1.20 at x≈−6.04. The cam leads between 4° and 68° using a half cosine; its max lead is 2.586 mm/rad. The slots have finite endpoints and constant-radius parking regions. The cutter leaves nominal .696/.517 mm inner/outer radial ligaments and only .060 mm axial bottom. This is not a Hertzian, fatigue or roller-retention rating.

The jaw input is a 0615N003S + 06/1 256:1, permanently meshed 16:32 spur and single-start 122T worm wheel. The hard lock is a separate square-keyed twelve-hole star, spring-biased Ø.70 shear pin, two separated fixed bushes, and a captive withdrawal cam with its own 64:1 motor and 1:1 spur. A fully landed pin overlaps 1.46 mm of straight Ø.764 bore; no seat/worm friction is credited. The separate brake spring cannot drive a plug inward. One-side nominal shaft float including star key and finite pin play is 4.155°. With the shaft contained axially to ±.015 mm by shoulder/collar, the expanded-hob material witness blocks wheel motion by .24° in **both** directions over all twelve phases. PARK indexes at 72.049°. These are local kinematics: an unseated pin, fractured pin, missing support stiffness, or power loss at a handoff defeats any unconditional lock claim. Input motor position is not pin or plug position.

**Work and load.** At an assumed zero internal guide preload, two-row determinate bearing model, guide μ=.05 and effective groove drag μ=.06, the deliberately adverse independent-component bound is 3.1715 N radial drive and 12.976 N·mm ring torque per N of total external contact normal. It includes the ≈5–6 mm axial load arm, cam tangential thrust, both guide reaction couples, and plug twist into opposed pad wings. Do not split a one-plug load three ways. With 100 N on one contact (illustration, **not** a Take 73 normal bound), this gives 317 N drive, up to 1.30 N·m ring, and an impractical 2.88 GPa nominal pin-bending sizing demand. Independent maxima overestimate simultaneous effects; they are not predicted yielding or measured friction. At μguide=.18 the feedback inequality no longer returns a finite bound. Overconstrained pad preload, dirt, frame bending, misregistration and powered stall can do worse. The cam lead tends to zero at the dwell: motor torque does not upper-bound radial jam force without bounded compliance, displacement and cutoff. No measured maximum actuator demand, no all-pole seating or loaded release proof.

At trial μworm=.10 and ηspur=.8 the jaw rated calculation gives 2169 N·mm at the ring using *maximum* catalog gearbox efficiency; tooth, shaft, gearbox input-speed and duty ratings remain separate. At 70 N·mm applied worm-shaft side torque (2× the 35 N·mm intermittent gearhead limit, **not** a measured seated load), the star shank sees 33.65 N lateral; μ=.23 gives 7.74 N extraction friction. At the actual maximum cam-lead pose, .82 mm of pin remains in the hole: spring+rub needs 13.746 N·mm even before spur loss. The 15.04 N·mm worst-envelope number adds noncoincident maximum compression. Either exceeds 6.99 N·mm optimistic continuous output. Physical unloading is not established by reversing the main motor or observing a current. No automatic power-off catch from a between-hole pose.

## 3. Disk, assembly and package

The registered three-lobe steel shell, real steel cartridge reserves, separate buttons, hinge and fixed disk are retained. Rigid disk frustum R11.89→12.42, x=−8.55..−6.95 at home, with six opposed 56° seat stock reservations to x=−5.30. Three Ø2.20 posts on R8.58 end at x=−8.20; no post on the front face. Travel +8.53 to nominal rigid rim x=+1.58. The new Ø3.50 lead stud sits at radius 7.80, azimuth 30.64°, and ends inside the frustum at x=−8.21. The separate 24T worm-driven rotating nut engages a .50-lead thread with 4.06 mm nominal axial overlap at both endpoints. No glass-filled/plastic or friction-only retention is credited to these steel paths.

A 4:1 independent disk gearmotor with 2:1 spur and 24:1 worm gives **533.2 N nominal ideal stall-generated thrust** (.221 N·mm×4×2×24×2π/.50). With the stated maximum gear efficiency, trial thread/worm friction and thrust loss, only 23.2 N from nominal rated torque; this is not a guaranteed output. The large offset from a corner of the rim to the stud and only 1.40 mm adverse axial guide-station separation give ~1.44 kN guide reaction per row for a single 100 N opposed rim load. These are sizing warnings, not limits on external force. **There is no actual seated disk force upper.** Static external wrench, preload/stiffness, gearbox and hard-stop closure, thrust-bearing stiffness and current-limiter dynamics are unbounded here. Nominal rigid rim at +1.58 leaves .2284 mm over the new 1.3516 mm lip threshold and positive .1294 friction-debited axial ratio only on the forward/in-bore branch. No tolerance/cocking/elastic endpoint or neighbor ejection is certified.

**Assembly order tested:** insert each plug radially with top removed (nominal support gap .003), then close the lid; with the lid already on there is an R.120 mm intersection. Introduce the face plate from the rear with three open-front grooves clocked to PARK; bolt the split rear thrust cap and bracket. That phase, screw access and backside collar retention remain unverified as a complete assembly. For the terminal disk: park plugs, leave terminal collars off, introduce disk/posts from front and engage rotating nut, cross-pin/split collars from rear, close covers last. The bare circular frustum clears the analytic bore by at least .10685 mm (parked) through this path; true post/bush radial clearance is .018. The conservative polygon bush overlap is **not** a physical collision. Three specific Ø.94 punch sweeps at home clear selected drives; no general tool-access signoff. There is a real failure after assembly: a finite inscribed portion of the 90° terminal collar intrudes into the motor cylinder at +6.56 stroke. The disk motor also crosses fixed frame ties. The optional later Ø3.40×3.50 pocket at r=10.30, t=±2.07 hits its station post by .10866 mm across x=−12.05..−8.55 at **all six seats**. No pocket cut into this disk.

The actuator is behind fixed metal nose guards and removable convex polymer pod shells, with .09 internal clearance and .26 trial wall; no actuator surface is credited as a smooth exterior guard. A real R.12977 ball lies in both fixed polymer and untouched rotating steel (world [11.34,18.58122,−10.51275]). Cutting away the shell to hide that conflict would invalidate the assumed guarding; port/labyrinth, joint sealing and a serviceable smooth finish are not demonstrated. A weighted finite-vertex bound for the actual as-drawn external guard blanks and cycled-cell reservation gives Ø60.9003967, matched by the containing sphere. This excludes this package, not smaller future enclosures. Separate powered housings across a registered 38 mm pitch have at least .20 mm lower support gap through independent ±15° yaw; the mating tail, covers’ internal conflicts and arbitrary approach are not certified by that number. Lowest nose guard is .27 mm above the level four-foot plane.

## 4. Mass, electronics and decision

Using provisional 17-4 PH 7.75, 7075 Al 2.81, polymer 1.27 and Cu-envelope 8.96 g/cm³, the **34.14 g** selected minimum includes net disjoint cam bands, real face/guide stock, disk, posts, lead stud, registered pierced steel shell/cartridges and nominal 2.90 g cell. **118.46 g** is a gross upper for the listed reservations only: 53.03 g steel drives etc., 11.97 g pierced tail gross, 7.50 g aluminum, 30.50 g motor envelopes, 11.31 g electronics envelopes, cell and 1.25 g uncut pod walls. Nominal four motor+gearhead catalog masses total ~21.4 g, not a guaranteed lower or upper. Missing full loom, fasteners/retainers, terminations, independent fault sensing, extra driver, sealing, assembly allowances and measured max component masses mean **no as-built upper or actual COM**. The representative gross-stock COM world (−1.00,−.61,1.56) gives +8.26 mm four-foot margin but −.25 mm on one three-foot support. Do not use this as a validated 70 g load box.

HCG431528HP20 bulk 30.8×16.8×5.4, separate folded tabs/protection; 2 A at 10–45°C. The 4 A 2–3 ms rating is not a release rating. Minimum 3 V, four nominal 16.2 Ω motors cold-stalled, .85 assumed converter, .13 A control + .15 A transient give 1.15 A cell-side; no sag, thermal, jam or cutoff proof. At 22°C rated winding current for the **3 V** winding is .142 A. The 16×13×3.5 board and antenna air reserve do not establish link range. Component references: [motor](https://www.faulhaber.com/fileadmin/Import/Media/EN_0615_S_FMM.pdf), [gearhead](https://www.faulhaber.com/fileadmin/Import/Media/EN_06-1_FMM.pdf), [BMD-350](https://content.u-blox.com/sites/default/files/BMD-350_DataSheet_UBX-19033354.pdf); cell values from supplied sheet.

**Keep:** the straight, fully roofed guide cassette and separate disk interface as datums only. **Change before spending on another whole package:** bring radial drive closer axially to the toe or enlarge the guide pitch, reserve the Ø2.20 post-and-stop corridors before placing a disk motor, and demonstrate bounded reactions and bilateral physically unloadable braking inside fixed guards. Do not transfer the bare-port work certificate or any other take’s preload/contact forces to this build.

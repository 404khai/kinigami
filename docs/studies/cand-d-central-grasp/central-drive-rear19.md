**Torque discriminator.** Use 10 effective longitudinal guide span, 5 effective antirotation radius, guide/thread μ=.23, 60° flank, pressurized nut equivalent radius 1.5 and provisional +3 N·mm other drag. At 45 N axial draw/8 N resisting post/30 N total radial reaction, 1:1 sliding jaw ramp, no equal jaw sharing, the optimistic centred-guide-friction screens are 62.8/16.9/86.2 N·mm at output screws. Moving friction reaction up to 5 off centre increases them to **79.8/22.0/149.0**. The two catalogue maximum-efficiency points give only 66.2 E and 112.2 J there *if* mesh efficiency .85 holds. This is not a stall, continuous rating, guarantee, safe limiter or proof of 30 N aggregate jaw resistance. Need aligned guides or local balanced thrust, measured bearing/gear and guide drag, positive powered-off locks, bidirectional fault caps and forced reversal without trapped preload. Disc travel alone cannot supply those. Two motors at .40 A each at these published points, plus joint/electronics; summed theoretical stall current 3 A at 6 V before joint, not an allowed operating mode. At least 47.889 g from old 23.569 subtotal +19 motors +5.32 four thrust bearings, before new deck/cell changes/jaws/links/boards/lock. Update loads from actual mass.

**Stop witnesses and redesign.**
1. J aft thrust race at x8, y9.40, z7.661 lies inside the old joint motor's x3.08..9.32, y5.35..11.50, z−9.85..8.85 box. Both the radius-4.5 annular race at that station and its mount require this volume. Moving the roof alone cannot clear it. Test J torque transfer via a long idler shaft at (+3.80,9.219), place the *J output wheel and entire thrust bank* forward of x9.57 and move J nut, thread and yoke back-link accordingly. Check long idler support and gear face width and not merely the two circles. E race requires the cell moved left (padded cell y−17.05..−11.25, only .25 clearance). Roof, cell mounts, boost board and hinge articulation need redesign.
2. The proposed full roof box, padded relocated cell box, retained front panel and aft-tolerance B3.215 stud **alone** require diameter ≥60.939 regardless of centre. Weighted variance of four stock points proves this, not a failed centre guess. This is a failure of the *defined outer stock*, not of every rounded motor/cell installation. Cut back front roof corner and/or shorten both-end head/face to tail pitch, relocate cell, then retest all fixed front guards and both ends.
3. No full comb, grille, front link, positive lock, guide supports, power hardware, wire exits, release-post arrangement or tilted all-face sweep has closed. Neither 9.30 mm travel nor an open-position signal proves full release. Opposing jaws in asymmetric loading, bench-measured spring and local strip under a joint/power fault remain separate gates.

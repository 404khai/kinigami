# Central stud with three radial jaws — exploratory, not a candidate

[Q100](../../decisions/Q100-gate1-run12-disposition.md) retires the rotary stripper, not the 60 mm envelope. This directory investigates separating central tensile capture from a six-contact, outward-opening face. No candidate declaration or previous gate result is changed.

* [External seats](seats.py): three R2 balls against 45° V's at radius 6.30. A contraction estimate constructs the nearby six-flat seated pose for the proposed geometric tolerances. A strictly positive **frictionless** contact solution is a local virtual-work/load-carrier test, not a bound on actual contact force or on the work of assembly. At 50 g, including 2 N lateral and 10 N mm parasitic couple, the sufficient pull is 24.77 N (service), **38.20 N** with the table-correction load set, 36.96 N for the joint fault. The latter two are separate load sets. [Flank study](edge.py) uses a circumscribed friction-cone polytope, debits for angle/position error, and a dual feasibility check over the modelled head-face regions. Its static Hertz screen is not a material rating or an ejection analysis.
* [Three-jaw mechanics and sequence](grasp.md), [calculator](grasp.py): 3.20 mm radius globe, separate proposed measured 39.05–44.73 N draw and independent outward-leading jaws. A nominally open jaw with 2.45 mm travel still bears on a freely displaced sphere. The rear striker does not independently bound neck tilt. Limits based on a fully supported flat do not survive a sawcut through the loaded face.
* [Slot and comb guard](grille.md), [slot](slot.py), [comb](comb.py): the parked slot can stop a ball or neck from following a fully recessed jaw. A single web during motion admits a pure side contact in the guard/probe model, not an established whole-unit pose. A second moving tooth can bound the resultant direction at an interior cut, but adjacent fixed ribs leave only 0.015 mm per side at the narrow corner; the outer cuts, local pressure and supports still matter. No all-pose jaw guarantee.
* [Shared package, including flared hex](central-drive-take19.md), [calculator](central-drive-take19.py): the smaller rear piston fits that bore. The original upper motor shaft and aft sphere endpoints already span **60.194 mm** in the larger version. At the wider .42 flare the fully recessed jaw needs 4.531 mm radial motion before cage drift, and the nose passes the guard crest. The motor comparison and dry stroke budget are not an assembled second actuator or a certified drive.
* [Central wire instead of ideal ball joints](tension_link.py): exact solution of the linear tensioned-beam equation, not an elastica/strength certificate. A finite-diameter clamped wire supplies shear **and** moment. E=205 GPa is a trial modulus. [Gibbs 17-7 wire data](https://www.gibbswire.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Gibbs-Datasheet-Wire-1-Type-17-Chrome.pdf) lists 335 ksi minimum **tensile strength** for .015–.020 inch CH900 wire; it does not supply a finished wire's yield, clamp concentration or fatigue allowable. Three-axis pose, terminals, the spring and a split piston must fit simultaneously. The parked gripper itself is not guaranteed to share the orientation of the seated tail.

Run the Python scripts here with Python 3, NumPy and SciPy (the Hertz routine is shared with the adjacent drawbolt study). Gripper, guarded-probe and packaging branches intentionally have different dimensions. Do not combine their most favourable numbers.

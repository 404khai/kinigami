# Take 6 — SCS0037 packaging and integration handoff (exploratory)

**Decision:** SCS0037-C001: **yes on a screened, re-machined package; no as a drop-in**. SCS0043-C001: **no for this layout**. Neither an as-built fit nor recoverable blocked release has been demonstrated. Do not freeze a positive retention conclusion from this note. All dimensions in mm; torques in N·mm. This package-only study still used two old cartridge/collar placeholders. The [integrated v6 dossier](../../candidates/cand-d/dossier-v6.md) records the complete cartridge at the frozen mass of 45.541 g. Do not substitute the package-only mass below.

## Continuous hinge sweep — not the G2 box test

Rotation about z, entire interval −15°..+15°. Lower foot, web, real teeth with 0.3 addendum and pinion all lie in z−11.65..−10.15. The upper surface is **0.32 below** the case and **0.20 below** the fixed lower ear. Web connects the r1.6 boss to r4.5 sector at u=1.3..4.6, width 1.4. Full sector has r≤9.0, polar angles atan2(8.47,6.2) ±(21+15) = 17.80..89.80°: x≤8.57, y≤9.00. The attached tail foot is behind the pin; the swept web/sector entering the head remains in the recessed pan x≤11.2, y−4.2..11.5, with ≥0.13 above the pan, ≥0.15 below wall-slot roof. Pinion Ø4.2 tip, (6.2,8.47), also inside pan. Intentional tooth mesh is not clearance. Body-to-body minimum x separation ≥1.8−(−1.8 cos15°+12.7 sin15°)=0.252. Motor box versus finished case: 8.47−3.12−5.30=**0.05** laterally. Check notably this tight gap under load.

Head-ear bosses r1.6 clear the moving tail face by 0.20 at every angle. Upper tail plate nose is a round boss with narrow neck behind it, not the swept diagonal of a 3.2-square solid. Its forward limit ≤1.6 (case begins x1.70). Fixed upper and lower ear straps extend along +y, x0.60..1.65, to y8.9. ## Mounts, fault screen and assembly

Closed steel cassette: two 7×0.8 cheeks, 0.24 welded top tie, 0.85 sole and two upward-facing axial keeper fences. Slip the measured case through the cassette along x, leading the cropped tab through an open-ended bottom channel to a fitted stop. Insert last 304 gate from underside in transverse keyways, with ≥1.0 seating each side; chassis floor then positively closes its exit. No adhesive, case squeeze, ear-friction or PA12 threads. Fit opposed captive metal pads, ≥7×2 loaded flat areas with zones centred at z−7.70/+8.70 and ≤0.010 total cross-case play. Verify flat/ear geometry and weld distortion; do not treat projected steel areas as ABS allowables. No two extra sole nuts from Take 3.

- **Design screen only: 200** in either direction (over the 6 V 176.52 nominal locked value, not a certified maximum). Opposed pads at least 14.4 apart give ≤13.89 N / 14 mm² = 0.99 MPa nominal bearing. Under ideal lateral support, E=193 GPa, 7×0.8 cheek, 21.41 span, nearest-support arm ≤4.25: simply supported single-load deflection ≤0.021, bending <66 MPa. This omits joint/ABS/sidewall motion; do not use it alone as loaded rotation. Bench limits for propagation: measure ±0.5° max case roll at **moving load** and measure the higher fault roll/retention separately; reject or recalculate any exceeded angular budget.
- Full load has a path even without assuming the floor is rigid: two 4×1.6 welded braces to +y wall at z±9.82; couple force 200/19.64 = 10.19 N. Reversal swaps tension/compression. Tap M1 in each strap end, 1.80 nominal steel engagement. DIN 963 head Ø1.76..1.90: even with Ø1.10 clearance, annular area ≥1.48 mm² → nominal ≤6.9 MPa shell-head pressure. Sidewall bears very near its closed lid/floor edges; bench-check wall bowing, creep, local tear-out and weld stiffness. Separately, if two sole hold-downs at y−2.95/+4.15 take all torque, couple force 28.2 N. Do not add capacities or count friction twice.
- Two new ears: 3.8 wide, two screw centres at **(Δy,Δz)=±(7.8,4.2)**. Ear tips at |Δy|=9.7; head radius 1.6 leaves ≥0.30 steel ligament to exterior, ≥4.2−1.6−(1.7+0.6)=0.30 to lateral slot lead-in and 0.959 to r6.3 Hirth OD. Actual screw/nut centres (8.4,−0.4) and (−7.2,−8.8). M1.6×2.55 includes head: 0.8 flange +1.05 remaining side pier +0.70 nut engagement, no tip projection through 0.8 nut. Document diagonal two-axis reaction and actual supported piers, not old pure-z levers; use one loaded bolt where contact sharing is unproven.
- First-fit hinge strap screen at 44.783 g: W=0.439 N, out-of-plane moment W×47.2=20.73; conservative single-plane hinge ear force 20.73/18 + (4W+W)/2 = 2.25 N. On 7.3 mm arm, section moduli 1.05×1.5²/6 and 1.5×1.05²/6 give 42/60 MPa in the individual bending planes. Not a complete combined fault/fatigue proof. Rear module is root-owned: at 200 a 5.3-radius stop sees 37.7 N, a dog at 4.85 sees 41.2 N typical of a **single** flank, and a Ø3.9 spline sees 102.6 N resultant tangential demand if reduced to one radius. A shallow blocked cam can multiply axial thrust dramatically (frictionless 3°/r4.875 ideal ~783 N). Do not apply ordinary ejection loads to the jammed case; bound travel/force paths or reject that state. Servo has no catalog output bearing rating.
- Sequence: finish steel inlay return slots/clinches and hinge wall pockets before electronics. Drive head ear screws from **inside empty head** using a removable short slotted/Phillips bit and transverse bit wrench through the open lid; axial tool space x3..34 is available, no long through-front-wall tool. Tail stud uses two straight Ø2.5 opposite-wall ports; upper tail leg has end-wall port. Fit tail moving plates, pin and mesh in empty shells, then fixed joint clamp. Bench-assemble servo+cassette+hub in spline-tip fixture with M1.7×3.5; feed joint driver longitudinally between braces while accessible. Drop the combined assembly through the open top, without fixed lid ledges, preserving tab gate and spring accessibility as specified by root. Install flush M1.6×2.00 floor screws (0.80 engagement), M1×3 side screws from outside, and flange nuts before cell/boards obstruct them.
- Lower tail floor screws M1.6×1.60 go through 0.25 inlay and 0.10 shim, **1.25 penetration**, not 1.25 full threads: with head depth up to 1.0 this leaves ~0.60 effective length in tapped 304. Dimple and finish the CSK seat in the 1.5 plate; verify full threads/torque and no tip projection. Hinge leg screws M1.6×1.90 →0.70 engagement. Tail-stud screws M1.6×1.70 →0.95 nominal in blind bosses. Lid screws M1.6×2.90 →0.70, joint-clamp ×5.30 per Take 3. All torque-bearing tapped parts 304.
- Route power/return on each side; one-layer x20..22 conductor crossing **below** case, z−10.75..−9.90, ≥0.66 insulated wire height. Stay outside pouch and side rails. Hinge wires leave above controller through smoothly finished window around y−5.3, z9..10.45; use slack in longitudinal aisles, not a tight loop pinched between face lines. Gauge along entire moving travel before closing. Driver-to-TTL/MCU connections insulated and strain-relieved; insert cell, soldered boards, optional removable lid nut ledges through side/front slots, then lids. Fit check includes installed pigtails, PET and groove/rivet protrusions. Never trim or compress the cell.

## Power, mass, status

Q95's U1V11F5 is not retained as a guaranteed 0.88 A 5 V rail: at minimum loaded 3.0 V even perfect efficiency would need 1.53 A versus its ~1.2 A typical input class. **Q96 rail implementation:** custom [TI TPS61022](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps61022.pdf) 4-layer 10.6×15.5×0.8 PCB, 4.25 installed height, true shutdown and forced PWM, short layout per §§8/10. [Coilcraft XEL5030-102MEC](https://www.coilcraft.com/getmedia/38f73356-5dfa-4ba3-b406-5d8b038c37d2/xel5030.pdf), 1 µH, 8.4 mΩ, 16.9 A Isat, 5.3×5.5×3.1, ≤0.58 g series mass; ≤1.673 g allocated populated board, not a measured result. Effective CIN≥10 µF, COUT 40..50 µF **after** dc bias/aging/temp, Cff ~1.1 nF across upper 73.7 kΩ (2 kHz zero), lower 10.0 kΩ; ±0.1% both. TI PWM Vref 0.585..0.615 and 20 nA leakage bound ⇒ dc **4.886..5.158 V at feedback point**. Use separate quiet Kelvin sense traces to boost connector; do not move feedback around an unanalysed long lead. Qualify **at servo terminals**: ≤0.050 Ω round trip including contacts (<44 mV at .88 A), dynamic undershoot/overshoot ≤40 mV (on scope at PWM edges and on unload); then ≥4.802 and ≤5.198. These are integrator screens, not manufacturer guarantees; do not start a release with a failed rail. Check actual motor-start and regenerative transients, startup with servo already connected, brownout and thermal rise at low cell voltage, EN low, no backfeed from TTL. No pass asserted without hardware/credible board analysis.

At 5.2×0.88=4.576 W and **provisional** 80% efficiency plus 3 mA×4.2 V overhead, 1.157 W loss, 5.733 W boost input (1.91 A at 3.0 V). At 3 V/5.2 V, 0.7 µH, 1 MHz, ideal duty 0.423 ⇒ ~1.81 A p-p ripple, ~2.82 A peak, below 6.5 A TI min valley at specified test point; this does not guarantee regulation. Forced-PWM no-load and measured 80% lower operating bound also need validation. Whole unit 9.635 W (~3.22 A at 3.0 V) vs **retail nominal** 26.64 W; high-rate cell lacks OEM tolerance, degradation/protection data. Set loaded-cell inhibition + gate boost EN via pulldown and MCU. Do not assume 30 AWG alone can carry full battery current; split short high-current branches/returns in parallel within harness length budget, or separately mass a rated harness after actual thermal/drop tests. 3.3 V SN74LVC2G125 tri-stated during reply, 5.5 V-tolerant input; test off-state and reset sequencing.

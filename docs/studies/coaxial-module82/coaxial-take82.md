# Take 82 — Can the axial-sleeve coupler fit in a complete Kin?

**Exploratory — reject this installation.** The modeled outer stock fits inside Ø59.962 mm over the four commanded phases and ±15° of own yaw. The fitting objects cannot coexist: the cage intersects the registered tail, the fixed ejector guide crosses the locking sleeve, and the ejector crosshead sweeps through the joint shaft. Even two nominally registered, straight Kins have intersecting covers. This is a counterexample to the installation, **not** a proof that the frozen coupler topology cannot be packaged. No change to Q12, Q85, Q100, criteria or earlier studies. No Gate, commit or push.

Source files perform finite 3D CSG, not box overlap tests. Raw positive volumes (45 phase/own-yaw samples) intentionally include bonded roots, bearings, envelopes, and mesh reservations; not every reported overlap is a fault. Paired negatives, sampled minimum gaps and commanded register are **not** continuous zero-collision certificates. The three material-ball examples above suffice to reject without any such certificate. No free capture window, seating detection, table ingress, sustained loaded release, impact or compliance simulation is claimed.

## Load, bearings and power-off hold

Independent recomputation of the Take 80 screw screen (mean Ø2.68, μ=.23, conservative *plain* collar r=2.0) below. Opposed thrust bearings improve support, but we do **not** silently set their torque to zero. The V-thread column adds the 30° half-angle and retains the same collar debit.

| Load | Axial N | Take 80 screw N·mm | ISO 60° sensitivity N·mm | Needed at 06/1 output, .88 mesh efficiency N·mm |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Unlock | 19.096 | 16.291 | 17.23 | 5.60 |
| Eject | 35 | 29.860 | 31.59 | 10.25 |
| Draw capacity screen | 54 | 46.070 | 48.73 | 15.82 |

- [FAULHABER 0615 N 003 S](https://www.faulhaber.com/fileadmin/Import/Media/EN_0615_S_FMM.pdf): rated .156 N·mm and .142 A at 22°C; [06/1 256:1](https://www.faulhaber.com/fileadmin/Import/Media/EN_06-1_FMM.pdf): 25 continuous / 35 intermittent N·mm, **max** efficiency .60. Rated motor ×256×.60 supplies at most 23.96 before the spur, or **73.80 N·mm** with the assumed .88 spur efficiency. To deliver the ISO sensitivity at 54 N needs at least .396 gearbox efficiency at that motor point. Ratings don't establish that efficiency, heating or loaded life. At the motor's rated 2,500 rpm an 8.10 mm M3 stroke through this ratio takes ~348 s; free-running motor speed exceeds gearbox's 8,000 rpm continuous input recommendation. No stall torque used as capacity.
- Draw mesh resultant **9.57 N**, versus .5 N radial at 3.5 mm and .5 N axial on the ordinary 06/1 shaft. Three hollow Ø1.50 pinion shafts each sit in **two** [NMB L-415](https://product.minebeamitsumi.com/en/product/category/bearing/miniature_small/parts/L415.html) 1.5×4×1.2 bearings (125 dynamic/38 static N); a clearance drive tang transmits torque, not intended thrust. Nominal centered mesh gives 4.78 N per pinion bearing. Screw radial reactions 8.74/.83 N vs [L-730ZZ](https://product.minebeamitsumi.com/en/product/category/bearing/miniature_small/parts/L730ZZ.html) 3×7×3 (384/129 N). One [EZO F3-8M](https://www.ezo-brg.co.jp/english/product/spec.php?eid=02414) 3×8×3.5 takes all 54 N in its direction vs 994 dynamic/932 static N; opposite race takes reverse. No 54 N on the motor. Tooth root/undercut, bore fit, residual coupling reactions, mounts, bearing abutment form, life and strength remain open. Catalog ratings are not installed output ratings.
- Spring wire .50, mean Ø2.65, 4.20 active turns, assumed G=79 GPa and free length 6.035: 7.896 N/mm each. Nominal loaded cage 33.28 N. Under ±10% rate, ±.05 free and ±.07 installed gap, min at DRAW **27.397 N**, jammed-reset upper **44.950 N**, min length 4.36 vs nominal solid 3.10. Peak calculated shear **1.044 GPa** is not a spring rating. Driver travel is stopped on frame, not on the cage. The .108 N difference from Take 80's provisional 70 g table demand of 27.289 leaves almost nothing for drag; this calculation is spring force, **not** established pin preload or an upper on externally imposed load.
- An eccentric force 14.5 off axis with 6.25 minimum sleeve-guide pitch adds return drag. Two-band no-squeeze model gives feedback 2μg e/p: required unlock thrust 43.09 N at .12, 115.87 N at .18; at .23 the factor is **1.067**, so this model gives **no finite upper bound**. Additional nut-guide reactions are unresolved, not free help. Worst single R.45 pushrod taking 35 N has 55 MPa compression; ideal pinned buckling estimate ~147 N on the 20.77 mm run. Pre-bend, cantilever bending, bush friction, guide drag, eccentric tail loads and unequal sharing are not bounded by this.
- **Unpowered:** lead 3.399° vs friction angles 12.953° at .23 and 4.574° at .08. Self-lock is conditional. At .05 the square-model friction angle is 2.862°; the zero-margin μ thresholds are .05939 square, .05143 for a 60° V-thread, ignoring uncertain thrust friction. With μ→0, 45 N can backdrive the screw at 3.58 N·mm. Flat sleeve dwell carries nominal radial reactions but does not block axial sleeve translation or loss of draw. Without a qualified minimum μ **an independent positive two-sign restraint is needed**; no such interlock is fitted here. The frame stops limiting spring compression are not that restraint. A future dedicated positive bolt/dog needs proven full engagement, retained driver and loaded withdrawal; no spring-closed latch, ordinary friction or motor current credit.

## Assembly, mass and disposition

Bench sequence: insert the bare sleeve from the rear before attaching yoke; fit the eject ring from the nose before attaching rods. These paths clear the listed barrel/guide/seat and lip/pilot pieces by conservative ≥.02497 and ≥.02997 respectively. Fixed rod bushes **cannot** be left in place for the same sleeve path. Install screw flange first, slide each thrust race on from its own end before enclosing the support; a preinstalled race blocks the flanged screw (17.87 mm³ overlap). Slide/turn on the bronze nut before radials and gears: six full turns remain after trial end chamfers throughout the commanded stroke. Fit rear gear before rear radial bearing/socket; leaving the bearing in blocks the gear (65.91 mm³). Actual circlip/groove/key access and separable load-support plates remain necessary. Populated shell service fails: the M2 driver cannot reach the rear service point through closed lids, and the cell lid needs outer clamshell removal. The revised split clears the two selected motor bodies along its own closure path, **not** all mounted parts; the front wire reserves intersect the closed cover. No virtual insertion, friction holding or tearing down bonded pieces counted as a successful sequence.

[Honcell HCG431528HP20](https://www.honcell.com/lithium-battery/cells/hcg-lipo-battery-cells/1933): 100 mAh, bare mass ~2.9 g. At 3 V minimum, 2 A (10–45°C operating screen) is 6 W; no 4 A short-pulse motor credit. Four 3 V starts with 16.2 Ω nominal resistance, −10% R, .28 A controls/transient and .85 conversion give **1.30 A** cell-side. This is a budgeting example, not a current limit or a guarantee through cold, sag, jam or cutoff. The [BMD-350](https://content.u-blox.com/sites/default/files/BMD-350_DataSheet_UBX-19033354.pdf) module is not a complete controller; RF keepout, wiring, sensor clearance, assembly and thermal design remain open.

**Mass:** selected nominal partial minimum **32.79 g** for four catalog drives, bare cell, six thrust bearings, three steel rollers and own registry balls/stems. Gross density-summed listed stock **116.08 g**, including uncut/swept gear stock, conservative bearing/electronics/spring reservations and double-counted bonded regions. It is **not** an as-built upper or measured mass. Representative subtotal COM at DRAW/zero yaw ≈(−20.36,2.01,−1.01); all-four-foot polygon margin ~5.99. No full-build COM, three-foot margin or support qualification. The 70 g load box remains illustrative.

**Next mechanical decision:** separate the hinge's swept metal from the complete ejector force path before any further drive sizing; give the ejector guides their own continuous, supported path and identify a positively driven roller keeper outside the tail's true profile. Resolve real space for a two-sign power-off restraint and a second mating shell at register before advancing. Keep any bore/retainer revision a separate, coordinated mechanics trial.

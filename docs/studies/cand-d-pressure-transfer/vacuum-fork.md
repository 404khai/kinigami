# Take 21 — vacuum holds the face; metal holds the lock

**Exploratory. Recommend a raised-axis, inverted blind socket and a floating, positively indexed T-bar for a transfer coupon. Do not declare a candidate.** With the proposed measured boundaries, the 60 g face can stay seated during unloaded T-bar rotation and positive axial withdrawal. ## Face support and transfer

The Take 19 low-axis R7.40 available disk can give only 17.43 N even with no central bore and a full 101.325 kPa differential. At nominal R6.30, μ=0, the signed fault corner in the upstream load box requires 23.58 N just to keep all six solved reactions nonnegative. That is a stop for the **old** package only.

Use the new R9.25 six-V face, 60 g, true differential near 85 kPa, effective sealing radii 5.90 and 12.75. At nominal dimensions pressure gives 34.11 N. The inherited six-flat local support calculation uses the existing service, table-corrected and fault boxes separately. At 2 N side and 10 N·mm couple their sufficient pull thresholds become 20.80, 31.53 and 28.72 N. For reflected buttons/grooves, refer all wrenches to the same actual button-triangle centre; a change in load reference or a deeper relief is not included automatically. Six loads are not assumed equal. A positive frictionless solution is a local escape test **at the actual seated pose**, not proof that vacuum seals a misaligned face or pulls it into that pose.

**Pressure and elastomer budget:** at all azimuths each effective sealing line stays within ±.050 radially; net pressure area 395.49–407.21 mm². Arbitrary, opposed half-circle boundary motion shifts pressure centre by ≤.100 mm, not zero. Charge another .035 mm aperture offset, 1.0 mm axial arm under ≤1.15° face tilt, and 1.265 N·mm from pointwise pressure nonuniformity ≤.50 kPa around the true area-weighted mean. The mean differential itself is known within ±.50 kPa. Separately bound nonplanar pocket perturbation to .20 N side/.40 N·mm, seal contact to 1.00 N outward, .20 N side, **1.50 N·mm** about the triangle. A 1 N seal reaction at one rim could otherwise give 12.8 N·mm. All seat pockets must be blind, pressure-connected to the correct chamber and outside both seal contact lines. A leak to the hollow head, islands at ambient pressure, ballooning or trapped vacuum pockets invalidate these inputs.

**No double preload:** pump in 12 physically bounded pressure increments, each followed by reducing measured fork draw; reverse the same path to lock. For fraction i, target mean pressure = 85i kPa, remaining fork draw = 35(1−i) N ±.40 at the output; after measured positive clearance it is zero. Hold each actual coordinate while moving the other. Bound pressure/thrust slew through the valve/drive stop latency, or meter finite gas increments and capture spring travel with a mechanical stop. A sampled nominal ramp without an overshoot/leak bound does not satisfy the experiment. Include the following calibrated fork wrench, not an on-axis draw assumption.

| State | Minimum net axial draw after seal | Maximum gross pull | Maximum side / couple | Required draw at that state's wrench limits |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Vented, mechanically locked, thermal/rollback charged | 32.64 | 36.36 | 1.00 / 8.38 | 28.81 |
| First pressure increment, draw still full | 36.20 | 38.49 | 1.06 / 10.01 | 29.38 |
| Full vacuum, fork physically clear | 32.41 | 34.82 | 1.39 / 10.55 | 30.28 |

Across all transfer setpoints the smallest reserve is 1.93 N; enclosing **every intermediate motion** by the substep's worst wrench and least net pull leaves **1.67 N**. At vacuum-only, spending another .35 N outward, .30 N side and 1.50 N·mm for a blocked tool leaves .69 N. These latter values must bound actual tool-to-tail wrench, not motor effort. A jam moment near a vanishing contact lever can produce an arbitrarily bad normal estimate from a rotary torque limit alone. No jammed motion is credited. Verify elastic path and energy limits on obstruction before allowing motion under suction.

At the 38.49 N combined gross ceiling, positive axial projection ≥.53686 per normal bounds the *sum* of V normals to 77.18 N with the table load; all can concentrate much more than a sixth. Full draw plus full suction instead permits 70.22 N gross and a 136.27 N sum bound. These are not flank strength ratings; run the actual new geometry/pressure field and single-flank strength before selecting hardware.

## Passive structural carrier: single inverted T, not a loaded rotary cam

**Section.** Two rounded feet on one rigid T enter a blind socket through a keyed opening. Insert aligned, advance past all rear lips, rotate 90° **in clearance**, release the fixed-head rotation/index drive, draw the two feet onto the spherical annular backside. Positive stops capture draw input and angular engagement without power. During release reverse the sequence under the independent, seated vacuum constraint. No pressure is required or credited in the final locked state.

* Relative to the passive face x=0, require *every* possible rear overhang (including clocking groove rim) at x≤1.65, blind floor x≥3.10 and chamber radius ≥2.98. At the rotary index the **entire moving head including any key tip** is at x=1.83–2.90. Ensured by opposed hard limits plus measured carrier coordinates and actual face pose, not by a torque dip. The .18/.20 axial clearances cannot be reused as elastic reserves elsewhere.
* Bar half-length ≤2.55, half-width ≤.55, stem radius ≤.78. Seated relative position error ≤.16 in transverse norm with .04 additional projection debit; aligned roll error ≤2°. Minimum opening half-length/half-width 2.90/.95, central stem relief R1.16. Long/short slot margins .131/.111; radial chamber/stem margins .171/.180. A deep blind pocket introduces trapped gas: vent it to the **intended** pressure reference without crossing the annular seal. Require local front cap stock, radiused opening and an assembly route; machining a uniform annular undercut and blind roof inside these diameters is not established.
* Pull the aligned bar back until its foremost possible point is ≥.20 behind the supported head front at every angle. From 2.90 forward, include .05 lost travel: **≥3.15 effective axial travel**. No structural transfer on motor/encoder position alone. After that, no passive central stud can reach into a fork pocket. Still demonstrate the finite head-button windows, active stem, stripper ends and both bodies in all subsequent poses.
* At lock use two R1.95-min convex foot patches on a concave R7.90–8.10 seat. Keep patches inside the spherical annulus, off slot and key walls; r≤2.50 at the contact. Cut/render the actual full profiles: the uncut domain must include a whole .084 mm patch plus loaded registration, tilt and elastic shifts. Merely rounding the rotating tips is not a Hertz certificate. With 1.65° total angular debit, f_x≥.8600N per normal on these feet.

**Necessary floating connection.** React draw through an axially tensioned, two-joint link (spacing ≥7; transverse endpoint mismatch ≤.060; each joint friction moment ≤.30 in *any* axis; no ground-stiff drive or slider during draw). Fixed pivot within .025 transversely and 11.5 axially of the triangle centre. Other returns/drive dogs together ≤.20 N side, ≤.70 N·mm about the triangle. Link side ≤(.060P+.60)/7. The bounds in the table include this link and the vacuum, not a centered or zero-torque actuator. Off-the-shelf yaw-keyed slides are **not** such a support: at r=2.00 a single foot at 36.36 N axial makes 72.72 N·mm; even a paired contact with fixed-head roll key can transmit 24.31 N·mm of friction torque. Measure six-axis output throughout unloading and after parking.

**Unequal feet and positive anti-rotation.** Actual axial lever of each foot projected on the bar's transverse axis must be ±2.00 within .16; common axial arm ≤3.0, individual departures ≤.15. With no other bearing, moment equilibrium bounds axial imbalance to 8.87 N even at peak draw, by charging measured net moment, .16P, axial-arm × net side, and differential-arm × total transverse force. Heavier foot then ≤26.30 N **normal**. A .15 mm differential seat height over ≥3.68 mm foot spacing requires ≥2.34° rocking in the relevant plane, in addition to face/cage angular error, before any carrier hits a stop. Provide/gauge at least ±4° there for the .15 mm example and separate transverse/yaw freedom. Match β, stem-hole clearance, foot centroid and torus trim at the extremes; output float while loaded and alignment reinstated for insertion are different states.

For passive angular retention, prefer **one loose tail-side clocking key** engaged by draw, clear of its axial floor; no index tying the loaded bar to a rigid head shaft. The straight key walls exclude alignment of the T with the exit aperture; geometrically capture with ≥.20 effective engaged flank overlap *after* loaded pose and wear and actual lost axial motion <.04 (not a friction detent). The fork must lift the key entirely into the rotation chamber before the rotary drive can engage. Only one opposed key wall may bear; no other stem, key root, face or return stop can carry axial load.

If that single key touches, the 26.30 N foot result **no longer applies**. At key contact radius 2.25–2.55, transverse coordinate error ≤.15, working-flank error ≤1°, its effective roll arm is ≥2.25(cos1°−μ sin1°)−.15(sin1°+μ). Opposing torque on the feet is at most [2.50μ+.20 sinβ+2.50·2sin(.5°)]ΣN_foot plus bounded support moment. Combine with P≥.8600 ΣN_foot−(sin1°+μ)N_key. Charging the key's axial traction and its ≤.40 differential axial arm in the imbalance calculation gives **≤17.72 N on the key**, ≤34.54 N normal on one foot, ≥5.29 N axial on each foot over 33.64–36.36 N draw. Small key-edge bearing/peening is a new test. A second interfering key invalidates this enclosure. The spherical steel/steel foot screen at the larger load is 2.38 GPa, .0833 mm patch radius, not yield, surface life or key capacity.

**Thermal rollback:** park the input of a series metal metering spring positively, and keep its hard overload shoulder *off* the load path in the rated range. Example k=20–24 N/mm through the actual load range, target 35±.40 N. Limit combined loss/gain to .040 mm equivalent at k_max (dog lash, elastic settlement, CTE mismatch, temperature-induced spring-force change and relaxation). This gives 33.64–36.36 N without power. Pure force balance would tolerate about .200 mm loss in this isolated locked example; that is **not** permission for the key to disengage, the draw shoulder to bottom, or the opposite thermal sign to overload the feet. Bypass stops on the *output* or a permanently grounded angular selector destroy this metering. Illustration: a 12 mm differential path at 15 µm/m/K over 50 K consumes .009 mm already; measure actual hot/cold force after power removal and thermal soak.

## Sequence including obstruction and seal loss

1. With the latch still on the prior safe side, establish true pose on the six V flats. Verify six-site contact/gap geometry or independent calibrated metrology of the actual two frames and fitted flats, plus differential pressure/leak integrity. Three heights observe a plane, not XY offset and roll. A blocked datum may produce excellent pressure at the wrong pose. If pressure/seating never qualifies, stop without moving the latch.
2. Hand load between pressure and draw while engagement still exists. Witness series spring force independently of commanded screw turns and limit the worst combined draw. Keep any rear pusher off the vacuum/foot reference; a large opposite unmeasured force could hide loaded feet. Spring at zero is **not** proof a seized indexed bar is off the land. Positively advance to the gauged free chamber and verify depth, pose, clocking-key lift, actual low load and release of any old holding flank. Use a force-limited advancing/centering mode, including its possible off-axis torque; an axial screw with high thrust can otherwise push the tail off its vacuum seats during a jam.
3. Drive unloaded angular motion between known indexes, then positively retract the aligned T behind the active head plane. Signed swept clearance on both directions is required; slot and travel margins above concern sound parts in the stated pose. If a foreign obstruction invalidates the envelope, keep the chamber isolated and the parts on their current side; do not force rotation. Reverse/re-engage only after clearing the obstruction and rechecking pose/pressure. A load/torque trip reports fault, not the T's angle or a successful restart.
4. **Loss case:** after the T aligns with the slot, a sudden chamber vent under pure 4W = **2.354 N tension** has no tensile carrier. Positive V contacts cannot provide it; a pressure alarm, accumulator on a severed cup, or fast motor reversal cannot retroactively hold it. Even a last sliver of T overlap has no uniform finite-pressure capacity. A bounded-leak demonstration is different: 1.67 N force reserve corresponds to ~4.11 kPa; for .020 standard cm³/s to ambient at 101.325 kPa, ideal rigid isothermal volume ≥.986 cm³ buys 2 s for that decay. Before crediting it, include compressible seals, moving chamber volume, pump/check-valve failure, temperature, gross tears, response and *actual time to a safe state*. If arbitrary seal loss during transfer is required, use a separately load-rated carrier/diverse chamber with its own surviving capacity or stop the branch.
5. Only after full T disappearance vent the ring and each trapped pocket, and drive a **noncapturing stripper** against actual tail structure. At 60 g, verified residual ≤1 kPa and measured full-area adhesion ≤.75 N, delivered push >4.100 N covers 4W compression + W table resistance and those two terms, **only when remaining reaction directions cannot retain**. Add internal friction to input sizing. Head buttons replacing the old R2 buttons can project 2.80 mm beyond their nominal plate if centre is .80 forward; a flush T alone is not swept separation. Extend stripper past the actual tallest obstruction with tilt/deflection reserve and verify separation at identifiable targets. A plate with three open Ø4+ button holes is not a continuous smooth face; prove shoulder/edge exclusion or use screened flush closures without a positive retaining lip.

## Distinct original fork option; package check

On a protruding B3.20 head on the **tail**, a single sliding U fork on the head with longitudinal R.45±.015 lips at t=±1.95±.025 is a simpler unilateral actuator than three separate jaws. For ±.20 projected transverse registration, the two cylindrical working normals give ≤55.00 N on one lip even with no sharing (P≤36.36; same floating-wrench requirement). Keep crossbar at u≤−3.65, initially extend tips through u=+2; drive towards negative u so the crossbar recedes. After draw release the axes must be at measured x=3.35–3.45 relative to the seated globe. Then sphere-to-cylinder gap ≥.088 mm throughout translation, neck clearance ≥.147 and flange clearance ≥.234 over 7.5 radius. End rounding/stock must remain inside those cylinder/crossbar envelopes. Sliding >5.565 mm clears tips past the registered sphere, **not** past a freely moving tail after vent. A blocked carriage may show zero spring preload while an old shoulder still bears. Positive measured gap, parking behind an actually closed guard, and a moving stripper for the projecting stud remain required. Do not mix these lengths with the inverted T.

For the raised inverted coupon, allow ≥Ø5.96 blind chamber **inside**, ≥3.10 depth plus a metal back; on the head reserve ≥Ø6.0 passage for the retracting bar, suggested Ø7 outside before local webs (Ri5.90 leaves 2.40 radial to the pressure line). Relative to the front, an example moving draw joint at x≈−2 and rear anchor at −9 has the requisite 7 mm engaged span; allow relief past −12 for retraction. In series, 3.15 mm hide travel plus up to 1.77 mm spring load travel and .30 end allowance means provision for roughly **5.22 mm** across the latch transmission, not a built one-screw sequence. Rotation pickup/pawl, free gimbal, metal axial dog, jam limiter, strip drive, six-axis/pose gauges and actual vacuum source/ports/isolation/reservoir require independent sections. At z=+1 and table z=−12, the *outside pressure line* at R12.80 leaves only .20 mm for seal groove, external frame and floor clearance; the radial wall and total 60 mm length are unproved. A higher axis/redrawn shell is a new section, not free space in Take 19.

**First physical test:** instrument a two-foot collar including the single clocking key, axial link, two independent true-pose targets per relevant DOF and the annular seal. Measure force and six-axis wrench cold/hot; deliberately block one foot, clocking key, vent and axial retraction separately. The gates are verified load transfer, no stiff bypass, force-limited jam and restart, then safe containment through a real pressure loss. No capture-window, unpowered transfer, package, strength or Gate 1 claim.

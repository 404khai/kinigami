## How much vacuum is actually needed?

Same six-row frictionless contact test as Take 18, R9.50 and 60 g; keep the old 50.8 mm load arms rather than crediting a shorter pitch. All six perturbed normal reactions must remain positive. Required net axial draw: 20.440 service, **30.897 table**, 28.151 joint fault for aggregate side load ≤2 N and aggregate parasitic moment ≤10 N·mm. This is a virtual-work discriminator, not a contact stress/tilted capture proof. At Δp82, worst projected area 437.532 mm² (including .50 mm² occluded area) and **3 N maximum total residual seal compression**, the table contact minimum is +.412 N. At 65 g it is only +.053; at 70 g it is −.305. Recompute with actual mass and actuator/stripper reactions. Ro12.5/Ri6 at Δp85 would leave only 1.21 N for seal compression even before area tolerances.

Average residual line force for *both* lips combined must be ≤.0250 N/mm. This is **not** an O-ring rating: use a compliant lip coupon against the actual surface, measure its complete force/moment with centre offset, wipe, oil, dust, loss of one sector and wear. [Parker vacuum-sealing guide](https://www.parker.com/content/dam/Parker-com/Literature/O-Ring-Division-Literature/ORD-5705-Parker-O-Ring-Vacuum-Sealing-Guide.pdf) discusses permeation and compound selection, not Kin's preload or leakage. Preposition and press both faces enough to establish initial continuous seals; vacuum cannot act across an open leak, and neither pressure nor a face switch by itself proves six seated contacts.

Do not assume an axisymmetric suction resultant. Of ±.10 boundary variation assign .055 to uniform radius, **.045 max local angle-dependent line variation**. Integrating the most adverse positive/negative half-circles of both annuli, adding .010 axis displacement, ±.3 kPa spatial variation, masked patch and 2.6 N·mm seal moment gives ≤9.42 N·mm through Δp100. This consumes almost all of the 10 N·mm budget. At 82 kPa, 2 N side, the table contact minimum is +.134 at 15 total N·mm, **negative at 20**. Measure wrench during clocking, unloading and blind closure; a rubbing T-bar can consume the remainder. Concurrent structural draw + vacuum can almost double bearing preload. Meter handoff or qualify that upper contact load; six positive reactions are not an upper strength limit.

Minimum swept volume **2,490.54 mm³** (smaller annular area ×10.45). No separate isolated reservoir: the piston remains mechanically held fully expanded and directly connected to the face for the entire hazardous motion. Dead volume, compliance, seated gap and temperature must be measured. If a .15/R9.5 angular error prevents the .24 maximum film gap or loses a seal, this table does not describe that mate. Fill/expand test including the valves and tubing, not CAD pocket volume alone.

Take ambient pₐ, dead volume D, swept V. After thermal equilibration p_abs ≤ pₐD/(D+V) without leaks; for a hot final gas charge conservatively multiply by 1.06. At **98 kPa ambient** that's 11.29 kPa absolute isothermal, or **86.03 kPa differential after warming**. At 95 ambient only 83.40 remains; at 90, 79.01. No-leak minimum ambient for Δp85 including warming is 96.82. Measure atmospheric and face *absolute* pressures; do not translate “85 kPa vacuum” to an absolute reading. At least three spatial taps or independent communicating-port verification; calibrate offsets and response in the ~10–100 kPa range. [TE MS5837-02BA](https://www.te.com/content/dam/te-com/documents/sensors/global/Channel/ms5837-02ba-ecard.pdf) is a 3.3×3.3×2.75 candidate; its advertised ±.5 mbar is **600–1000 mbar**, not evidence of that accuracy at the evacuated endpoint.

Define leak units explicitly: cm³/min of gas at 101.325 kPa and 293.15 K. At ≤313.15 K, each 1 such unit adds ≤.724 kPa/s referred to even the minimum expanded volume. Charge entering while the piston moves also counts: use integrated gas throughput from vent closing onward, not a test starting at a fictitious perfect endpoint. With a sealed but stopped intermediate machine and **any positive leak**, Δp eventually falls below the holding threshold. A severed seal may do so immediately. A controller alarm, check valve, brake on the motor, or a larger finite volume cannot remove that counterexample.

## Shared drive, timing and actual clearances

**One powered shaft; no second motor.** First option: [Precision Microdrives 208-100](https://www.precisionmicrodrives.com/datasheets/208-100-V0.pdf), Ø8×36.5 plus 5.8×Ø1.5 shaft, 9.5 g, rated **80 N·mm at 11 rpm/3 V CW**, max .15 A rated-load/.42 A startup. No reverse, shock, arbitrary stall duration or minimum starting torque inferred. Axis along +y at (x,z)=(13,15.35), body y−21.0..15.5, cap to +21.70. With real circular stock R4.45 the guarded body fits a 57.77 diameter witness. Left cell under motor leaves .35 min in z with the guard. Install/removal must precede one removable side cover.

One tentative gear train: at y17.0..18.6, module .20, 16:64 to parallel y-shaft at (x,z)=(5,15.35) across 8.00 centre spacing. At y≈13 a 1:1 pair, m.25, 56:56 over 14.00 down to (5,1.35). A 16:64 m.25 bevel pair turns that shaft into the x timing sleeve about A. Nominal 16:1 overall; with guards the upper 64T end gear witness is 59.032 diameter. Support both sides of each overhung takeoff, do not side-load the Ø1.5 output shaft without a separate supported hub. The **lower layshaft crosses the old joint motor** at (5,8.1,1.35), and the old joint also crosses the main working drum at (8,8.1,1.35). Replacing the shell alone cannot work: move/redesign the joint motor and route its output clear of both axes. Cell now x.8..31.85/y−17..−11.20/z±10.55; boost PCB, vent manifold, gearbox support and sensor harness are not placed. No joint-motion or thermal clearance claim.

**Single-stroke mechanical profile.** Proposed common timing sleeve with a captured variable-lead helical groove (barrel cam, **not** a continuously threaded nut). Radius 8.10, body x≈6.2..18.2, pin centres x17.45 at home →6.95 expanded. Annular piston and rear dry finger move together; closest piston back x18.85 leaves .65 ahead of barrel. A guided sleeve inside the barrel connects to the piston on its dry side. First 8° allows positive vent closure; 8–103° pump rise 10.50, 103–246° concentric full-volume dwell, 246–341° reset *already vented*, then park. Close and open both run **CW one full turn per transition**: a separate positively indexed half-speed latch cam gives one insert/lock cycle and one unlock/retract cycle per two pump turns. No one-sided pin spring intended as a powered draw or return. Return/restart after a jam needs an independent safe-state path; CW-only normal operation does not solve it.

The alternative literal 2 mm-lead screw can make the same piston stroke in 5.25 turns: e.g. a timed sector 94.5° ×20:1 speed-up, with the separate return sector driving via an extra idler for opposite sign. A common motor can still drive the latch. But the two opposite gear paths, positive lock on the pressure-loaded nut in the dwell, re-index before engagement and displaced pump connection all require real volumes. **A rigid single nut attached to both T and piston cannot satisfy fixed piston volume while translating/turning/retracting the T.** No fit or fault credit to sector gears or an imagined clutch. The common sleeve is the simpler study survivor.

Under provisional ≤4 N total piston-seal drag, .23 guide friction, 10.5 span, 8.1 eccentricity, pressure difference up to 98 kPa and a smooth half-cosine rise, a one-follower screen gives 43.8 N follower thrust. Dry μ=.23 slot requires ~726 N·mm on sleeve including nominal +5 N·mm other drag; at 16:1 and **assumed** .67 total efficiency, 67.7 N·mm on the motor, below rated 80. Minimum efficiency .567 just for this screen. A seized/damaged seal, additional moment from friction eccentricity, ramp/guide cocking, bearings or slow start can erase it. Single-follower force and Hertz/bending at the pin and teeth, half-speed latch cam, preload stop, bearing races and jam limiter remain unsized. This is neither a guaranteed drive bound nor qualification of an 87 s duty cycle.

**Cycle-time cost of rated torque:** 87.27 s/turn at the rated point, 23.03 s pumping, 57.70 s from start of pump travel to first allowable vent. At 98 kPa ambient, 4 s additional interlock time and 1 kPa reserve above Δp82, the whole integrated leak budget is only **.068 reference cm³/min**. At .10 the screen misses that reserve. Arm threshold example ≥85 measured conservative differential; stop before main movement if the projected end threshold (including sensor error, leakage and temperature) is not met. Track pressure evolution throughout and test worst slow/blocked cycles. A [Pololu 4796](https://www.pololu.com/product/4796/specs) 12:1 alternative at its 68 rpm/98.07 N·mm *maximum-efficiency point* could turn faster, but that is **not a rated guarantee**; do not substitute its benign time for the 208-100. A [Lee HP Disc Pump](https://www.theleeco.com/product/hp-series-disc-pump/) only reaches −40 kPa at stall: at most ~18.5 N gross in this ring even before seals. The deep-vacuum [KNF NMP830.3](https://knf.com/fileadmin/Global_files/Downloads/Product_OEM_Process/Datasheets/DB_NMP830.X_EN_28112023.pdf) is 248 g by itself. No external airline or pump credited.

## Interlocks and release

1. On approach, preposition on correctly clocked metal contacts; mechanically vent the ring until both lips make contact. Verify all three targets and seating contour. Pressure measurements alone cannot distinguish a sealed tilted mate.
2. Close local vent, pull piston once. Three distributed readings, ambient reading, direct piston position and a decay check must admit the entire remaining time. Open the flush mask **under vacuum**, extend collapsed T through passive slot, turn behind the shoulders, draw through a calibrated metal spring into a power-independent backup/lock. Measure T depth, clock, locked shoulders and actual spring preload. **Only after verified structural lock** vent/reset the cylinder for long-term attachment. No permanent vacuum structural credit.
3. From fully locked, repeat evacuation before releasing any structural draw. A steel-supported, physically monitored draw stop must unload while vacuum retains the six contacts. Rotate T back to the slot only after axial unloading and shoulder clearance. Retract the *entire* bar behind the head, then positively close and witness the flush blind. Both acquisition and release need contact wrench and cap stress checks through combined vacuum and latch loads.
4. Only on confirmed lock (closing) or wholly blinded head (opening) may the mechanical vent dog lift a local poppet **before** pump reset. A rotor dwell admits pressure verification instead of assuming commanded valve position. Trial Ø.65 port and ≥.22 poppet lift: at Cd=.60, 98 kPa/293 K and 1.5 kPa remaining differential, a crude terminal-flow screen gives ~.27 s to admit the gas; throat/sonic flow, filters, annular channels, blockage and seal pockets require an actual pressure test. Require Δp≤1.5 at the face before stripping. Blocked vent: do not compress the trapped chamber or tear the lips.
5. During the open cycle only, a separate half-speed cam drives the rounded outer stripper to a real shoulder, then backs off after separation. At 60 g, ≤2 N measured adhesion, ≤1.5 kPa residual distributed vacuum and 5W adverse service, >5.64 N delivered before guide drag is necessary. An 8 N allocation is unverified. At actual three-target plane intercept ≥37.75, true slope ≤.075 including measurement error and .10 axial debit, minimum conditional clearance is .720 over the cap footprint and 1.325 over nonprojecting body stock to R23. This uses the *measured tail plane and recessed passive features*, never stripper travel. All other fixed parts and dynamic tilt must share those bounds. An elevated lip or an open central slot invalidates the directional proof.

**What stops a candidate decision:** (i) displaced joint, pressure-tight end plate, regulator, actual mass and unplaced lock/shutter/intermittent timing parts; (ii) measured seal compression, pressure distribution, low leak, flange deflection and cap contact patch under simultaneous loads; (iii) no guaranteed finite-time transition to a positively safe mechanical state under stall, obstruction, seal puncture or long power loss. For (iii), demonstrate an independent stored-energy path that parks/blinds the latch before pose hold can expire, including the jam case, or specify a separately captured, load-rated intermediate state. A larger evacuation piston alone does not fix it. First test a metal annular face with several calibrated leaks, one blocked port, deliberately delayed T/leaf motion and one eccentric seal preload. No candidate edit, formal run or independent review.

[Official run 11](../../results/gates/gate1-v1/run11-2b8ea96-ReleaseSafe.json) evaluates four declarations under the registered Gate 1 v1 criteria, protocol revision 1. No survivor. Cand-d v6 passes G1–G5, G7 and G8; G6 fails. R10 stays blocked.

1. Accept the [retention review](../../candidates/cand-d/retention-review-v6-r1.md). The cross-pinned collar can be assembled, closing no longer depends directly on a stall estimate, and the servo has a steel reaction path. These resolve real v5 issues. The 49.03 N·mm rated servo can cover the nominal paired-lug estimate, but not the conservative one-lug dry/mixed breakaway budgets of 86.19/99.13 N·mm. Steep ejection feedback 1.175 supplies no finite upper bound. The rocker may bind or make edge contact. This is an adverse sizing analysis, not a measurement that every unit jams. Do not substitute motor stall or simply change the false release flag.
2. Accept the [Q61 originality review](../../candidates/cand-d/originality-review-gate1-v6-r1.md). Beckhoff's Hirth interface, TYCOON's servo-rotated receiver and the spring-driven bayonet patent each rate high only in the coupler dimension. No F1/F2 high; neither excluded silhouette. This is a paper gate, not an intellectual-property opinion.
3. Close the direct-drive Q95/Q96 cam architecture at v6. Preserve the declaration/reviews and both official failures. A second motor, a higher stall number, an unproven load split, or substituting low friction would not remove the one-sided feedback. The 59.357 mm envelope result proves neither functional success nor universal 60 mm infeasibility.
4. Before declaring a successor, require a new design decision addressing a physical partial-contact release path, rated-force margins, actual tool/drive/bearing stack, first-to-last contact, overload isolation and recovery. Study a permanently engaged **internal** screw driving positively retractable jaws, not a mating thread that can cross-start. Originate fresh independent reviews only against the new frozen declaration. Gate 2, a broader capture envelope and hardware qualification are not authorized by this result.

[Official clean-tree run 12](../../results/gates/gate1-v1/run12-a0833e3-ReleaseSafe.json) gives no survivor. Cand-d v7 passes G1–G5, G7 and G8, and fails G6. R10 stays blocked. The 57.868 mm containing diameter and zero stock-box interference are paper packaging checks, not proof of internal loaded motion.

1. Accept the [independent retention review](../../candidates/cand-d/retention-review-v7-r1.md). The ball/keeper topology removes the v6 loaded cam and admits a conditional seated reaction calculation without equal sharing. But its common stripper supplies only 1.20 N in its one-pad adverse calculation; 2.45 N is needed at the 50 g design load for compression and tabletop drag alone. Its 0.090–0.210 mm axial hard clearance does not provide Q85's driven take-up. Even the separate post-freeze pad pickup is not a demonstrated solution. A commanded opening is not a physically cleared neighbour.
2. Accept the [independent originality review](../../candidates/cand-d/originality-review-gate1-v7-r1.md). It reviews the changed coupler against 126 references, includes real positive-ejection precedents, and has no excluded fictional high or repeated high on one reference. This is neither a novelty claim nor a retention finding.
3. Retire v7's rotary shutter/stripper drive. Preserve the useful six-coordinate wrench calculation, but do not transfer it to new contact normals or to a different edge shape without recalculation. A successor needs an independently limited take-up stage, actual spring/stop load path, strength through the release edges, driven reverse and ejection with the same external loads, independent position/fault discrimination and assembly access.
4. The [post-freeze design study](../studies/cand-d-drawbolt/README.md) does not justify substituting a directly mating screw: finite full-thread preload is not a last-flank tensile support. A permanently engaged screw upstream of separate keepers has a different load path and is still worth examining. Neither finding rules out a 60 mm Kin. Do not open R10, relax Q85, or authorize a replacement candidate with this disposition.
